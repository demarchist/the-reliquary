Malcathet is the Succubus Demon Lord whom [[Cirrus Al Kayan|Cirrus]] names the Mother of Vampires. The party has not confronted her in a manifested body. They encountered her as a presence inside the [[Ashbringer|Crystal Sunsword]]—powerful enough to reject a Wish and intimate enough with [[Queen Concetinna Frostmantle|Queen Concetinna]] to wear her face.

## The Queen's Shadow

The identification gathers several older clues into one shape. Concetinna had withdrawn from ordinary public life while continuing to host the [[Red Wizards of Thay|Thayan delegation]]. She overruled [[Lord Aimu]] to grant those Wizards access to the Sunsword, then handled the blade in a manner that provoked a paladin's visceral sense of wrong. Later she—or something using her appearance—entered [[Tarkathee Daru|Tark's]] dreams, called herself both queen and mother, displayed lengthening fangs, denied being a vampire, and told him she had felt him die.

Her denial may have been literally true. A demon lord associated with the making or command of vampires would not need to be one.

## The Mark in the Sunsword

Tark found a delicate fingerprint pressed into a warped place in the Ashbringer's otherwise flawless crystal. It looked like a flaw left while the blade was soft, but [[Jack Mayer Johnson Mathews|JmJm's]] attempt to remove it proved that the mark was active.

When he touched it and Wished the blade unmarred, a pallid figure with luminous yellow eyes reached from inside the crystal and touched the same point from the other side. The Wish failed. Sulphur filled the room, JmJm was hurled backward, and the reflection changed into Concetinna's porcelain likeness. Cirrus recognised Malcathet.

The fingerprint therefore behaves less like damage than an anchor, seal, or point of contact. It may explain why the Thayan delegation wanted access to the weapon, why the Queen had to touch it personally, and why Lord Aimu's instincts rebelled even when formal divine scrutiny found nothing.

## What Remains Uncertain

Cirrus's recognition establishes the being he believed he saw; it does not yet establish the exact relationship between Malcathet and the Queen. Concetinna could be Malcathet in mortal shape, a willing or unwilling vessel, a projection the demon uses, or simply the most useful face with which to answer the party. The session ends before any of those possibilities can be tested.

What is clearer is the scale of the opposition. The crisis in [[helioglabulus|Helioglabulus]] is not merely a vampire conspiracy protected by compromised nobles. A demon lord can reach through its most potent anti-vampire weapon and veto reality-changing magic. The party's best tool may also be a door through which its enemy is watching.
