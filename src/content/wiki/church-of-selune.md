The Church of [[selune|Selûne]] is the established religious tradition devoted to the Moonmaiden. Its centre relevant to the campaign is in [[silverymoon|Silverymoon]], from which the High-Elven cleric [[Temura]] and three acolytes—Yuri, Clemence, and Gilga—were sent to connect the [[Fane of Selune]] with the wider faith.

## The Fane Mission

The mission gave [[Cirrus Al Kayan|Cirrus's]] young [[Cult of Selune|cult]] something it could not bestow on itself: recognition by an older institution with texts, clergy, and continuity beyond its founder. Temura pressed Cirrus to preach from the Astronomican and objected when the self-appointed high priest wandered from received doctrine.

That oversight was never fully negotiated. Cirrus welcomed the church's servants into his temple while continuing to treat the Fane as the expression of his personal relationship with Selûne. Temura entered as both subordinate and observer, roles that could coexist only while disagreement remained theoretical.

## The Tooth Golem

The disagreement became concrete after [[Zulkhir Atlan Issan]] sent thousands of bloody teeth to the Fane. Cirrus had Temura fabricate them into a demonic likeness of himself and then transformed the effigy into a golem. Temura questioned the purpose and considered contacting the church in Silverymoon.

Within the Fane, even that possibility was described as spying for the “real” church. Cirrus ordered Temura watched. The phrase reveals the problem more clearly than any formal schism: some of Cirrus's closest followers already understand the Fane and the wider church as rival claimants to religious legitimacy.

The Church has not yet acted, and the record does not establish whether Temura sent a report. Its importance lies in the authority it might exercise if Cirrus's methods continue to diverge from the goddess he sincerely believes he serves.
