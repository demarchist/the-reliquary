The Rockseeker Clan is an extended Dwarven family renowned across Faerûn for its expertise in metalworking. Its most prominent member is [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren Rockseeker]], but the clan's reach extends from the party's own Keep at Thunder Bluff back to an ancestral forge in [[sundabar|Sundabar]]'s Undercity.

## The Three Brothers

Gundren has two brothers, both introduced in the earliest days of the campaign, before the party had even reached Phandalin:

- **[[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]]** — the Gunslinger and master Artificer who built the [[rockseeker-manufactory|Rockseeker Manufactory]] at Thunder Bluff.
- **Barthen** — proprietor of Barthen's Provisions in Phandalin. He hired the party to escort a supply wagon, put up reward gold for Gundren's rescue, and later travelled with the group himself — driving their wagon across the desert and sounding the alarm at a Dwarven patrol horn on the road. Now runs the [[rockseeker-manufactory|Rockseeker Manufactory]] at Thunder Bluff alongside Nundro.
- **Nundro** — the third brother, who travelled alongside Barthen during the party's desert crossing. Co-runs the Manufactory with Barthen.

## The Sundabar Forge

Barthen and Nundro gave up their small forge in Sundabar's Undercity to their uncle and his family, who have run it ever since as working clan members: Ma' Selma, Pa' Oriff, Tord, Therl, Von, Elaim, Gillydd, Sannl, and Nora. Most of this branch of the family hasn't appeared on-page directly — but Nora is the name [[leanansidhe|the Leanansidhe]] borrowed when she disguised herself as "Gundren's cousin," a female Dwarven cleric, during the Reaver cleansing ritual in Sundabar — suggesting Nora, at least, is someone the party's circle would recognise by name.

## The Manufactory

At Thunder Bluff, the clan's expertise is concentrated in the [[rockseeker-manufactory|Rockseeker Manufactory]] — a sprawling complex of forges, foundries, and workshops within the Keep, and a major employer in the region. Gundren built it, but day-to-day operations now fall to **Barthen and Nundro**, with Smith Slagbelcher — relocated from Sundabar at Gundren's request — serving as foreman.
