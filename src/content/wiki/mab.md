Mab is the reigning Queen of the Winter Court — the Unseelie Fae, whose nature is as unchanging and absolute as the season she embodies. Where the Summer Court favours growth, beauty, and open bargains, the Winter Court's gifts come wrapped in obligation, and its cruelty is as sincere as its charm.

The party has yet to meet Mab directly, but her court is not unknown to them. [[leanansidhe|The Leanansidhe]] — the debt-collecting Archfey who has crossed paths with the group more than once — is a high Noble of the Winter Court, and answers, however loosely, to Mab.
