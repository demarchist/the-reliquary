## Description

Ishta is a Drow warrior of House Faen Tlabbar and Patches' sister. She wears full adamantine plate armour covered in intricate web filigree and spider motifs, carries a writhing snake-headed whip, and fights with the zeal of a true Lolth devotee. Her silver hair cascades in long braids, her head is backlit by a dark halo, and her eyes blaze with purple fire.

## The Luskan Ambush

Ishta appeared aboard the ship *The Moniker* in Luskan harbour alongside Jarlaxle and a Drow wizard, leading a squad of House Faen Tlabbar warriors. She confronted Patches directly, calling her an apostate and demanding her death. The ambush was complicated by six barrels of smokepowder beneath the feast table — a failsafe that Morik the Rogue detonated, destroying the ship entirely. Ishta's fate in the explosion was unknown for some time.

## Reported Alive

At [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]]'s Thespian Banquet, a simulacrum of [[jarlaxle|Jarlaxle]] reported on the whereabouts of all five of Patches' sisters. Ishta had survived Luskan and was stationed in the Bloodstone lands alongside the House Wizard [[jacopo|Jacopo]] and a handful of elites. Jarlaxle's agents had also picked up back-channel inquiries from her about fleeing [[house-faen-tlabbar|House Faen Tlabbar]] through [[bregan-daerthe|Bregan D'aerthe]], much as Patches herself once did — though Jarlaxle cautioned it could just as easily be Ishta laying a trap of her own.
