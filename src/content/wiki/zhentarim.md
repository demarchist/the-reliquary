The Zhentarim, also known as the Black Network, is a powerful mercantile and criminal organization operating across Faerûn. Its network includes merchants, mercenaries, smugglers, information brokers, and patrons able to move money or people where formal institutions cannot. Its methods range from legitimate commerce to coercion and violence, but its defining habit is pragmatism: interests can be aligned even where loyalties cannot.

The Black Network's influence extends through many major cities and trade routes, making it a faction the party must account for in political calculations. [[Lady Faye]] and her dealings with her champion and former Zhentarim agent [[Aliyah Faen Tlabbar|Patches]] demonstrate the organisation's most useful face: rapid access to contacts, credit, and deniable expertise.

## The Edge of the Network

[[Candlekeep]] is a documented limit. When Patches asked Faye about bribing the college, Faye admitted that Zhentarim influence there was sorely lacking. She could lend ten thousand gold to grease palms, but offered no assurance that money would overturn Candlekeep's rules.

That distinction corrects the myth that the Black Network is simply everywhere. Money travels farther than authority. The Zhentarim can make an approach possible without controlling the institution at the other end, and autonomous centres of knowledge may be more resistant to its usual leverage than ports, courts, or markets.
