The Warlocks of Ceregorach are a scattered cadre of warlocks who derive their power from [[ceregorach|Ceregorach]], the Laughing God — a trickster archfey deity whose domain encompasses cunning, deception, and the cruelty of a jest that cuts too deep. Their number includes Derro Savants operating out of the [[whorlstone-caverns|Whorlstone Caverns]] beneath [[gracksuluth|Gracksuluth]], as well as warlocks scattered far from the Underdark entirely — among them a Thayan Archmage.

[[bilrigger|Bilrigger the Savant]] is their most prominent member — or at least the face that the warlocks show to outsiders. His private chambers in the Whorlstone were furnished with conspicuous wealth, and he was known to move through [[gracksuluth|Gracksuluth]]'s political landscape with unusual confidence for a Derro. The group discovered that the Bilrigger who met them in his dining room was a Simulacrum — a constructed double — suggesting the real Bilrigger operated at a careful remove from direct encounters.

[[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus al-Kayan]] had a history with Ceregorach before Selûne claimed him: the suffering he endured under the Laughing God's influence was real, and Bilrigger's attempts to rekindle that relationship were ultimately unsuccessful. The theological argument between warlock and former warlock ended in violence, the simulacrum shattering into its component teeth on the dining room floor.

Droki, a lesser Derro serving as Bilrigger's courier and errand runner, served as the group's reluctant guide through the Whorlstone Caverns in their search for the real Bilrigger — and for the Vampire Savant [[milgre|Milgre]], whose vampire spawn the Council of Savants had grown tired of cleaning up.

## The Zulkhir Atlan Issan

Far from the Underdark, a second warlock of Ceregorach revealed himself in the Bloodstone Lands: **Atlan Issan**, a [[red-wizards-of-thay|Thayan]] Archwizard bearing the title of Zulkhir — one of the Magocratic rulers of Thayan society — meaning his presence was no rogue operation, but Thay itself reaching into the region. Cloaked in greater invisibility, he opened a Gate to loose a Balor onto the battlefield, taunted [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] and [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] directly, and named himself one of only two Warlocks of Ceregorach left. He nearly killed [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] before [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]]' Faerie Fire lit him up long enough for the group to land solid hits — though he escaped by Teleport before they could finish him. [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] recovered a sample of his blood from the scorch mark he left behind: black, tar-thick, and unmistakably no longer human.

## The Teeth at the Fane

While the group was on [[acheron|Acheron]], a delivery arrived at the [[fane-of-selune|Fane of Selûne]]: thousands of bloody teeth heaped around the feet of the great statue of [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]], with a note from **Zulkhir Atlan Issan** goading him to return to the Bloodstone Lands and face him — as one of only two remaining servants of the Tooth Fairy. Cirrus had the teeth packed up and carried to his chambers, and spent the night building: a demonic effigy of himself made of the teeth, then True Polymorphed into an iron golem.
