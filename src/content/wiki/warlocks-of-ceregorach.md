The Warlocks of Ceregorach are a scattered cadre who derive power from [[Ceregorach]], the Laughing God and Tooth Fairy. They are not a conventional church with a single temple or hierarchy. Known members include a serial killer, a Derro Savant, a samurai, and a Thayan ruler; at least one also used a mortal spy. They are connected by patronage, teeth, and recurring structures of suffering and coercion. Only Bilrigger explicitly defended that suffering as spiritual cultivation; the others' private theology remains unknown.

[[Cirrus Al Kayan|Cirrus]] was once one of them. His pact with [[Mediocris Dente]] required treasure and a tooth beneath his pillow every night, enforced through an isolating dream-prison. Killing Dente broke that pact but did not end the greater patron or its other servants. Cirrus's subsequent hunt is therefore both a divine mission from [[Selune]] and an attempt to dismantle the system that made him.

## The Four

Selûne identified four principal warlocks for Cirrus to hunt: the halfling [[Strigu]] in Neverwinter, the Derro [[Bilrigger]] beneath Gracklstugh, a Red Wizard in Thay, and a samurai in Kara-Tur.

Strigu, the Red Grin Killer, used Meenlocks to torment victims before removing their lips and teeth. He died at the Fallen Tower Inn after rejecting Cirrus's offer of redemption; Cirrus removed his jade pact-tooth and offered it to Selûne.

Bilrigger received Cirrus with a theological debate rather than an ambush. He argued that Ceregorach's abuse had forged Cirrus into something stronger and that love was merely a softer leash. The body across the table was a simulacrum that collapsed into thousands of teeth when defeated. The real Bilrigger was later found in his sanctum with his head and green molar split cleanly in two, killed hours before the party arrived. His courier [[Droki]] survived the encounter and lost his pact during Cirrus's attempted conversion.

## Zulkhir Atlan Issan

Far from the Underdark, [[Zulkhir Atlan Issan]] revealed himself in the Bloodstone Lands. As both a Thayan Archwizard and a Zulkhir—one of the rulers of Thay's magocracy—his presence means the patron's private war is entangled with Thayan state power.

Cloaked in Greater Invisibility at the Aimu Estate, Atlan opened a Gate for a Balor, directed the Thayan assault, and described Cirrus's death and teeth as steps along his road to godhood. [[Aliyah Faen Tlabbar|Patches]] exposed him with Faerie Fire, but he escaped by Teleport once the battle turned. The blood he left behind was black and tar-thick.

Atlan later sent thousands of bloody teeth into the [[Fane of Selune]], heaping the old patron's tribute around the feet of Cirrus's monumental statue and challenging him to return to the Bloodstone Lands. This escalation is characteristic of the faction at its most sophisticated: the teeth are not merely spell components or a calling card. They are the memory of the victim's pact, repurposed as psychological warfare.

## Teeth as Theology

Across the network, teeth function as tribute, badge, construction material, and threat. A single tooth beneath a pillow makes the pact intimate. Strigu's mutilated victims turn it outward. Bilrigger's tooth-built simulacrum makes the cult literally reproducible from collected bodies. Atlan's delivery makes the accumulated scale visible.

The practice matches Bilrigger's argument. The cult takes suffering produced by the patron and points to whatever survives as proof that the suffering was valuable. Teeth give that abstraction a physical ledger.

## The Last Two

Atlan calls himself one of only two servants of the Tooth Fairy who remain. With Strigu and Bilrigger dead, the other is very likely [[Samonosuke]], the presumed Kara-Turan samurai who sent [[Letji]] after Cirrus. Letji voluntarily answered Selûne's questions after death; whether he volunteered for the spying mission is unknown. This conclusion matches Selûne's original four targets, but it should not be read as proof that no lesser pact-holder or hidden cultist exists.

The important change is strategic. What began as a dispersed hunt has narrowed to two senior servants, and at least one of them is now hunting back.
