Nobutada is a small, red-skinned Oni who introduced himself to the group in his mother's hidden lair beneath [[helioglabulus|Helios]], announcing himself plainly: brother of [[li-xian|Li Xian]] and [[neero|Neero]], son of [[kishin-doji|Kishin Doji]].

## The Dining Hall

The group encountered him after he emerged from a VIP chamber sealed behind an ornate paper door — calligraphy and artwork of fiends devouring humans worked into its surface — descending the steps to confront the group at their table with open sneering contempt. He stated, without particular heat, that he had business in the North that did not involve fighting them, and turned to leave.

Nobutada clapped his hands, and every large Oni reclining in the hall rose and assembled behind him without a word. He led the whole warband out of the establishment and down the spiral stair, back into [[the-undercroft|the Undercroft]] — toward the ruined tomb chamber where the group had just destroyed a [[red-wizards-of-thay|Thayan]] necromancer's ritual — leaving the hall's horde of baby Oni to surge forward over the abandoned plates and bones.
