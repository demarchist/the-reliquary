Nobutada is a small, red-skinned Oni who introduced himself to the group in his mother's hidden lair beneath [[helioglabulus|Helios]], announcing himself plainly: brother of [[Li Xian]] and [[Neero]], son of [[Kishin Doji]].

## The Dining Hall

The group encountered him after he emerged from a VIP chamber sealed behind an ornate paper door — calligraphy and artwork of fiends devouring humans worked into its surface — descending the steps to confront the group at their table with open sneering contempt. He stated, without particular heat, that he had business in the North that did not involve fighting them, and turned to leave.

Nobutada clapped his hands, and every large Oni reclining in the hall rose and assembled behind him without a word. He led the whole warband out of the establishment and down the spiral stair, back into [[The Catacombs of Helioglabulus|the catacombs]]—toward the ruined tomb chamber where the group had just destroyed a [[Red Wizards of Thay|Thayan]] necromancer's ritual—leaving the hall's horde of baby Oni to surge over the abandoned plates and bones.

## Authority without Display

Nobutada's size and restraint make him easy to underestimate. He neither demonstrated power nor demanded a duel, yet the court obeyed a single clap. His statement that his northern business did not involve fighting the party may have been courtesy, contempt, or simple prioritisation; in every case, it shows a commander confident enough to leave enemies alive inside his mother's home.

His family connection also reclassifies two old adversaries. Li Xian and Neero were not merely Oni employed by Zhengyi in unrelated attacks. They belonged to an organised royal house whose surviving son can mobilise a room of warriors at once. Whether Nobutada inherited their obligations to Zhengyi, seeks vengeance for them, or considers their deaths irrelevant remains untested.
