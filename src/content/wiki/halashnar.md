Halashnar, the Pallid Prince, is an Elder Evil originating from the [[far-realm|Far Realm]] — a being from beyond the known planes entirely. Its story survives only through carved reliefs found in an ancient Elven shrine beneath the Chardalyn mines, translated by [[mordenkainen|Mordenkainen]] and [[beryan|Beryan]]'s contacts on [[evermeet|Evermeet]].

## The Vessel

During or before the Crown Wars, the Elven Archmage [[abraxim|Abraxim]] traversed the Outer Gates of Reality and slipped past its Watchers into the Far Realm, where he was consumed — or infected — by Halashnar. He returned to the world as the Pallid Prince's vessel, changing everyone he encountered, until the Elves, led by Sehanine Moonbow, an aspect of [[selune|Selûne]], defeated Halashnar and drove Abraxim away past great icy mountains, theorised to be the Spine of the World.

## Lingering Power

Mid-battle with [[quil-sygoth|Quil'Sygoth]] — a vast, worm-like creature reeking of Illithid brine pools — the group's minds were touched by a melodic, intimate invitation to *join Quil'Sygoth and the family of Halashnar*. Quil'Sygoth's regeneration bore the unmistakable signature of Pact magic tied to either Halashnar or Abraxim, suggesting the Pallid Prince's power is still active somewhere in the world, whatever became of it after the Crown Wars.
