The Windrunner Armor is a set of legendary equipment that [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] worked toward for years, following schematics he acquired during the group's travels. The armor's construction required a number of extraordinarily rare components, the last of which — [[elder-brain-ichor|Elder Brain Ichor]] — was obtained through a trade at [[izzys-stuff|Izzy's Stuff]] in [[helioglabulus|Helios]].

With all ingredients finally in hand, Tark began working with [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]] to schedule the armor's creation at the [[rockseeker-manufactory|Rockseeker Manufactory]].

## Forging

Back at the [[thunder-bluff|Barony]] after the battle at the [[aimu-estate|Aimu Estate]], Tark spent a morning breaking down every dragonscale he had been collecting across his travels. The scales were taken to the [[rockseeker-manufactory|Manufactory]] and wrought into a weave. Combined with Tark's scroll, the central eye of a beholder, the [[elder-brain-ichor|Elder Brain Ichor]], and a Gith Psion's heart, the armor was finally fashioned. The full properties of the completed armor remain to be seen.
