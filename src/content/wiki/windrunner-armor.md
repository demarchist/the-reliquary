The Windrunner Armor is a legendary set of equipment that [[Tarkathee Daru|Tark]] pursued for years from a set of rare schematics. Its construction required dragonscales gathered across the campaign, the central eye of a Beholder, a Gith Psion's heart, and [[Elder Brain Ichor]]. Tark obtained the final ingredient through a costly exchange at [[Izzy's Stuff]] in Helioglabulus, then brought everything to [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] and the [[Rockseeker Manufactory]].

## Forging

After the battle at the Aimu Estate, Tark broke down the scales he had collected and had them wrought into a flexible weave. The schematics and psionic components transformed the result into something closer to a set of gravity-controlling robes than conventional plate: protection built around freedom of movement rather than mass.

The completed armor represents a long habit of Tark's. He does not merely recover from what the world takes from him. His blind eye became magical sight. His lost hand became a relic of Bahamut and Vaketh. Years of dangerous trophies became the means to fall in whichever direction he chooses.

## Learning to Fall Up

Tark's first day in the armor was spent in the Barony's forest crashing through branches, skipping across water, and alarming children as he learned to change the direction in which he fell. After twenty-four hours of practice, the manipulation became controlled enough to emulate sustained flight.

The distinction matters. The Windrunner does not merely give Tark wings. It lets him assert a personal answer to gravity.

## Acheron

That answer survived its first hostile planar test on [[Acheron]], where every face of a Great Cube maintains its own field of gravity. Tark lost his orientation during a face transition and began falling into the sky. The Windrunner let him override the local pull, recover, and float back to the new ground while companions without that control scrambled for purchase.

Its power also proved logistical. During the salvage of [[The Acheron Spelljammer|the Acheron spelljammer]], Tark altered the gravity of hull sections too large for ordinary handling and let them fall through the party's Gate into [[Thunder Bluff]]. An item forged as the culmination of a martial quest became the tool that moved an archaeological site between planes.
