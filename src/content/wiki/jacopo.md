Jacopo is the House Wizard of House Faen Tlabbar in [[menzoberranzan|Menzoberranzan]], formerly tasked with training [[finn|Finn]] — [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches']] captive son — in the arcane arts. He clearly resented the duty and took every opportunity to beat the young elf. [[finn|Finn]] reported the abuse to his mother during a Dream spell, noting that whatever veneer of civility had once existed in the household had dropped entirely.

Finn has since been rescued by the party and now resides safely in [[dokkar|Dokkar]] among the good-aligned Drow of Svartalfheim, out of Jacopo's reach.
