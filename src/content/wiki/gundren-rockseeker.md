Gundren Rockseeker is a male Mountain Dwarf Gunslinger and a member of the Faerûn Defense Force. He is renowned both as a fearsome marksman and as perhaps the greatest Artificer in all of Faerûn.

## Appearance

Gundren stands just under four feet tall with a hardy, compact frame. His thick, gray-brown skin is rough and bristly, and he sports an enormous braided beard that hangs down below his waist, adding to his rugged and imposing appearance.

## Personality

Gundren has a gruff, no-nonsense demeanor and a rough-and-tumble attitude toward life. He is not afraid to use force and violence to solve problems, and believes in working hard to earn what he has. He is practical and straightforward, focused on achieving his own goals. He is not known for his generosity.

## Combat

In battle, Gundren fights with an enormous magical cannon, lobbing exploding shells and grapeshot at enemies. He is always accompanied by [[gwen|Guinevere]], his astoundingly sophisticated clockwork lion — his magnum opus as an Artificer and the envy of Artificers the world over.

## The Modron Connection

When the party explored a crashed Amnian spelljammer on Al Askar, Gundren was drawn to its core — a captured Modron Hierarch enslaved as the ship's intelligence. The ship felt strangely familiar to him. He could understand the Modron's melodic speech, and when he touched the brass creature, a flash of light knocked him unconscious. The symbol of Primus glowed on his forehead, his eyes moving beneath closed lids as though in a trance. The Modron's intelligence appeared to have transferred into Gundren — a connection that deepened his obsession with Titanic and ancient artifice, driving him relentlessly toward the Titanic Forge within Mt. Saqar. It would be some time before Gundren learned this was not his first such encounter.

## The Derelict and the Nonagon

While resting at the [[rockseeker-manufactory|Manufactory]] after the Aimu Estate, Gundren found his mind snagged on a half-remembered image: a river, and a gleaming mechanical eye beneath its waters. His brothers recognized the description at once — the [[river-surbrin|River Surbrin]] by Mithral Hall, where Pa' Rockseeker used to take the family gold-panning. The last time Gundren made that trip, over a century ago, he'd gone with his father and their gnomish friend [[cornubel|Cornubel]]. Cornubel never came home. Gundren had no memory of why.

Returning to the site with the group, Gundren's memory reassembled itself piece by piece. A shooting star had crashed near their camp; the next day, chasing the promise of sky-iron, they'd found debris — an unfamiliar golden alloy, fragments of complex machinery, and a great mechanical eye at the bottom of the river. The wreck, buried beneath a century of ice, proved to be [[the-derelict|a Modron vessel]], its hull built from Fabrial circuitry and artifice beyond anything the group could parse. At its heart sat a cracked, two-dimensional nonagonal core with a kaleidoscopic interior that seemed to defy physics — a dying Nonagon of the Modrons, calling out to Gundren and his father's minds and charming them into touching it.

The entity fragmented on contact. It killed Cornubel outright but took refuge instead in Gundren and Pa' Rockseeker, coexisting inside both of them — reshaping their personalities and seeding the affinity for technology and artifice that has defined Gundren ever since. Neither of them retained any memory of the encounter, or even that Cornubel had been with them at all. Cornubel's unexplained disappearance haunted Pa' Rockseeker for the rest of his days, growing more remote, more calculating, and colder — and eventually drove the family into exile, the beginning of the history that led to Gundren's present life.

The group recovered Cornubel's desiccated remains from beside the core for a proper burial and marked the wreck's location for the Rockseeker clan to excavate. It recontextualizes the Modron Hierarch of Al Askar: whatever pulled at Gundren aboard that ship, it was answering something already living in him, seeded over a century earlier at the bottom of a mountain river.

## The Rockseeker Manufactory

Gundren built the Rockseeker Manufactory within the walls of the Keep of Thunder Bluff — a massive, sprawling complex of forges, foundries, and workshops where his extended family, renowned for their expertise in metalworking, produce some of the finest weapons and armor in Faerûn. Day-to-day operations are now run by his brothers Barthen and Nundro, with Smith Slagbelcher serving as foreman. The Manufactory is a major employer in the region.
