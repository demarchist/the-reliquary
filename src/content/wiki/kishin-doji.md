Kishin Doji is an Oni Queen whose name the group first heard spoken aloud by her own son, [[nobutada|Nobutada]], deep in [[the-undercroft|the Undercroft]] beneath [[helioglabulus|Helios]]. She has never appeared before the party in the flesh, but her domain has: a hidden lair, sealed behind a powerful Mirage Arcane, occupying the same ancient tomb-stair the group had been descending through the human catacombs.

## The Domain

Past a heavy iron door showing signs of frequent recent traffic, the group found a spiral stone stair thick with the sweet haze of opium. As they climbed, the human stonework shifted and morphed — stone giving way to wood and cloth, sconces to paper lanterns — until the stairwell had transformed entirely into the trappings of an Eastern tavern. [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] confirmed the illusion for what it was: a powerful Mirage Arcane laid over the space, marking it unmistakably as a monster's lair.

At the top, the establishment proper opened into a foyer staffed by Tengu attendants, and beyond it a plush dining hall where powerful Oni reclined at low tables around a bamboo fighting ring, dozens of baby Oni climbing over each other and the scraps of their elders' meals. A curtained back room produced sounds of revelry shading into violence — tearing flesh and cries of pain half-hidden beneath music and laughter. A red silk curtain sealed off a side hallway from which moaning carried faintly to the group's table.

## Her Sons

Kishin Doji is mother to at least three Oni: [[li-xian|Li Xian]] and [[neero|Neero]], both killed by the group in earlier campaigns, and [[nobutada|Nobutada]], who confronted the party in her domain's dining hall and departed peaceably, taking the establishment's assembled Oni patrons with him down into the deeper Undercroft — toward the ruined tomb where the group had just destroyed a Thayan necromancer's ritual.

## Standing in Her Domain

The Tengu at the front desk refused entry to a pair of vampire nobles — including [[dimitri-muskov|Dimitri Muskov]] — on the grounds that vampires hold no authority within Kishin Doji's domain, "especially spawn." Whatever the Queen's relationship to the vampiric plague spreading through Helios above, it is not one of alliance, or at least not one that extends courtesy to the newly turned.
