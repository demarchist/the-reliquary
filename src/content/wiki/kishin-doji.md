Kishin Doji is an Oni Queen whose name the group first heard spoken aloud by her own son, [[Nobutada]], deep in [[The Catacombs of Helioglabulus|the catacombs]] beneath [[helioglabulus|Helios]]. She has never appeared before the party in the flesh, but her domain has: a hidden lair, sealed behind a powerful *Mirage Arcane*, occupying the same ancient tomb-stair the group had been descending through the human catacombs.

## The Domain

Past a heavy iron door showing signs of frequent recent traffic, the group found a spiral stone stair thick with the sweet haze of opium. As they climbed, the human stonework shifted and morphed — stone giving way to wood and cloth, sconces to paper lanterns — until the stairwell had transformed entirely into the trappings of an Eastern tavern. [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] confirmed the illusion for what it was: a powerful Mirage Arcane laid over the space, marking it unmistakably as a monster's lair.

At the top, the establishment proper opened into a foyer staffed by Tengu attendants, and beyond it a plush dining hall where powerful Oni reclined at low tables around a bamboo fighting ring, dozens of baby Oni climbing over each other and the scraps of their elders' meals. A curtained back room produced sounds of revelry shading into violence — tearing flesh and cries of pain half-hidden beneath music and laughter. A red silk curtain sealed off a side hallway from which moaning carried faintly to the group's table.

## A Dynasty behind Old Enemies

Kishin Doji is mother to at least three Oni: [[Li Xian]] and [[Neero]], both killed by the group in earlier campaigns, and [[Nobutada]], who met the party in her domain's dining hall and departed without fighting. Li Xian served [[Zhengyi]] and murdered Tark's parents; Neero infiltrated a Silverymoon household and abducted a child. Those attacks once looked like separate deployments of a widely used monster. Their shared parentage reveals a dynasty operating across regions and years.

Nobutada's unquestioned command of every adult Oni in the court shows that the family relationship is also a chain of authority. When he announced business in the North, the entire warband followed him down toward the ruined Thayan workshop. Whether Kishin Doji directed the movement, knew the laboratory had fallen, or tolerated an independent operation beneath her threshold is not yet known.

## Standing in Her Domain

The Tengu at the front desk refused entry to a pair of vampire nobles — including [[dimitri-muskov|Dimitri Muskov]] — on the grounds that vampires hold no authority within Kishin Doji's domain, "especially spawn." Whatever the Queen's relationship to the vampiric plague spreading through Helios above, it is not one of alliance, or at least not one that extends courtesy to the newly turned.

That distinction makes Kishin Doji more than another member of the coalition threatening Helios. Her court has its own law, servants, supply of victims, magical borders, and hierarchy. It behaves like a sovereign enclave situated beside the Thayan body factory and beneath a vampire-compromised capital. Cooperation among those powers should not be confused with unity.
