Oni are powerful shapeshifting fiends associated throughout the campaign with [[zhengyi|Zhengyi]], eastern weapon traditions, and infiltration. Mature Oni can pass as humanoids, turn invisible or gaseous, recover rapidly from wounds, and combine heavy physical strength with cold and enchantment magic. Their appearances are rarely random encounters; they arrive inside a larger design.

## The Shape of the Threat

The first encountered Oni travelled under the identity of Neero, a Wood-Elven bard. It charmed an entire farmhouse into accepting it, concealed half-ogre allies nearby, and attempted to abduct a human infant during a blizzard. When exposed, the disguise gave way to a nine-foot blue-skinned body with white horns and milky eyes. Its small eastern knife extended into a full katana, and wounds closed almost as quickly as the party could make them.

[[Li Xian]] operated at a far higher level. He murdered [[Tarkathee Daru|Tark's]] family on Zhengyi's orders, used disguises and possession across years, and continued serving the Witch-King through the northern campaign. Other Oni fought beside Amnian infiltrators, Thayan forces, and the army gathered around [[vulkan|Vulkan]] above ruined Palischuk.

The recurring pattern is flexibility. Oni can be assassins, impostors, battlefield casters, kidnappers, or visible shock troops. Their shapeshifting makes every role an extension of infiltration, even when the creature eventually chooses open violence.

## The Nursery beneath Helios

At [[gravenhollow|Gravenhollow]], [[Horatio Von Bumidere|Buumi]] saw hundreds of infant Oni in [[The Catacombs of Helioglabulus|catacombs beneath Helioglabulus]]. The tusked, baby-like creatures fought, devoured their weakest, and experimented with shapeshifting while keeping clear of three black vampire coffins.

The party later reached the same complex and found two blue Oni infants in a Thayan necromantic workshop, gnawing corpses and shouting profanities at the Wizard working nearby. They died when Fireballs struck the chamber.

The matching location turns the vision into evidence of an organised nursery rather than a single clutch. Someone is raising Oni beneath the capital in numbers large enough to replace losses and staff future campaigns. Their cannibalism may be cultural, developmental, deliberately cultivated, or all three; the record does not yet distinguish among those possibilities.

## A Coalition's Common Soldier

Oni connect the otherwise strange coalition surrounding the Bloodstone Lands. They serve Zhengyi, appear beside Red Wizards, operate around vampires, hunt for Vulkan, and grow beneath a court linked to [[Malcathet]]. That does not prove every Oni shares one allegiance. It does show that the architects of the campaign treat them as a reusable force capable of moving between espionage, ritual, and war.

Their eastern appearance and weapons also overlap with Tark's lost history and with the broader Kara-Turan threads surrounding [[Samonosuke]] and [[Letji]], but no common command between those figures and the Oni has been established.
