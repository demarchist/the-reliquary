The Ghostwise Halflings are a rare people who come down from the mysterious highlands roughly once a decade to trade before retreating back into obscurity. In [[helioglabulus|Helios]], they've entered local folklore as the *Kryschek* — small faerie-folk descended from the mountains, said to bring good luck — a folksy misreading of the truth by people who've only ever glimpsed them in passing. Most Ghostwise Halflings have stark red hair.

[[lady-isabel|Izmakella]], who has lived in Helios for years under a human guise, told [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] it had been twenty years since she'd last seen one of them — before Tark himself. The people of Helios had already taken to calling Tark a Kryschek without his understanding why. It's the first real lead he's had on where his people might have come from.
