Candlekeep is one of the most famous universities in the world, identified by [[lady-isabel|Izmakella]]'s scrying spell as the location of a Mandolin — one of the seven [[instruments-of-the-bards|Instruments of the Bards]] — displayed in an ornate glass case within a larger hall of artifacts. It was the group's first solid lead in [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]]'s quest to assemble the full set.

The group Teleported directly to the display-case hall, arriving in front of the Instrument of the Bards' case just as an alarm began to blare. What triggered it, and what Candlekeep makes of uninvited guests appearing beside one of its treasures, remains to be seen.
