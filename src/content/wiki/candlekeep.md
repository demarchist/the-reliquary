Candlekeep is one of the most famous universities and libraries in the world, identified by [[lady-isabel|Izmakella]]'s scrying as the location of a Mandolin — one of the seven [[instruments-of-the-bards|Instruments of the Bards]] — and the first solid lead in [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]]'s quest to assemble the full set. Beyond its walls sits a waystation town: a warren of shops for scholarly supplies and lodgings for travelling scholars.

## The Elminster Annex

The group's Teleport from the [[river-surbrin|Surbrin]] deposited them not in a reading room but in the middle of the **[[elminster|Elminster Annex]]** — a display hall of relics and records tied to the archmage — just as its arcane security woke: armour stepping off plinths, painted figures drawing weapons and stepping from their frames, and the Djinni [[emir-suleiman-al-zahir|Emir Suleiman al-Zahir]], charged with the Annex's defence, gathering out of a gale of wind. Nothing may be taken from the displays; al-Zahir instead escorted the group to negotiate.

## Dean Rochester

[[dean-rochester|Dean Rochester]] — gold-robed, ringed, and working from an office marked with [[mystra|Mystra]]'s symbol — holds the conditions attached to the collection. The Mandolin had been donated on the requirement that only a worthy bardic hero could take it, and the college would judge JmJm's worth: recover a book missing for a century, traced to the plane of [[acheron|Acheron]]. After JmJm stormed out and the Dean turned obstinate about "broken" wards, terms were reached — a Planeshift fork to be replaced on site, the borrower's Ioun stone as a tracking aid, a standing tome-exchange pact with [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]], and an Abyssal fork sold to [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]]. What finally moved the Dean was JmJm Wishing 25,000 gold into his hands with a flattering note.

## The Trade

The group cleared the [[lunar-elf-spelljammer|wreck]] on Acheron, destroyed the Allip that had been the book's borrower, and recovered the book. They Instant Messengered it and the planar fork back to Candlekeep, and received the **Mandolin** — the [[instruments-of-the-bards|Instrument of the Bards]] — and the Abyssal fork in return, along with a note addressed only to "The Bard."
