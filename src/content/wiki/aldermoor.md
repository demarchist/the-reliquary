Aldermoor is a small, prosperous city on the south-east border of the Barony of [[thunder-bluff|Thunder Bluff]], on the north bank of the River Rauvin under the southern lip of [[the-moonwood|the Moonwood]]'s plateau. It stands where the Trade Way meets the lesser road that crosses the Rauvin Bridge into [[the-evermoors|the Evermoors]], and it is the easternmost stop on the Trade Way in the Barony: wagons rest here before the long run north-west to [[selunia|New Daggerford]], some six and a half miles up the road, or before pushing east beyond the Barony's settled edge.

Day to day the city is peaceful: full markets, competing inns, apprentices, children in the square. It is also vigilant by habit, with rotations on the bridge, walks on the walls and fog bells when the Evermoor mist climbs, because it holds a hard corner of the map so that the rest of the Barony can stay soft. Its folk say it is "where the alder holds the moor at arm's length": the name is the alders of its wet banks and the breath of the moors across the river.

### The bridge

The Rauvin Bridge carries the road south into the Evermoors, used by traders, hunters, soldiers, the desperate and fools. Its works are on the city's bank only: a fortified gatehouse with watch rooms and murder-holes at the north end. The south approach is open road and weather, for if the dead come, the city means to stop them on the span rather than hold a fortress in the marsh. Anything walking from the Evermoors into the highlands in force must cross here or risk the river, which is why Aldermoor is more than a market town. Its doctrine is *open for trade, never unwatched*.

### Around the city

- **North**, a trail from the North Gate climbs into the Moonwood Verge, the fringe of the plateau's forest, with the Elder Moonwell and a ring of standing stones. It is the last ground the city's Watch bothers to log.
- **East**, the Trade Way runs on through the Eastway. Prosperity makes the road worth robbing, and a bandit calling himself the Toll-Lord has kept a false toll on it.
- **South**, across the bridge, lie the Sallows and, deeper in the Evermoors, the Blighted Grove, where the restless dead are the least of it.
- **West**, the Trade Way follows the Rauvin's north bank toward New Daggerford, past the ruins of Old [[kossenberg|Kossenberg]]. [[shadowmoon-village|Shadowmoon Village]] is several miles west on the far bank: the Shadowmoon Clan trade at Aldermoor's market days, and their young bloods are a known trouble on the road and at the bridge.
- **Beneath the city**, the Weeping Cistern holds Aldermoor's waterworks, older than its present walls, and below it run the drains folk call the Weepings.

A Resident Magistrate keeps the Barony's law, taxes and caravan papers in the city, and several of the Barony's institutions keep outposts there: a room, a shopfront, a shrine-wing or a forge-shed apiece.
