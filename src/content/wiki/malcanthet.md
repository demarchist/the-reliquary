Malcanthet is the Demon Queen of the Succubi, Mother of Vampires. [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] named her when [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]]'s attempt to Wish the marring fingerprint out of the [[ashbringer|Crystal Sunsword]] was rejected from *inside the blade*: a pallid, yellow-eyed reflection reached out of the crystal to touch the fingerprint at the same instant the bard did, threw JmJm ten feet across the room in a reek of sulphur, and resolved into the porcelain features of [[queen-concetinna-frostmantle|Queen Concetinna]].

That a reflection wearing the Queen's face had the power to reject a Wish told Cirrus what the group is walking into beneath [[helioglabulus|Helios]]. Queen Concetinna of the Bloodstone Lands may no longer be human, or even flesh and blood. The disappearances by the score each night, the vampiric murders, the reclusive and "compromised" nobility, the necromancy that killed [[brusso-muskov|Lord Brusso]] and would not let his spirit return — all of it now reads as one operation, and the Queen at the centre of it is a mask over a Demon Lord.
