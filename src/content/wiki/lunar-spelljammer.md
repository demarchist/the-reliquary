The Acheron Spelljammer is an old vessel that crashed across one face of [[Acheron]]'s Great Cube. By the time the party found it, decades of exposure and scavenging had left the hull rusted, rotted, and stripped of meaningful magic or technology. Swordwraiths and skeletal dead haunted the wreckage, while an [[Allips|Allip]] moved through its broken interior.

It should not be confused with [[The Derelict]], the golden Modron ship beneath the [[River Surbrin]]. The two wrecks appeared in consecutive sessions, but only the Derelict is confirmed as a Modron vessel with a damaged intelligence at its core. The Acheron ship's maker, world of origin, and connection to the wandering Allip all remain uncertain.

## The Borrower and the Book

[[Candlekeep]] traced a century-overdue book to Acheron. Using the High Elf borrower's collateral Ioun stone, [[Fëanon Sërindë|Fëanon]] scried the wreck and saw a cloaked Allip within it. He believed the undead spirit was likely what remained of the borrower, but the identification was never independently confirmed.

The party destroyed the Allip and found the overdue volume in the ruined captain's quarters. Whether the borrower's death was caused by the book, the crash, the surrounding undead, or some other disaster remains unknown. The sequence makes forbidden knowledge an attractive explanation, not an established one.

## A Tentative Origin

Every useful system had been removed, yet [[Jack Mayer Johnson Mathews|JmJm]] recognised something in the surviving artistry. He believed the vessel might have belonged to the Lunar Elves and immediately wanted it salvaged.

The attribution rests entirely on JmJm's eye. No maker's mark, crew record, or functioning system confirmed it, and nothing establishes that the Lunar Elves are the same people elsewhere called Moon Elves. What matters for JmJm's dossier is that his response was not greed for working artifice. He recognised a culture in the shape of a dead ship and valued the shell after every practical advantage was gone.

Why the style felt familiar is another open question. JmJm's past in Sigil, his planar nature, and the parts of his life he does not discuss offer possibilities but no answer.

## Salvage

The party opened a Gate from the wreck to [[Thunder Bluff]]. Their goblinoid war party hauled timber and metal through while [[Tarkathee Daru|Tark]] used the [[Windrunner Armor]] to alter the gravity of the largest pieces and let them fall across the threshold. Acheron's dead lost corporeality moments after entering the Material Plane, but the material they carried remained.

The sudden appearance of Goblins and ship debris drew the Barony guard. [[Bohd]] arrived by wyvern, understood enough of the situation to organise the response, and had the wreckage dragged clear while the Gate remained open. Most of the vessel was recovered, though in many pieces.

The Acheron Spelljammer is therefore not a ship awaiting repair. It is an archaeological and cultural puzzle in the Barony—one JmJm considered worth bringing home before anyone knew what, if anything, it could become.
