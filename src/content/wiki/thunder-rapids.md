The Thunder Rapids are the stretch of the River Rauvin that runs past [[selunia|New Daggerford]], and they give the whole region its name. The roar is said to be audible for miles. In a little under five miles the river falls some nine hundred feet, through a twisting slot in the ancient rock of the [[thunder-bluff|Thunder Bluff]] shelf.

This is no broad, wave-torn water. The slot is narrow and its floor is choked with blocks the size of houses, shed from the walls faster than the river can clear them. The water is forced to squeeze, twist and plummet through whatever gaps they leave. It runs white and thunderous over the ledges, and clear glacial turquoise in the pockets behind the rocks, against stone that is dark and slick with algae at the waterline.

## The Run

Going downstream, from east of the city to the west:

- **The Doorstep** (70 ft) — the horizon line. Above it the Rauvin is broad and quiet, barely a hundred feet below the fields. From the water it simply ends at a line, with spray rising beyond. It is the one ledge still visibly moving: its walls are too low to shed much rock onto it, so it creeps upstream a little each generation, and the barony watches it.
- **The Threshold** (105 ft) — the slot's first great drop.
- **The Grate** — a sieve, just below the Threshold.
- **The Gullet** (70 ft) — a ledge at the end of a narrow slot.
- **The Cellar** — an undercut, at a hard turn in the boulder garden.
- **The Tumbler** (70 ft) — the way out of the boulder garden.
- **The Pantry** — an undercut on the outer bend above Keep's Tooth.
- **Keep's Tooth** (125 ft) — the first drop under the prow of the [[keep-of-thunder-bluff|Keep]].
- **The Postern** (145 ft) — the greatest on the river, at the corner of the prow.
- **Helm's Stair** (100 ft) — off the end of the pool beneath the Keep, turning as it falls.
- **The Crypt** — an undercut at the head of the next slot.
- **The Colander** — a sieve, wall to wall, above the Mangle.
- **The Mangle** (40 ft) — at the end of another boulder garden.
- **The Larder** — an undercut on the bend above the Last Word.
- **The Last Word** (33 ft) — past the city's western wards.
- **Spillover** (26 ft) — into the Anvil.
- **The Anvil** — the pool at the foot of Hammerfall, where the river's flow doubles.

## The Dangers

The river's worst hazards are the ones that don't look like much from above.

- **Sieves.** At the Colander and the Grate, blocks are stacked across the channel from wall to wall. The river goes through the cracks rather than over the top. To anyone on the water it looks like a dead end where the river disappears, and it strains out anything solid.
- **Undercuts.** The river has carved hollows beneath the rock on its outer bends, and great slabs lean out over them. The current goes in underneath. Boatmen call them rooms of doom, and the barony's names for them are no kinder.
- **Slots and blind drops.** The line through the rock is rarely wider than a boat and can't be seen from the gap before it. Miss an angle and the current turns the craft side-on and passes it from rock to rock.

## Hammerfall

West of the city the lake's river reaches the edge of the shelf at an armoured stone lip and goes over. Hammerfall drops nearly a thousand feet down the gorge wall onto the Anvil, with the hammers of the [[rockseeker-manufactory|Rockseeker Manufactory]] working on the rim beside it. The lip is the barony's work: left to itself the fall would eat its way back up the river toward the city.
