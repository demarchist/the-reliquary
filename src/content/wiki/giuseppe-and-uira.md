Giuseppe and Uira are two acolytes of the Guild — the Mage's Guild that controls all teleportation in the realms — assigned by Ossea to install a teleportation circle at [[feanons-tower|Fëanon's Tower]] in the Barony of Thunder Bluff. While overseeing the installation, they pursued personal projects of their own on the side.

With the teleportation circle complete, Giuseppe and Uira have since left the Barony, their work finished.
