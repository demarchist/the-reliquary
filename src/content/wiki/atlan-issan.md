Atlan Issan is a Zulkhir of the [[Red Wizards of Thay]]—one of the rulers of Thay's magocracy—and one of the last [[The Warlocks of Ceregorach|Warlocks of Ceregorach]]. In the Bloodstone Lands he is generally identified by title and first name: **Zulkhir Atlan**.

His rank makes his actions politically significant. Atlan was not a renegade mage lending private help to local conspirators. When he led the assault on the Aimu Estate, a ruler of Thay committed Thayan wizards, Oni, undead, a gated Balor, and coordination with vampires to the operation. His personal war with [[Cirrus Al Kayan|Cirrus]] is nested inside state power, not separate from it.

## The Archwizard at the Estate

Atlan fought from Greater Invisibility, controlling the field while other Red Wizards and Oni absorbed the party's attention. He opened a Gate to the Abyss and released a Balor, cut through the battle with Prismatic Spray and Cone of Cold, and taunted Cirrus and [[Fëanon Sërindë|Fëanon]] from concealment. He described Cirrus as a sacrifice along his road to godhood and promised his teeth to their lord.

[[Aliyah Faen Tlabbar|Patches]] exposed him with Faerie Fire. Once his Balor was banished and his subordinates began to fall, Atlan Teleported away rather than trade his life for the field. Cirrus recovered a few drops of blood from the scorched grass where he had stood.

The blood was black, thick, and tar-like. It did not behave as human blood should. Whether this is the result of his pact, his pursuit of divinity, Thayan alteration, or something still unrevealed, Atlan is no longer straightforwardly human.

## Teeth as a Weapon

Atlan's next strike was not a spell cast across a battlefield. It was a delivery to the [[Fane of Selune]]. Thousands of bloody teeth were heaped around the feet of the monumental statue of Cirrus, accompanied by a letter challenging him to return to the [[Bloodstone Lands]].

The choice of location and material was exact. Under [[Mediocris Dente]], Cirrus had been forced to place a tooth beneath his pillow every night as tribute. He later built the Fane as the home of his new faith and raised his own image through its central hall. Atlan placed the old bondage at the feet of the new identity, converting the most humiliating symbol of Cirrus's abuse into a threat inside his sanctuary.

The method of delivery is unknown. It proves Atlan knows where to reach Cirrus and can arrange a provocation inside the Barony; it does not prove that he personally penetrated the Fane's defenses.

Cirrus did not throw the teeth away. He ordered [[Topsy & Turvy|Topsy]] to pack them into his chambers and told everyone not to disturb him while he built through the night. What he intended to make remains unknown. The response nevertheless marks a reversal: Cirrus once surrendered teeth on his patron's terms; now he takes control of the tribute sent against him and decides its use for himself.

## One of Two

Atlan named himself one of only two servants of the Tooth Fairy who remain. Earlier, [[Selune]] had sent Cirrus after four warlocks: the halfling [[Strigu]], the Derro [[Bilrigger]], a Red Wizard, and a samurai in Kara-Tur. Strigu and Bilrigger are dead. The Red Wizard is Atlan. The remaining samurai is very likely [[Samonosuke]], the master who sent [[Letji]] to spy on Cirrus.

That synthesis explains the number without making it final. Other servants may exist outside Selûne's original four, and Ceregorach has survived at least one apparent death before. But within Cirrus's quest, Atlan is not merely the next target. He is the enemy who understands what the quest means to Cirrus and has chosen to make that understanding hurt.

## The Road to Godhood

Atlan has explicitly described a road to godhood, but neither its method nor its destination is known. His black blood suggests transformation already underway. His service to Ceregorach suggests dependence on an older power even as he claims to be ascending. His office as Zulkhir gives him access to a nation's arcane machinery while his private symbolism remains fixated on teeth, sacrifice, and Cirrus.

Those identities may support one another, or they may eventually conflict. For now, Atlan stands where several of the campaign's wars meet: Thay's designs on the Bloodstone Lands, Ceregorach's surviving network, and Cirrus's refusal to remain the creature his first patron made him.
