Temura is a high-elven Cleric of [[selune|Selune]] who arrived at the [[fane-of-selune|Fane of Selune]] with three human acolytes — Yuri, Clemence, and Gilga — to officially bring [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus']] temple into the wider religion. Bronze-skinned with bleached blonde hair, Temura has a sharp, predatory smile that never quite reaches his eyes and an eagerness to please that borders on insistence. He pushed [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] for a proper sermon and reading from the Astronomican, the holy book of [[selune|Selune]], and voiced his disapproval when the warlock strayed from the texts.

## Between Two Churches

Temura is not merely another convert in Cirrus's congregation. He arrived with ordination, doctrine, and a commission from the established [[Church of Selûne]] in [[silverymoon|Silverymoon]]. His presence offered legitimacy to the Fane while also placing someone inside it who recognised an authority beyond its founder.

That distinction became dangerous when [[Zulkhir Atlan Issan|Atlan's]] tribute of teeth reached the temple. Cirrus summoned Temura to *Fabricate* the teeth into a demonic image of himself, then transformed the effigy into a golem. Temura complied with the first working but made his disgust plain. He questioned Cirrus's intentions and wondered aloud whether the church in Silverymoon should be informed.

Temura's motives remain ambiguous. His earlier insistence on correct texts could be rigid orthodoxy, sincere pastoral concern, political oversight, or some combination of all three. But the tooth golem gave his concern substance. He is now being watched as a possible spy by the institution he was sent to regularise.

The cleric therefore sits on the fault line between devotion to Selûne and obedience to Cirrus. If those remain compatible, he can bridge the Fane and the wider faith. If they do not, his continued presence will force both organisations to decide which relationship they actually built.
