The Derelict is a [[Modrons|Modron]] spelljammer that crashed into the [[river-surbrin|River Surbrin]] near Mithral Hall over a century ago, its impact witnessed as a falling star by [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]], his father, and their gnomish friend [[cornubel|Cornubel]] on a gold-panning trip. By the time the group excavated it, the wreck had been buried beneath a thick shell of mountain ice, its golden hull unmistakably non-humanoid in origin. It is unrelated to [[The Acheron Spelljammer|the decades-neglected wreck recovered from Acheron]]: both are spelljammers linked to planar mysteries, but only the Derelict contained the Nonagon that altered the Rockseekers.

## Construction

The ship is built from Fabrial circuitry and artifice well beyond anything the group could fully parse — a level of technology that outstrips even the Amnian spelljammer wreckage found on Al Askar. Its access hatch took considerable searching to locate.

## The Core

At the heart of the vessel sits a nine-sided tetrahedron — a core that appears to defy physics, simultaneously two-dimensional and possessed of a kaleidoscopic, polygonal interior. It is cracked and clouded, and was, over a century ago, host to a dying Nonagon of the [[Modrons]] that called out telepathically to anyone nearby and charmed them into touching it. The entity fragmented on contact, killing Cornubel and embedding itself in Gundren and Pa' Rockseeker instead, reshaping their minds and erasing their memory of the whole encounter.

Cornubel's desiccated remains were found beside the core, undisturbed for a century. The group recovered him for burial and marked the wreck's location for the [[rockseeker-clan|Rockseeker clan]] to send an excavation team.
