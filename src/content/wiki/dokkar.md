Dokkar is an extra-planar city of good-aligned Dark Elves devoted to the goddess Elistraee, located in Svartalfheim on the plane of Ysgard. Unlike the treacherous Drow cities of the Underdark, Dokkar represents what Dark Elf civilization could be — a place of art, community, and devotion to a benevolent deity.

The city is accessed through magical means including a Well of Many Worlds. It contains the Penitent Gardens, the Cathedral of Twilight, and the workshops of artisans like Madame Toux who crafts Moonweave cloth. Finn eventually resides here, and Patches discovers connections to her ancestor Kallista, a priestess of both Elistraee and Selune.

## Moonfire

Dokkar hosts Moonfire, a joint gift of [[eilistraee|Elistraee]] and [[selune|Selûne]] and a festival occasion in its own right, held at a "regular" cadence — regular, at least, for a timeless extra-planar afterlife. [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] needs the rite to finish transmuting the gemstone staff at the heart of his planned Archwizard's staff, and inquired about receiving the blessing directly. He was told there is a waitlist centuries long — though the Ruling Houses' Council can supersede it. What the Council might want in exchange is, as yet, unknown.

## Visiting Dragons

The group has twice now heard rumblings of dragons coming to lair in Dokkar. On their most recent visit, they learned two were expected but only one arrived — [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] wonders whether this connects to [[azoo-the-archer|Azoo]], the Copper Dragon they helped resurrect and escort to her assigned lair on Bytopia rather than here. The connection, if any, is unconfirmed.
