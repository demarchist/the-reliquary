The Red Wizards of Thay are an ancient and powerful order of arcane practitioners from the eastern land of Thay. They supply weapons, assassins, and magical resources to various factions, making them a persistent and dangerous threat across Faerûn.

Their Thayan Drop Boxes — enchanted containers filled with weapons and resources — are discovered by the party, revealing the extent of their covert operations. The Red Wizards' involvement in regional conflicts adds another layer of danger to the party's already complex political landscape.

## The Delegation in Helios

By [[lord-aimu|Lord Aimu]]'s account, the Red Wizards have maintained a delegation at the Damaran court for some weeks — entertained openly by [[queen-concetinna-frostmantle|Queen Concetinna]] at the social functions she has continued to host while otherwise sequestered. The Wizards themselves appear only occasionally, even at their own honour-events, and have spent most of their time in the quarters provided.

A few days before Aimu fled the capital, the delegation requested an audience with the new Holy Relic of Lathander — the [[ashbringer|Sunsword]]. Aimu advised the Queen to refuse; she overrode him. He and [[lady-timea|Lady Timea]] escorted the Wizards and the Queen into the Cathedral, where the delegation spent a measured length of time examining the blade and the Queen handled it personally before returning it to its plinth. Something in the exchange put Lord Aimu on edge — the examination, the Queen's gaze on the radiance, the cool quality of her grip — and the nausea of a paladin's instinct climbed in him through the rest of the day until he took the blade that night.

The Wizards' interest in the Sunsword is openly *strong*. The reason is not yet known.

## Death over Frostmoore Vale

A Red Wizard rode at the head of a flight of eight [[ashen-white-dragon|Ashen White Dragons]] into [[frostmoore-vale|Frostmoore Vale]] at sundown — crimson robes flowing, pallid skin marked from temple to neck with dark tattooed runes, mounted on the largest of the dragons. He came to interdict the party's recovery of the [[ashbringer|Sunsword]] from [[lord-aimu|Lord Aimu]]'s chapel, working in coordination with the [[vampire-knight-of-frostmoore|Vampire Knight of Frostmoore]].

His confidence cost him. He sneered at [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]]'s attempted parlay and struck the floating monk down with a single arcane blast — and then [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] put a poisoned crossbow bolt into his thigh that paralysed him instantly. The Vampire Knight kicked off his saddle and flew across the gap to catch his ally, but the entire group focused fire on the rescue: half a dozen arrows and crossbow bolts hit the Vampire as he made the catch, and every one of them passed *through* the paralysed body in his arms.

The Red Wizard died in the Vampire Knight's arms. The Vampire took his body into a sphere of cold darkness and disappeared from the field. Whatever the Thayan's name and faction within the Red Wizards' hierarchy, his death over a Damaran nobleman's hidden estate signals a depth of Thayan involvement in the Vaasan-Damaran political situation that the party has not yet mapped — and that the delegation in Helios has not yet been confronted about.

## The Night Attack

The dusk engagement proved to be only the first blow. Hours later, in the dead of night, the Red Wizards returned in force for the [[the-thayans-attack|second attack]] on the [[aimu-estate|Aimu Estate]] — and this time they came with the full apparatus of their necromantic craft. A chanting voice rolled out of the treeline and an [[the-thayans-attack|earthquake]] tore the manor apart; a Red Wizard floated above the trees and blasted the rescuers with a lightning bolt; more Wizards cast from the forest to summon a horde of ghouls down the mountainside; and an Oni fought among them, striking from cover. The attack was coordinated with two vampires — the returned [[vampire-knight-of-frostmoore|Vampire Knight]] and the thrall [[gaddock|Gaddock]], the latter driven by a horn of black crystal that bound him to [[zhengyi|Zhengyi]].

When [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] dropped one of the floating Wizards into a [[bag-of-devouring|Bag of Devouring]], an unseen caster answered with a peal of cold laughter and a blast of arcane power — evidence that the force in the trees was larger than the handful the party had seen. The night attack made plain that the Thayan interest in the [[ashbringer|Sunsword]] was no diplomatic curiosity but a military objective, pursued with dragons, vampires, and the walking dead.

## A Zulkhir in the Field

The unseen commander at the Aimu Estate was [[Zulkhir Atlan Issan]], one of the rulers of Thay's magocracy as well as one of [[The Warlocks of Ceregorach|Ceregorach's last warlocks]]. He opened a Gate to the Abyss for a Balor, attacked under Greater Invisibility, and escaped by Teleport after the party destroyed his force. [[Cirrus Al Kayan|Cirrus]] recovered drops of tar-black blood from where he had stood, evidence that whatever power Atlan has pursued has changed him beyond ordinary humanity.

A delivery of thousands of bloody teeth arrived at the [[Fane of Selune]] with Atlan's letter challenging Cirrus to return to the Bloodstone Lands. The move binds two explanations for the Thayan campaign together rather than choosing between them. Thay has strategic interests in the Sunsword, Saurian weapons, and the politics of Damara; its ruling Zulkhir also has a personal religious war with Cirrus. State policy and private patronage are operating through the same archmage.

## The Harvest beneath Helios

The Thayan operation in [[helioglabulus|Helios]] is built to sustain itself. Nine-foot [[Thayan Flesh Golems|flesh golems]] collect citizens and batter through resistance. Armoured warlocks fight with green-flamed blades, Eldritch Blasts, and Armour of Agathys; when killed, their bodies snap back into working order unless destroyed completely or fed into the [[Bag of Devouring]]. Hundreds of zombies screen the older tunnels beneath the castle.

At the centre of one branch of [[The Catacombs of Helioglabulus|the catacombs]], a Red Wizard operated an elaborate workshop inside a tomb. A bleeding prisoner fed a floating black book that controlled the surrounding horde. Five iron frames or cages and extensive apparatus stood near a sarcophagus, while infant [[Oni]] fed on corpses and a Mummy Lord waited inside the tomb.

These are not disconnected monsters. Golems transport bodies from the streets; undead troops secure the routes; the book coordinates them; and the workshop processes whatever arrives. Combined with the delegation's court access and the nobles' withdrawal, the Red Wizards appear to be operating civic infrastructure of their own beneath the capital.

The political and occult objectives may also converge in the Sunsword. After the Queen handled it for the delegation, a presence [[Cirrus Al Kayan|Cirrus]] identified as [[Malcathet]] could answer through its fingerprint and reject a Wish. Whether the Wizards installed that connection, studied it, strengthened it, or merely recognised it remains unknown.
