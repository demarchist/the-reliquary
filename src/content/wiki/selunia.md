Selunia is the city at the heart of the Barony of [[thunder-bluff|Thunder Bluff]]. It was founded as New Daggerford, and most people still call it that; Selunia, the name it is slowly coming to be known by, honours the goddess [[selune|Selûne]]. The barony itself is often called the Barony of Selunia. It has grown from a handful of houses into a walled city under the stewardship of the [[faerun-defense-force|Faerûn Defense Force]], and Lord [[neverember|Neverember]] designated it a base of operations, sending a garrison and resources to support its development.

## The City

New Daggerford stands on a hill where the shelf ends at the gorge, between [[lake-seluna|Lake Selûna]] and the [[thunder-rapids|Thunder Rapids]]. The streets climb from the gates to the Keep, which crowns the hill on its prow over the gorge, and the west side of town leans down toward the lake's river. From the walls the land falls away on every side. It was never planned. It grew along its old roads, and its lanes run wherever the blocks between them left room, tight and tall near the middle and loose and gardened near the walls. The Trade Way runs straight through it as its main street. The wall follows the landward side only, because the gorge needs no help, and suburbs straggle out along the roads beyond each gate. The cobbled road to the [[fane-of-selune|Fane]] leaves by the north gate, and the road along the shelf runs on north-east to [[kossenberg|Kossenberg]].

- **[[keep-of-thunder-bluff|The Keep of Thunder Bluff]]** stands at the city's heart, on a prow of rock over the gorge, with the [[temple-of-helm|Temple of Helm]] inside its walls.
- **The Crossroads**, where the Trade Way meets the street to the Keep, is the middle of town. Over the Moon stands there, a three-storey manor of brick and timber that is the tavern, the inn and the town hall. **[[the-establishment|The Establishment]]** and the **[[jmjm-theater|JMJM Theater]]** face each other across the street.
- **[[rockseeker-manufactory|The Rockseeker Manufactory]]** lies just outside the western wall, on the gorge's rim.
- **[[high-hold|High-Hold]]**, the ruined castle on the next prow of the bluff, has the city grown up round it on three sides; nobody builds on it.

## The River Works

The lake's river runs down the city's west side between stone banks, stepped down by low weirs and held to its line, because the land beyond it lies lower than the water. The river goes over the edge at [[thunder-rapids|Hammerfall]], from a lip the barony armoured to stop the falls eating their way back toward the streets. The Trade Way crosses it at the Upper Bridge.

## Landmarks

Beyond the walls the rolling landscape stretches to the [[the-moonwood|Moonwood]] and the [[evermoors|Evermoors]]. Key landmarks include the [[keep-of-thunder-bluff|Keep of Thunder Bluff]] with its red-hued stone, the [[rockseeker-manufactory|Rockseeker Manufactory]] where [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]]'s dwarves produce firearms and extradimensional latrines, [[feanons-tower|Fëanon's Tower]] with its teleportation chamber, the Fane of [[selune|Selûne]] on the shore of Lake Selûna where [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] tends to the moon goddess, and Over the Moon — the tavern where residents and adventurers gather. [[mordenkainen|Mordenkainen]] himself arrived to complete the city's defensive walls during the party's Underdark campaigns.

## A Growing Community

Freed slaves from across the North have made their way to Selunia, drawn by the promise of a fresh start under the party's protection. The first caravan of thirty-two freed slaves arrived from [[neverwinter|Neverwinter]], and newcomers are told to seek out Li Tan upon arrival. The Barony received 150 parcels of new land from [[silverymoon|Silverymoon]], and a mutual protection pact with [[blingdenstone|Blingdenstone]] yields three thousand gold in cut gemstones and five hundred gold in salt per month. Local life includes festivals honouring Lathander, the presence of Beryan the Sage at Fëanon's library, and the cultural mixing of former enemies — Drow, humans, dwarves, and halflings — living side by side.

## Under Threat

Despite its walls and garrison, Selunia remains vulnerable. The dragon [[ancalagon|Ancalagon]] retaliated against the party by dropping abducted civilians onto the streets of New Daggerford — multiple innocents killed on impact. [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]], returning from the [[shadowfell|Shadowfell]], stood at the Fane and detected absolute silence in the nearby [[the-moonwood|Moonwood]] — an ominous sign of unknown danger pressing against the Barony's southern border.
