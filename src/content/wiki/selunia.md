Selunia is the greater barony: the name the people of [[thunder-bluff|Thunder Bluff]] give their whole country, in honour of the goddess [[selune|Selûne]]. Spread around [[lake-seluna|Lake Selûna]] and anchored by [[feanons-tower|Fëanon's Tower]], the city of [[kossenberg|New Kossenberg]] and the [[keep-of-thunder-bluff|Keep of Thunder Bluff]], it has grown from a frontier outpost into a thriving community under the stewardship of the [[faerun-defense-force|Faerûn Defense Force]]. Lord [[neverember|Neverember]] designated Selunia as a base of operations, sending a garrison and resources to support its development.

## Landmarks

The country lies where sunlight gleams off Lake Selûna and the rolling landscape stretches to the [[moonwood|Moonwood]] and the [[evermoors|Evermoors]]. Key landmarks include the [[keep-of-thunder-bluff|Keep of Thunder Bluff]] with its red-hued stone, the [[rockseeker-manufactory|Rockseeker Manufactory]] where [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]]'s dwarves produce firearms and extradimensional latrines, [[feanons-tower|Fëanon's Tower]] with its teleportation chamber, the Fane of [[selune|Selûne]] on the shore of Lake Selûna where [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] tends to the moon goddess, and Over the Moon — the tavern where residents and adventurers gather. [[mordenkainen|Mordenkainen]] himself arrived to complete New Kossenberg's defensive walls during the party's Underdark campaigns.

## A Growing Community

Freed slaves from across the North have made their way to Selunia, drawn by the promise of a fresh start under the party's protection. The first caravan of thirty-two freed slaves arrived from [[neverwinter|Neverwinter]], and newcomers are told to seek out Li Tan upon arrival. The Barony received 150 parcels of new land from [[silverymoon|Silverymoon]], and a mutual protection pact with [[blingdenstone|Blingdenstone]] yields three thousand gold in cut gemstones and five hundred gold in salt per month. Local life includes festivals honouring Lathander, the presence of Beryan the Sage at Fëanon's library, and the cultural mixing of former enemies — Drow, humans, dwarves, and halflings — living side by side.

## Under Threat

Despite its walls and garrison, Selunia remains vulnerable. The dragon [[ancalagon|Ancalagon]] retaliated against the party by dropping abducted civilians onto the streets of New Kossenberg — multiple innocents killed on impact. [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]], returning from the [[shadowfell|Shadowfell]], stood at the Fane and detected absolute silence in the nearby [[moonwood|Moonwood]] — an ominous sign of unknown danger pressing against the Barony's southern border.
