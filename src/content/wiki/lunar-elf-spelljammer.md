The Lunar Elf Spelljammer is the wreck the group cleared on [[acheron|Acheron]] while recovering [[candlekeep|Candlekeep]]'s missing book. It had crashed and splintered across a metal cube decades earlier, rusted and rotted, its hull crawling with Swordwraiths, wraiths, and the [[dean-rochester|book-borrower]]'s own spirit, now an Allip.

The ship had long since been stripped of anything magical or mechanically useful, but [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]] found the artistry familiar and named it a spelljammer of the **Lunar Elves**. He insisted on salvaging it regardless, and the group Gated the wreck back to the Barony — [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] reversing the gravity of the larger pieces so they fell through the portal, the Goblinoid [[acheron|War Party]] hauling the rest before losing corporeality on the far side and dropping it all in a heap for the bewildered Barony guard. [[bohd|Bohd]] arrived on his wyvern and had the guard move the debris clear of the Gate, and most of the ship was recovered — in many, many pieces. By the following days it was being catalogued at the [[rockseeker-manufactory|Manufactory]] alongside the [[the-derelict|Modron Derelict]].
