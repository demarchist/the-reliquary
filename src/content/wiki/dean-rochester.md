Dean Rochester holds authority over [[candlekeep|Candlekeep]]'s collections and the conditions attached to them. He is a thin, elderly human with a monk's tonsure and brass spectacles, dressed in absurdly ornate golden robes and a great many precious rings, working from an office marked with the golden symbol of [[mystra|Mystra]].

## The Mandolin

He recognised [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] at once as "Fëanon of the Lady's College," and took an immediate dislike to [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]]. He was not opposed to releasing the Mandolin — one of the seven [[instruments-of-the-bards|Instruments of the Bards]] — but it had been donated on the condition that only a sufficiently worthy bardic hero could take it, and the college would have to judge JmJm's worth. His proposed test: recover a book that had been missing for a century, traced to the plane of [[acheron|Acheron]].

When JmJm stormed out, the Dean turned obstinate — demanding payment for wards the group had "broken" by Teleporting in. He was eventually mollified, agreed terms (a Planeshift fork to be replaced on site, the borrower's Ioun stone as a tracking aid), and separately entered a standing tome-exchange agreement with Fëanon and sold [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] an Abyssal fork. What finally warmed him was JmJm Wishing 25,000 gold into his hands with a flattering note; he sent back a thank-you by Instant Messenger and released the iron fork.
