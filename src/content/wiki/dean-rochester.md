Dean Rochester is a senior authority at [[Candlekeep]] and the institutional gatekeeper responsible for the Mandolin in the Elminster Annex. He is a thin elderly human with a monk's tonsure, brass spectacles, precious rings on his fingers, and extraordinarily ornate golden robes. Even his hospitality is theatrical: he summons chairs with a snap of his fingers, while the door to his office bears the golden sign of [[Mystra]]. [[Emir Suleiman al-Zahir]] serves as the practical bridge between Rochester's authority and the Annex's defenses.

The display invites an easy reading of vanity, and Rochester is certainly proud. It does not make him unserious.

## Custodian of Conditions

The Mandolin's donor stipulated that it could pass only to a sufficiently worthy bardic hero. Rochester treated that condition as a duty rather than a price tag. He was willing to release one of [[The Instruments of the Bards|the Instruments of the Bards]], but he would not allow [[Jack Mayer Johnson Mathews|JmJm]] to declare himself worthy merely by arriving and wanting it.

His proposed trial solved an institutional problem at the same time. A High Elf had borrowed a book a century earlier; Candlekeep had recently traced the missing volume to [[Acheron]] and still held the borrower's Ioun-stone collateral. Before proposing the retrieval, Rochester studied the party, estimated their power, and smiled knowingly. Candlekeep supplied the planar access and sympathetic link while the party supplied the expedition. The sequence suggests Rochester deliberately matched a capable company to a stranded institutional problem, making worthiness measurable as useful service.

## Three Kinds of Leverage

The meeting brought three very different forms of influence into Rochester's office.

JmJm offered force of personality and eventually twenty-five thousand gold conjured by Wish. [[Aliyah Faen Tlabbar|Patches]] quietly tested whether [[Lady Faye]] and the [[The Zhentarim|Zhentarim]] could buy pressure inside the college. [[Fëanon Sërindë|Fëanon]] arrived with a reputation Rochester already knew from the Lady's College and offered scholarly exchange.

Rochester responded differently to each. He was affronted when JmJm stormed out, claimed compensation for wards supposedly damaged by the party's Teleportation, and sent thanks after the gold arrived. The Black Network proved to have little influence inside Candlekeep. With Fëanon, Rochester established an ongoing exchange of rare and extra-planar books and supplied lodging and the information required for the expedition.

This is not evidence that the Dean is incorruptible. It is evidence that his preferred currency is institutional value, and that he is perfectly willing to let personal pride and public duty profit one another.

## The Completed Bargain

JmJm laughed after Rochester's thank-you note arrived and declared some people easy to manipulate. The outcome complicates that boast. Rochester received twenty-five thousand gold, the return of a century-overdue book, a planar fork, and a new scholarly relationship with Fëanon. He never waived the donor's condition.

When the task was complete, Candlekeep sent the Mandolin and [[Cirrus Al Kayan|Cirrus]]'s separately negotiated Abyssal fork with a note addressed to *The Bard*. Rochester therefore kept the bargain exactly: demanding, transactional, personally prickly, but not faithless. JmJm got the Instrument. The Dean got everything he asked for and more.
