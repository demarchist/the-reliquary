Modrons are mechanical beings from the Plane of Mechanus, organised into a hierarchy whose ranks include entities such as Hierarchs, Decatons, and Nonagons. Their technology combines precise brass machinery, geometric forms that do not always obey ordinary dimensions, and forms of artifice well beyond what even [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren Rockseeker]] can readily reproduce.

They are often described as creatures of perfect order. The party's encounters have almost never found them under orderly circumstances.

## The March and the Labyrinth

The first direct evidence came through [[Gravenhollow]]. Gundren witnessed Modrons constructing a [[Delzoun Instant Fortress]] inside a much larger Modron structure. Later reports placed a great Modron March in [[The Labyrinth|the Labyrinth]] of the [[Underdark]] roughly twenty years earlier. A substantial portion of the march had passed into the maze, and mechanical stragglers continued to emerge for years afterward—usually lesser beings, occasionally something larger and more complex.

When the party eventually entered the Labyrinth, Gundren found a piece of Modron machinery and became so absorbed by it that [[Fëanon Sërindë|Fëanon]] watched him with alarm. Nearby ruins contained complex metal fittings, ancient worked panels, and steam-driven mechanical guardians. Not every machine in those ruins was proven Modron-made, but Gundren's reaction was the first behavioural sign that his interest was more than professional curiosity.

## The Captive Hierarch

On Al Askar, the party found a captured Modron Hierarch wired into the core of a crashed Amnian spelljammer as an enslaved intelligence. It spoke in a melodic language understood by both Gundren and [[Tarkathee Daru|Tark]], repeating: *Decaton, help me.*

Gundren touched it.

A flash knocked him unconscious, the symbol of Primus glowing on his forehead. The Hierarch's intelligence appeared to transfer into the Dwarf, joining rather than replacing him. What first looked like the origin of Gundren's connection was later revealed as its second layer.

## The Nonagon in the River

More than a century earlier, Gundren, Pa' Rockseeker, and their gnomish friend [[Cornubel]] had found [[The Derelict|a Modron spelljammer]] crashed beneath the [[River Surbrin]]. At its heart was a dying Nonagon housed in a cracked geometric core—simultaneously two-dimensional and kaleidoscopically deep. It charmed all three travellers into touching it.

The fragment killed Cornubel and took refuge in the two Dwarves, erasing their memory of the encounter and reshaping their personalities. Gundren's affinity for technology, and at least part of the cold calculation that entered Pa' Rockseeker after the trip, may therefore have been cultivated by a Modron mind living inside each of them rather than arising independently.

On Al Askar, the Crystal Dragon Jareem later read Gundren psionically and described him as a life-raft carrying *two* Modron minds. The Hierarch did not overwrite the older Nonagon fragment. Both remained.

## The Barony and Acheron

Modrons subsequently appeared in the courtyard of [[The Keep of Thunder Bluff|Thunder Bluff's keep]], constructing a large magical apparatus. Gundren suspected a gate to Mechanus and immediately worried about securing it. Whether the workers came for the minds within him, for the recovered Hierarch, or for some unrelated purpose has not yet been established.

On [[Acheron]], the relationship became practical rather than merely archaeological. Goblinoids cornered an injured Modron and lost several of their number to its defense. Gundren stopped the mob from destroying it, repaired the construct, and learned of a Modron channel or passage along the party's route. The repaired being travelled with the war party for a time; its eventual fate is unrecorded.

The act matters because it reverses the pattern of the earlier encounters. The party had found Modrons crashed, enslaved, fragmented, lost after a March, or embedded in a foreign mind. On Acheron, Gundren met one that could still be helped and acted protectively before asking it for information—a strong indication that he regarded it as more than machinery, even if his motive was not stated.

## Gundren's Place in the Hierarchy

It is tempting to describe Gundren as chosen by Primus or assimilated by the Modrons. Neither is confirmed. He remains himself: stubborn, inventive, emotional, and capable of protecting a damaged construct in a way no doctrine of perfect order required.

But he is not simply an Artificer who studies Modron work. He is a refuge for two of their minds, can understand at least some of their speech, bears the sign of Primus, and repeatedly draws Modron machines and beings into his orbit. The unanswered question is no longer whether Gundren has a connection to the hierarchy. It is what the hierarchy believes he has become.
