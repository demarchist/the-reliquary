The Catacombs of [[helioglabulus|Helioglabulus]] are a deep complex of sewers, compressed ruins, cisterns, tombs, and undercroft passages beneath the capital and its castle. Their oldest human masonry appears to predate the visible city above. By the time the party entered, the [[Red Wizards of Thay]] had turned those forgotten layers into both a transport network and a necromantic industry.

## Seen from Gravenhollow

The catacombs first appeared in [[Horatio Von Bumidere|Buumi's]] vision at [[gravenhollow|Gravenhollow]]. Searching for [[Gaddock|Gaddock's]] coffin, he saw ancient human tombs crowded with hundreds of infant [[Oni]]—small tusked creatures fighting, feeding on the weak, and learning to shapeshift. They kept clear of three black coffins in a well-maintained vaulted chamber.

The group inferred that one coffin belonged to Gaddock. They suspect the others belong to [[Queen Concetinna Frostmantle|Queen Concetinna]] and the [[Vampire Knight of Frostmoore]], but the vision did not identify them directly.

## The Road Below

[[Mister Luko]] supplied maps through the undercity after [[Thayan Flesh Golems|Thayan golems]] attacked the [[Rising Sun]]. Bloody handprints, clothing, torn nails, and drag marks showed that abducted citizens were being moved along the route. In the great blue-lit cistern below the sewers, the party saw one of the golems carrying two bodies down a concealed stair.

That traffic reframes the disappearances above. Helios is not merely being terrorised. Its population is being harvested and delivered beneath the castle, while the reduced presence of the [[Crimson Dawn Knights]] leaves the collection routes open.

## The Necromantic Workshop

Beyond the cistern, hundreds of zombies screened an ornate tomb converted into a laboratory. The chamber contained a Red Wizard, an undead warlock, five iron frames or cages, tools and apparatus around a sarcophagus, infant [[Oni]] feeding on corpses, and a chained prisoner bleeding into a floating black book.

The book animated or coordinated the surrounding horde. When [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] destroyed it, blood flooded the chamber and every zombie outside collapsed at once. The sarcophagus then opened and released a Mummy Lord, suggesting that the tomb's original occupant had been incorporated into the Thayan defence rather than merely displaced by it.

## A Buried State

The catacombs unite several parts of the Bloodstone conspiracy in one physical system: missing citizens become material; golems collect them; warlocks and zombies protect the routes; Thayan necromancers process them; infant Oni mature among the dead; and the suspected coffins of the realm's vampiric powers lie farther within.

This is more than a secret lair. It is a second government under the capital—one concerned with bodies, transformation, and control while the official court withdraws behind guarded doors above.
