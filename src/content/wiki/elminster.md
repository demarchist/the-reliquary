Elminster is one of Faerûn's most storied archmages, a Chosen of [[mystra|Mystra]], and — by the group's growing suspicion — a hand behind [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]]'s quest for the [[instruments-of-the-bards|Instruments of the Bards]]. The group has not met him. They met his collection.

## The Elminster Annex

[[candlekeep|Candlekeep]] maintains a wing given over entirely to Elminster: the **Elminster Annex**, guarded by the Djinni [[emir-suleiman-al-zahir|al-Zahir]] and defended by animate arcane armour and figures that step out of their own paintings. The group's Teleport from the [[river-surbrin|Surbrin]] landed them in the middle of it, surrounded by cases — a depleted wand of one of his apprentices, the journal of a lord who witnessed the Shadow Princes of Shade Enclave attack the Dales, and more. It was also, on its plinth, the Mandolin they had come to Candlekeep to find.
