Emir Suleiman al-Zahir is the Djinni charged with the arcane security of the **Elminster Annex** at [[candlekeep|Candlekeep]] — the wing of the library given over to relics and records of Elminster. When the group's Teleport dropped them into the middle of the Annex's display hall, its defences woke around them: arcane armour stepping off its plinths, painted figures drawing weapons and stepping out of their frames, and a gale of wind that gathered itself into al-Zahir's storm-edged shape.

He cannot permit anything to be removed from the displays, but he offered to escort the group to [[dean-rochester|Dean Rochester]] to negotiate for the Mandolin. On the walk, [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] claimed to understand the nature of the Djinn, having once True Polymorphed into one; al-Zahir likened this to cultural appropriation and told him a Warlock could never know what it is to truly live in the grand city of **Ossa**, among its noble houses. After the deal was done, a far more human-looking al-Zahir delivered the rusted iron planar fork to the group's cottage himself.
