Thunder Bluff is a thousand-foot cliff overlooking the River Rauvin along the Trade Way between [[neverwinter|Neverwinter]] and [[silverymoon|Silverymoon]], the thunder of rapids echoing off its sides. To the north rises the magical forest of the [[the-moonwood|Moonwood]]; to the south, the grey expanse of the [[the-evermoors|Evermoors]] stretches to the horizon. Between them lies [[selunia|Selunia]], the greater barony, and at its heart the walled city of [[selunia|New Daggerford]].

## Geography

The country descends in great steps from the frigid heights of the north toward the endless southern swamps, each tier distinct in character. A traveller coming down from the Moonwood passes in a dozen miles from ancient forest, over the edge of a two-thousand-foot wall, across a misted lake and a farmed plain, and into a gorge where the river can be heard long before it can be seen.

### The High Moonwood Plateau

Dominating the northern and western reaches, the [[the-moonwood|Moonwood]] occupies a vast elevated plateau — the remnant bed of a long-receded glacier. Its surface lies more than two thousand feet above the lands to the south, and the ice left its mark: long ridges and hollows all running one way, north to south, with dark little lakes lying in the hollows under dense ancient forest. A mighty river, born from the glacial meltwaters of the Spine of the World far to the north, carves a valley through the heart of the plateau and flows southward to its edge.

### The Red Hills and Lake Selûna

The southern margin of the Moonwood ends in the Red Hills: sheer walls of red sandstone that stand up to twenty-four hundred feet above the water, in a great horseshoe more than three miles across. The great river goes over the head of the horseshoe in a broad, broken curtain more than seventeen hundred feet high, and two lesser streams fall nearly two thousand feet from the shoulders on either side, sending curtains of mist down onto [[lake-seluna|Lake Selûna]] far below.

The horseshoe is no accident of the river. Beneath the plateau lies the scar of something that fell from the sky in an age before the sandstone was laid down: a buried crater, packed with soft sediment. As the falls ate their way north through the cliff, they broke into it, and the soft fill washed away. That is the lake. What would not wash away was the crater's heart, a knot of rock welded in the impact and harder than anything else for miles. That is [[serinde-isle|Sërindë Isle]], and [[feanons-tower|Fëanon's Tower]] is founded on it.

East and west of the lake the cliffs do not end so much as fade. The red sandstone thins toward its margins, and where it runs out the wall has nothing left to stand on and sinks away into wooded hills.

### The Thunder Bluff Shelf

South of the lake lies the Thunder Bluff shelf — a broad bench of ancient, hard rock, planed nearly flat, with low hills left standing where the rock resisted. The shore of the lake is still moor and wood, crossed by a single road to the [[fane-of-selune|Fane]]. Beyond it the land has gone under the plough, and it is farmed in hedged fields from the cliffs' foot to the city's walls.

The Trade Way comes up the north bank of the Rauvin from the south-east, keeping to the river until the shelf opens out, and crosses it to the north-west. It is the shelf's one great road: broad and well metalled, with worn shoulders and old trees along it outside the walls, hamlets at its halts, and New Daggerford as its main street.

The lake's river leaves by its old course and runs down the west side of the city, held to its line by stone banks and low weirs. The land to the west lies lower than the water; without the barony's works, the river would leave. A millrace off it drives the wheels of the [[rockseeker-manufactory|Rockseeker Manufactory]].

Kossenberg is older than any of it: the farming village was working the shelf before the barony was a barony. It stands on the road along the shelf north-east of the city, toward the cliffs' foot, its crofts running back into the fields. East of the farms, in the woods on the scarp, is [[tarks-dojo|Tark's dojo]], and south-east of it, under the eastern hills and further out from the city than anything else on the shelf, is Doc's druid grove: [[old-stone-grove|the Old Stone grove]], a sunken dale with the standing stone in its clearing. In the woods north of Kossenberg, on the way to the Fane, a hole in the ground among heaps of gravel is all anyone sees of [[snorbuls-hole|Snorbul's Hole]].

### New Daggerford

The shelf ends at the gorge, and New Daggerford — slowly coming to be called [[selunia|Selunia]] — stands right on its edge: an irregular walled city of some five thousand roofs, grown along its old roads rather than laid out. Its lanes are narrow and no two run the same way, and they tighten around the crossroads where the Trade Way meets the street to the Keep. There, across the street from each other, stand the [[the-establishment|Establishment]], plain as a barn and doing its best to stay unnoticed, and the [[jmjm-theater|JMJM Theater]], which is doing the opposite.

### Thunder Bluff and the Keep

Where the city's hill meets the gorge, a prow of rock stands out over the river with the drop on three sides. This is Thunder Bluff itself. The [[keep-of-thunder-bluff|Keep of Thunder Bluff]] stands on it, its seamless red walls rising straight from the edge, with the copper dome of the [[temple-of-helm|Temple of Helm]] inside them and a thousand feet of air beneath.

### The Thunder Rapids and the Rauvin

East of the city the Rauvin is a broad, quiet river, lying barely a hundred feet below the fields. Then it reaches a line of hard rock, the Doorstep, and to anyone on the water it simply stops, with spray rising from beyond the edge. From there the [[thunder-rapids|Thunder Rapids]] fall some nine hundred feet in under five miles, through a twisting slot in the shelf's ancient rock. The slot is narrow, and its floor is choked with blocks the size of houses, shed from the walls faster than even this river can clear them. The roar is said to be audible for miles, and gives the entire region its name.

The river follows a great fault. The shelf is the side that rose and the land to the south is the side that fell, so the gorge's south rim stands well below the north, by as much as four hundred feet under the Keep, where the city's hill comes to the edge. West of the city the lake's river joins it the hard way, going off the shelf's edge at [[thunder-rapids|Hammerfall]]: nearly a thousand feet, onto a pool called the Anvil. Below that the doubled Rauvin runs on westward toward the broader continent. South of it, the land falls away to the vast, low-lying [[the-evermoors|Evermoors]] — a seemingly endless expanse of dark swamps, marshes and wetlands, with slow-moving black water and a brooding, primordial air.

## The Barony

The Barony of Thunder Bluff was awarded to the party by High Marshal Arasume in the aftermath of the failed Amnian coup at [[silverymoon|Silverymoon]]. The entire region for miles around lies within the barony's political boundaries, governed by a council of adventurers who call themselves the [[faerun-defense-force|Faerûn Defense Force]] — though their detractors know them as the Heralds of Woe. Its city, New Daggerford, is slowly coming to be called [[selunia|Selunia]], and the barony often goes by that name too.

When the party first arrived, the ranger Moogie gave them a survey: the ruined castle of High Hold (Moorwatch), destroyed two years earlier by the Orcish Horde; the village of [[kossenberg|Kossenberg]], at the foot of the Moonwood; Old Kossenberg, the ruins of the original settlement, down on the Rauvin's bank east of the city; Lake [[selune|Selûna]] and its ranger lodge; the Red Hill with its iron deposits and the Glittering Caves beneath; and Oldstone, a sacred menhir older than local memory, in what became Doc's [[old-stone-grove|Old Stone grove]].

## Settlement

A tribe of orc deserters who had fled the Horde and been driven from caves beneath the bluff by the spirit troll Shadowclaw were the barony's first citizens. The party offered them land in the barony — the ruins of Old Kossenberg were the first thing named — in exchange for proof of strength: destroying Shadowclaw from the caverns. The tribe settled in Thunder Bluff itself, at [[shadowmoon-village|Shadowmoon Village]] on the gorge's south rim, directly across from the Keep. It is still a primitive place, palisade, huts and longhouses, but it has grown large. Freed slaves from across the North later made their way to [[selunia|Selunia]], drawn by the promise of a fresh start.
