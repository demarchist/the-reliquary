Rex Kwondo is a self-styled martial-arts master operating in the Barony of [[thunder-bluff|Thunder Bluff]], who built a following by trading on [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]]'s good name to teach what [[lord-aimu|Lord Aimu]]'s paladins considered buffoonery. The paladins evicted him from the Dojo as a charlatan — and were genuinely aghast when Tark defended the man rather than thanking them. Confronted with their own arrogance, they set out to find Rex and make it right.
