Rex Kwondo is a martial instructor whose dojo lessons were regarded by [[Lord Aimu|Lord Aimu's]] paladins as buffoonery. More seriously, he taught under the borrowed authority of [[Tarkathee Daru|Tark's]] good name without the monk's apparent permission.

The paladins expelled him and expected Tark's approval. Tark defended Rex instead.

## Borrowed Authority

The incident is comic, but the mistake cuts both ways. Rex used Tark's reputation to lend legitimacy to his teaching. The paladins then used that same reputation to justify punishing him, assuming that protecting Tark's dignity mattered more than learning Tark's wishes.

Tark's defence forced them to recognise their own presumption. They immediately set out to find Rex and remedy the situation. Whether he returned to the dojo, apologised for using Tark's name, or altered his teaching is not yet recorded.

Rex is one small sign that Tark has become an institution outside his own control. His name now authorises schools, attracts imitators, and inspires enforcement by people who know his legend better than they know him.
