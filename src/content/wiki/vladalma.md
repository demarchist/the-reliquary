## Background

Vladalma is the Matron Mother of House Faen Tlabbar, one of the noble drow houses of Menzoberranzan. She is the mother of Patches (Aliyah Faen Tlabbar) and exercises her authority with ruthless determination.

## Role in the Campaign

Vladalma operates from the shadows, using agents like Lo'Dan to spy on her daughter Patches and ordering assassination missions that create complications for the party. Her machinations represent the ever-present threat of drow politics reaching into surface affairs.

## Notable Traits

- Matron Mother wielding considerable political power in Menzoberranzan
- Manipulative mother who uses her daughter as a pawn
- Commands a network of spies and assassins

## The Vision of Vaasa

In the Halls of the Future at [[gravenhollow|Gravenhollow]], [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] sat with the archivist [[ustova|Ustova]] and saw a vision of her own death: fields of flowers blooming across a flat summer tundra, her friends' bodies scattered around her, and Vladalma standing over her with a ritual dagger in her charcoal hands as [[ancalagon|Ancalagon]] looked on with glee. Patches recognised the flowers from the party's recent time in the Bloodstone lands and concluded her mother intends to work with Ancalagon — and possibly [[zhengyi|Zhengyi]] — in the tundra of [[vaasa|Vaasa]].

## Hit Squads

[[lodan|Lo'Dan]] confirmed to Patches that Vladalma has hit-squads, each led by one of Patches' sisters, scattered across the world searching for her. A simulacrum of [[jarlaxle|Jarlaxle]] later reported that Menzoberranzan itself has gone strangely quiet since the Demon Lords were banished back to the Abyss, with Vladalma's priestesses growing reclusive — raising fears of another Silence of Lolth, though their divine magic still functions normally.
