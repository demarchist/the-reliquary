The Undercroft is the buried network of sewers, catacombs, and ruin beneath [[helioglabulus|Helios]], reaching from the city's Thieves' Cant-marked drains down to human stonework older than the city built above it. After the attack on the [[rising-sun|Rising Sun]], [[mister-luko|Mister Luko]]'s survivors furnished the group with a multi-layered map of the Undercity, transcribed by [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]], tracing the routes the Thayans have been using to drag victims down from the streets toward the castle.

## The Sewers and the Cistern

Guided at first by one of Luko's footpads, the group followed clear signs of recent, violent traffic — torn fingernails, bloodied handprints, scraps of clothing — down through sewers that gave way in stretches to old catacombs and compressed ruin before opening onto a great Cistern: a vast cavern lit a soft blue by bioluminescent algae, fed by a dozen tunnel mouths pouring greywater a hundred feet down to a pool below. A concealed stair spirals down the cistern wall, used by the Thayans' golems to carry victims into the depths, guarded from below by a gargantuan Otyugh that rose from the water to feed before the group drove it back under.

## The Old Ruins

Past the Cistern the tunnels open into far older construction — the oldest human stonework in the city — and the stench of rot thickens. A wide corridor here held a horde of several hundred Zombies, tall enough that flying party members could have cleared it outright, milling in the dark beneath a hidden tomb chamber sealed behind a heavy stone door.

## The Tomb Chamber

Scouted by [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] along the ceiling above the horde, the chamber housed a [[red-wizards-of-thay|Thayan]] necromancer's working lab: an ornate sarcophagus, five iron cages, tables of tools, and a black book floating open beneath a chained prisoner bled steadily into its pages — the engine animating the horde outside. A closed iron door stood behind it, unopened. Two infant Oni gnawed at the dead nearby — the same breed [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]] once saw swarming a vault of black coffins in his [[gravenhollow|Gravenhollow]] vision. Whatever lies behind that door, the group has not found it yet.

The group stormed the chamber, killed the Oni and the prisoner in a trio of Fireballs, and destroyed the book — dropping the entire horde outside dead in an instant. The sarcophagus proved to hold a Mummylord, which rose to fight alongside the [[red-wizards-of-thay|Thayan]] Necromancer as the group closed in.
