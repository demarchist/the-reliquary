Jarlaxle is the flamboyant leader of Bregan D'aerthe, the outlaw Drow mercenary organization. Operating outside Menzoberranzan's official power structure, he maintains a network of agents and operatives including the psionicist Kimmuriel and the local leader Lo'Dan.

Jarlaxle negotiates with the party for the release of Finn (Patches' son) and provides aid against the Demon Lords threatening the Underdark. His pragmatic, self-serving charm makes him a valuable but unpredictable ally.

## The Banquet Simulacrum

Summoned by [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] through [[lady-isabel|Izmakella]], Jarlaxle arrived uninvited at [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]]'s Thespian Banquet — sweeping in past a startled Steven the Thespian with a low bow and a "I was summoned, my lady?" He revealed the body in the room was only a simulacrum, the real Jarlaxle indisposed in [[menzoberranzan|Menzoberranzan]], and settled in with a glass of wine to answer Patches' questions.

He laid out the current positions of all five of her sisters: Bushra and Nasra in [[baldurs-gate|Baldur's Gate]], Dahlia within an hour's magical travel of the Barony, [[ishta|Ishta]] in the Bloodstone lands with the House Wizard [[jacopo|Jacopo]], and Fairuza at their mother [[vladalma|Vladalma]]'s side in Menzoberranzan. He noted Menzoberranzan had gone strangely quiet since the Demon Lords were banished, its priestesses reclusive, and flagged two threads his agents had picked up — Bushra summoning extraplanar entities in Baldur's Gate, and Ishta making back-channel inquiries about fleeing [[house-faen-tlabbar|House Faen Tlabbar]] through [[bregan-daerthe|Bregan D'aerthe]], much as Patches once did.

Before leaving, he struck a trade deal with JmJm — Khonsfa goods at a discount in exchange for a herd of Carrion Crawlers for Snorbul's stables.
