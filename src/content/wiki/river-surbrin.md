The River Surbrin runs through the summer slopes of the Spine of the World Mountains near Mithral Hall, its upper reaches a country of alpine wildflower meadows and small lakes below the snow line. For generations it has been a traditional Dwarven gold-panning site — Pa' Rockseeker brought his sons there as boys, and [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]]'s brothers Barthen and Nundro recognised the description of it instantly when it started surfacing in Gundren's memory unprompted, a hundred years on.

## The Derelict

Over a century ago, a gold-panning trip to the Surbrin turned into something else entirely. Gundren, Pa' Rockseeker, and their gnomish friend [[cornubel|Cornubel]] witnessed a shooting star crash nearby and went looking for sky-iron the next day — finding instead the debris of [[the-derelict|a Modron vessel]], buried in the riverbed, with a great mechanical eye visible beneath the rushing water. The encounter cost Cornubel his life and left something of a dying Modron Nonagon fused into both Gundren and his father's minds, though neither retained any memory of it until the group excavated the wreck from beneath a thick layer of mountain ice a century later.
