Woburn Walsh is a friend of [[magistrate-li|Magistrate Li]], recruited to help build out the Barony of Thunder Bluff alongside [[gelmira-aldmann|Gelmira Aldmann]]. He specialises in close combat and security, providing muscle for the Barony's early defence. Woburn resides at the Barony as one of its NPC followers.
