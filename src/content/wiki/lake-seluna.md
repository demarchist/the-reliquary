Lake Seluna is a deep, dark body of water within the Barony of Thunder Bluff, nestled at the base of the Red Hills cliffs where the [[the-moonwood|Moonwood]] plateau meets the Thunder Bluff shelf. It is constantly replenished by the great river cascading off those cliffs in roaring waterfalls, and by glacial outflow from the mountains to the north, keeping its surface perpetually shrouded in mist. In its center lies the rocky, forested island of [[serinde-isle|Sërindë Isle]] — home to Fëanon's tower. On the mainland shore, on a point of land where the lake's south arm meets its eastern bay, stands the [[fane-of-selune|Fane of Selune]], Cirrus Al Kayan's towering white marble spire.

The lake serves as a natural boundary between these two rival landmarks, with Fëanon's tower rising from the island and Cirrus' Fane looming on the shore.

## The Horseshoe

The Red Hills stand around the lake's northern end in a great horseshoe more than three miles across, up to twenty-four hundred feet above the water. The lake runs on south past their ends, in a long arm reaching out across the shelf. Its shores are left wild, moor and wood, with one road through them to the Fane. The river leaves by its old course on the west side, down the barony's works and past [[selunia|New Daggerford]], to go over [[thunder-rapids|Hammerfall]].

## The Buried Crater

The lake lies in the scar of something that fell from the sky before the red sandstone was laid down: a crater, buried and packed with soft sediment, with nothing at the surface to show it. As the falls ate north through the cliff they broke into it, and the fill washed out. The shrine at the [[fane-of-selune|Fane]] has at its centre a fallen meteorite, a "tear" of Selûne, and the lake's name may be older than anyone remembers. The iron of the Red Hill and the Glittering Caves beneath may be children of the same impact.
