Dimitri Muskov is a younger lord of [[house-muskov|House Muskov]] in [[helioglabulus|Helios]]. He greeted the group at the front gate when they arrived to complete [[mister-luko|Mister Luko]]'s heist, acting as the insider who had arranged the entire operation.

## The Plot

Dimitri had hired Luko to steal the Muskov family Seal for his own political ends. He did not particularly care if his family discovered the theft after the fact — he simply needed the Seal out of the house. When Luko's first team vanished without a trace, Dimitri found the sole survivor — brutally wounded — in a closet on the third floor and smuggled him down to the kitchen, keeping him alive and hidden with the help of a servant girl named Anabelle.

## Role in the Heist

Dimitri welcomed [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]], [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]], and [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]] through the front door as expected guests while [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] and [[cassian|Cassian]] slipped in invisibly through the kitchen. He led the visible group to the wounded survivor and explained the situation. When the heist was complete, he declined to take the Seal himself — it was to go to Luko, who would sequester it on his behalf.

When the group decided to investigate his father [[brusso-muskov|Lord Brusso]]'s condition on the second floor, Dimitri was hesitant and opted to hang back.

## Turned

Weeks later, [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] spotted Dimitri in the flesh again — pallid, red-eyed, fanged — arguing his way into [[kishin-doji|Kishin Doji]]'s hidden domain deep beneath the city, loudly demanding entry to drink the establishment's "exquisite rice wine." He'd become a Vampire spawn. The Tengu manning the door turned him and his companion away at knifepoint: vampires hold no authority in the Queen's domain, they said, "especially spawn." Dimitri backed down and stormed off through the establishment's front doors, unaware he'd been watched. Whether he was turned by [[gaddock|Gaddock]], the [[vampire-knight-of-frostmoore|Vampire Knight]], or something closer to home in House Muskov is not yet known — but it confirms the vampiric plague spreading through Helios's nobility has already reached a second house.
