Finn is the son of Aliyah Faen Tlabbar (Patches), kidnapped by the Drow and taken to Menzoberranzan where he is trained in wizardry and sorcery. His rescue becomes one of the driving motivations behind the party's dangerous journey into the Underdark.

The quest to free Finn takes the party through the Underdark's most perilous locations — from Velkynvelve to Gracksuluth to Menzoberranzan itself — and involves negotiations with Jarlaxle and Bregan D'aerthe. Finn later resides in Dokkar among the good-aligned Drow of Svartalfheim.

## The Planeshift Amulet

With Vladalma's hit-squads active and Finn still stranded in [[dokkar|Dokkar]], [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] commissioned an Amulet of Contingent Planeshift to bring her son home to the Prime Material Plane. [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] enchanted the ornate necklace using the [[iron-tome|Iron Tome]] — layering in Planeshift, Contingency, a distortion of his own devising he calls Fëanon's Deep Slumber, and immunity to Charm and Fear.

The group delivered the finished amulet to Finn in person, finding him in the Penitent Gardens reading by a fountain among a circle of Dark Elves — including the Ancient Matron [[kallista|Kallista]], who greeted Patches with the slow, dreamlike mannerisms of the long dead. Finn was thrilled and took an hour to attune to the necklace, finally free of the fear of instant kidnapping that had kept him confined to Dokkar. The moment attunement completed, he Planeshifted straight to the Barony — without waiting for the rest of the group, who had planned to travel back together. He is now once again resident on the Prime Material Plane, training as one of [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]]'s Acolytes.
