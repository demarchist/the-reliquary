Titania is the reigning Queen of the Summer Court — the Seelie Fae, radiant and generous by nature, ruling over domains of eternal bloom and reckless beauty. Where the Winter Court trades in debts and obligations, the Summer Court's bargains are open-handed — though no less binding, and no less dangerous to a mortal who misjudges their footing.

The party has yet to encounter Titania or her court directly, though their dealings with the Feywild and its Archfey — from the [[The Bog|Bog]] to the Wild Fae beyond it — suggest her domain is not far removed from the dangers they already know.
