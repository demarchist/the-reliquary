Abraxim was an Elven Archmage of great power and greater arrogance, active during or before the Crown Wars — more than ten thousand years ago. His story survives only in carved reliefs found in an ancient shrine beneath the Chardalyn mines, later translated by [[mordenkainen|Mordenkainen]] and his staff Stick, with help from [[beryan|Beryan]]'s contacts on [[evermeet|Evermeet]].

## The Outer Gates

According to the reliefs, Abraxim traversed the Outer Gates of Reality and slipped past its Watchers into the [[far-realm|Far Realm]]. There he was consumed — or infected, the distinction unclear in the record — by [[halashnar|Halashnar]], the Pallid Prince, an Elder Evil from beyond reality. He returned to the world as a vessel for that entity, changing everyone he encountered, until the Elves — led by Sehanine Moonbow, an aspect of [[selune|Selûne]] — defeated Halashnar and drove Abraxim away past great icy mountains, theorised to be the Spine of the World.

## A Living Thread

Abraxim's fate after his banishment is unknown. The creature [[quil-sygoth|Quil'Sygoth]], fought by the group in the ruins beneath the same shrine, regenerated through Pact magic bearing the signature of either Abraxim or Halashnar — meaning the pact, and at least one of the two names behind it, may still be active somewhere in the world.
