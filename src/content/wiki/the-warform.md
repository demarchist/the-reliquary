The Warform is a great Demon of War native to [[acheron|Acheron]]: a black, oily, shapeshifting mass of hundreds of arms, legs, and bodies — an amalgam of a thousand warriors and beasts. Goblinoid warbands drop to their knees and cry its name in awe when its cube drifts near.

When it appeared during the group's crossing, its cube passed overhead and the Warform leapt on a thousand limbs, landing with a small earthquake among the trailing edge of the group's [[acheron|War Party]] and slaughtering everything in reach with weapons, claws, and gouts of acid. The group chose not to fight it — they were on Acheron for a book, not a god of war — and ran for the face-change. As the Warform moved to follow across the cube's edge, [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] Wished a Reverse Gravity onto it, and the fiend fell silently up into the endless void and vanished. Whether that is the end of it is unknown.
