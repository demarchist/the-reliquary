Bohd is the captain of Bohd's Band, a mercenary company hired by Silverymoon to shore up the defences of the Barony of Thunder Bluff. A half-orc with a mane of crimson hair, red and gold armour, and a massive battleaxe, he is competent, boisterous, and full of bravado.

He rides a blood-drinker wyvern named Kim, which he houses in a makeshift aviary atop the Temple of Helm. He can extract venom from Kim on request. When Patches tested him in the arena, he proved good — able to glance her repeatedly and learn from her trip attacks — but ultimately no match for any of the Heralds. He was deemed a solid addition to the Faerûn Defense Force.

## Command in the Barony

Bohd's value is clearest when the Heralds return home with problems no ordinary watch captain could anticipate. When a Gate from [[Acheron]] began disgorging goblinoid petitioners and pieces of [[The Acheron Spelljammer|a ruined spelljammer]], Bohd arrived quickly on Kim and called through the portal for an explanation. Once briefed, he moved the Barony guard from alarm to logistics, ordering the wreckage cleared from the Gate so the salvage could continue before the spell expired.

It was a brief incident, but a useful measure of command. Bohd did not need to understand planar soul metaphysics or why the party wanted a dead ship. He identified that the apparent invasion was controlled, established contact with the people responsible, and turned confused defenders into an effective work crew.
