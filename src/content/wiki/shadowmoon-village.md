Shadowmoon Village is home to the Orc tribe led by [[luumsh|Luumsh]] and Keek — deserters who fled the Horde two years before the party's arrival, settled on the land between the [[the-evermoors|Evermoors]] and the Barony of [[thunder-bluff|Thunder Bluff]] after proving their strength by destroying the spirit troll Shadowclaw. As the tribe grew and formalised its identity under the Barony's banner, they became known as the Shadowmoon Clan, and their settlement as Shadowmoon Village.

Shadowmoon Village stands as one of the Barony's first and staunchest allied settlements, its people working the land and contributing to Thunder Bluff's defence in exchange for citizenship and safety.
