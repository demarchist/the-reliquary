Shadowmoon Village is home to the Orc tribe led by [[luumsh|Luumsh]] and Keek — deserters who fled the Horde two years before the party's arrival, settled on the land between the [[the-evermoors|Evermoors]] and the Barony of [[thunder-bluff|Thunder Bluff]] after proving their strength by destroying the spirit troll Shadowclaw. As the tribe grew and formalised its identity under the Barony's banner, they became known as the Shadowmoon Clan, and their settlement as Shadowmoon Village.

The village stands on the low south rim of the Rauvin's gorge, directly across the [[thunder-rapids|Thunder Rapids]] from the [[keep-of-thunder-bluff|Keep of Thunder Bluff]], with the moors of the [[the-evermoors|Evermoors]] running away south behind it. It is still a primitive settlement, but a large one: a palisade the better part of half a mile across, with watchtowers on it and gates to the north, facing the Keep, and to the east. Inside are hundreds of round huts gathered in family compounds about their fire pits, dozens of longhouses, and at the heart the clan's moot hall in a ring of its own stakes, with the totems before it. The clan's strip fields lie round the palisade on the moor. Nobody crosses the gorge here: the clan's road runs east along the rim to the rope ferry over the quiet reach, above the Doorstep, where the [[thunder-bluff|Trade Way]] comes down to the far bank, and a lesser road runs on east along the south bank.

Shadowmoon Village stands as one of the Barony's first and staunchest allied settlements, its people working the land and contributing to Thunder Bluff's defence in exchange for citizenship and safety.
