The Thayan Flesh Golems are nine-foot humanoid constructs deployed by the [[Red Wizards of Thay]] beneath [[helioglabulus|Helioglabulus]]. Black leather boots, gloves, trench coats, and conical hoods conceal their bodies; only milk-white eyes show through the masks. Beneath the leather is a mosaic of expertly stitched flesh wrapped around machinery and enchanted gemstones.

They are not crude cadavers given motion. Their construction combines necromancy, artifice, and a lightning-fed core with enough sophistication for [[Fëanon Sërindë|Fëanon]] to recognise a distinct Thayan method.

## Function

The golems operate as both siege engines and collectors. Two smashed upward through the [[Rising Sun|Rising Sun's]] security while Thayan warlocks followed behind them. A third was later seen carrying two unconscious or dead citizens through the blue cistern toward [[The Catacombs of Helioglabulus|the catacombs]]. Scrapes and blood along the undercity route showed repeated journeys.

The pattern suggests that the attacks across Helios serve an industrial purpose. These constructs do not merely kill resistance; they gather bodies for whatever the Thayan necromancers are building below the castle.

## Defences and Failure

Ordinary thieves' blades penetrated the flesh but caused no meaningful harm. Enchanted weapons could dismantle them. Direct lightning was ineffective against at least one, its core dispersing the energy harmlessly.

Destruction is itself dangerous. When the first golem's inner machinery failed, it exploded in an electrical flash that disintegrated two nearby assassins. Fighting one at close quarters therefore means surviving both the construct and its death.

The leather disguise has a practical terror to it. Citizens see a silent executioner with human proportions; only those capable of cutting it open learn that Thay has fused corpse-work and precision machinery into the same weapon.
