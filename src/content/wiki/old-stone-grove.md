The Old Stone Grove is [[doc|Doc]]'s druid grove, in the Barony of [[thunder-bluff|Thunder Bluff]]. It lies south-east of [[tarks-dojo|Tark's dojo]], further out from the city than anything on the shelf, down in a glade under the eastern hills: a basin, a forested dale nestled among rolling hills. It lies deep in the woods that cover the eastern shelf. The shelf road runs on from the dojo round the lake below it, and a track leaves it south of the lake and climbs east into the dale's mouth. At its centre is a clearing with flowing water, and at the centre of that stands Oldstone, a massive standing stone older than local memory.

## The Dale

Above the dale, on a bench under the hills, is a small spring-fed pond, and it spills over a step in the rock into the dale that the springs and the fall have cut back toward it. The head of the dale is a cul-de-sac under a sheer drop of red stone, the last thin edge of the Red Hills, with a thread of water down it and rocky scree heaped at its foot, and there is a harsh line where the scree gives way to the lush trees and wildflowers of the grove. The brook winds down across the dale's floor and past the stone to a small pool where the dale opens out, and runs on to the lake below the dojo. The sides are wooded, steep at the head and easier further down, and the track comes up the dale's floor from its mouth into the clearing.

The grove is hallowed ground. Fiends, undead and their like cannot enter it, and any possessed creature brought there is freed of its possession.

## Places in the Grove

- **The Old Stone** stands in the clearing by the brook, rough and leaning a little.
- **The Oni's crack** is a thin cave in a crack at the base of the cliff, under the overhang at the head of the dale. The Oni that took a child from a farm hid there; it came crawling out, bloody, and the group killed it, and found inside the body of Old Man Elmet, whose identity it had stolen.
- **Cercei's cave** is the small cave Doc shaped in the dale's west slope for [[cercei|Cercei]], the young black dragon, so that the grove's hallowed ground would shelter her mind from [[corpsetaker|Corpsetaker]] while her body recovered.
- **Doc's hearth** is near the stone, where he has served the group yam stew.

## History

The group found the grove while tracking the Oni that had taken a child from a large farm outside the village: south along the shallow river from the Moonwood, until Tark, flying over the land on his staff, saw the dale and its stone. They recognised it as hallowed, and [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] saw in it the answer to a problem she had been carrying: Cercei could be brought there, out of Corpsetaker's reach.

At the Spring Planting festival, the whole town of [[kossenberg|Kossenberg]] processed to the Old Stone Druid grove, where Doc attended the Moon Elves in blessing the lands, before everyone went back to the village centre to dance round the Maypole.
