Quil'Sygoth is a vast, worm-like creature the group fought in ruins beneath a mine, reeking of Illithid brine pools and connected, however distantly, to the Mindflayers. Mid-battle it extended a melodic, intimate invitation into the group's minds to *join Quil'Sygoth and the family of [[halashnar|Halashnar]]* — a name that meant nothing to the group at the time.

## Endless Regeneration

Quil'Sygoth would not stay dead. Every time the group brought it down, it began to regenerate, forcing them to work quickly to keep it from reconstituting itself. Its regeneration bore the unmistakable signature of Pact magic — a pact, the group concluded, with either [[abraxim|Abraxim]] or Halashnar. If so, the pact was still active and very strong, meaning one or both of those ancient figures were still out there somewhere, still funneling power into the creature.

## The Sarcophagus

The group ultimately re-imprisoned the corpse in an ancient crystal sarcophagus found nearby, which drew the creature inside and resealed itself once the body was placed within. [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] later teleported the sarcophagus away without telling the rest of the group — a decision that caused a furious confrontation once discovered. The sarcophagus, and Quil'Sygoth within it, is now kept in a vault in [[feanons-tower|Fëanon's Tower]].
