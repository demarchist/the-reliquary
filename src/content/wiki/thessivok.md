Thessivok is a Copper Dragon Wyrmling and the unlikely follower of [[doc|Doctor Breakfast]], the gnome druid of the [[faerun-defense-force|Faerûn Defense Force]]. Small even by wyrmling standards, with scales that catch the light in shifting coins of amber and copper, Thessivok arrived in the [[selunia|Barony of Thunder Bluff]] in the chaotic aftermath of [[ancalagon|Ancalagon's]] assault on the settlement — drawn, in the way young Copper dragons often are, by noise, destruction, and the irresistible promise of something interesting.

## Arrival

Thessivok came to the Barony following [[ancalagon|Ancalagon's]] attack in the closing sessions of Chapter 14, when the settlement was still clearing its dead and counting its wounded. The precise circumstances of how a Copper Dragon Wyrmling ends up bonded to a gnome druid who presents to the world as a humanoid cat-person have not been fully explained. This is consistent with how most things involving Doc B tend to unfold.

## Personality

Thessivok has taken to the Barony with the focused enthusiasm of a creature that has recently discovered it has an audience. The wyrmling's Slow Breath — a column of golden haze that halves the speed of everything it touches — has proven particularly popular with the children of [[selunia|Selunia]], who have incorporated it into games the adults cannot entirely follow. Thessivok finds this deeply satisfying. The wyrmling engages the townsfolk with a combination of genuine warmth and low-grade chaos that the settlement has, largely, chosen to embrace.

## Izmakella

The adult Copper Dragon [[lady-isabel|Izmakella]], visiting the Barony, has taken note of the wyrmling with the particular attention of someone who cannot quite help themselves. She does not describe what she is doing as mentorship. She would find that word reductive. What she offers instead are observations — delivered at unpredictable intervals, with the authority of several centuries of accumulated mischief — on the finer points of breath timing, crowd management, and when exactly to stop before someone's parent gets involved. Thessivok receives this guidance with wide eyes and then immediately does something slightly different. Izmakella watches from a distance and says nothing, which from a Copper dragon is essentially approval.

## Doc B

Thessivok's bond with [[doc|Doctor Breakfast]] is not one either of them has explained in detail. Doc is quiet about most things that matter to him and loud about very little, and his relationship with the wyrmling follows the same pattern — present, attentive, and largely uncommented upon. That a gnome druid who once appeared to the world as a cat-person and may or may not be gnome-sisters with a Grimalkin named [[kazoo|Kazoo]] has ended up with a Copper Dragon following him around the Barony strikes no one who knows Doc as particularly surprising.
