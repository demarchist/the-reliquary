## Overview

House Faen Tlabbar is a drow noble house within Menzoberranzan's hierarchy. It is the family of party member Patches (Aliyah Faen Tlabbar), led by her mother, the cunning Matron Vladalma.

## Politics

The house maintains its position through careful alliances and economic interests. Vladalma uses agents like Lo'Dan to monitor and manipulate her daughter Patches on the surface, reflecting the ever-present reach of drow politics.

## Significance

House Faen Tlabbar's influence creates ongoing personal complications for Patches, whose surface life is constantly threatened by her mother's schemes and the expectations of drow society.

## The Hunt for Patches

[[lodan|Lo'Dan]] confirmed that Vladalma has assembled hit-squads, each led by one of Patches' sisters, scattered across the world searching for her. A simulacrum of [[jarlaxle|Jarlaxle]] later mapped their positions: Bushra and Nasra in [[baldurs-gate|Baldur's Gate]], Dahlia within an hour's magical travel of the [[thunder-bluff|Barony]], [[ishta|Ishta]] in the Bloodstone lands with the House Wizard [[jacopo|Jacopo]], and Fairuza at Vladalma's side in [[menzoberranzan|Menzoberranzan]]. A vision in the Halls of the Future at [[gravenhollow|Gravenhollow]] showed [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] her own death at her mother's hand in the tundra of [[vaasa|Vaasa]], with the Black Dragon [[ancalagon|Ancalagon]] looking on.
