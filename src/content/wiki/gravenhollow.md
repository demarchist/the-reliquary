Gravenhollow is a legendary demi-plane of earth housing an enormous library maintained by three Stone Giant archivists. The library contains knowledge spanning all planes of existence, organized into chambers of Past, Present, and Future.

The demi-plane is guarded by a Basilisk named Veldyskar and can be summoned using an ancient red-stone ring. The party visits Gravenhollow to gain crucial knowledge about the threats they face — including the locations of the Last Word fragments and the plans of their enemies.

## The Attack

[[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] gated the group in from the [[rockseeker-manufactory|Rockseeker Manufactory]] to find the library's great door standing ajar and no one to greet them. The foyer held a pool of fresh Giant blood, and the Earth Elemental Myrmidons that guard the halls attacked the group on sight, enraged by whatever had happened. Deeper in, the group found one of the three Stone Giant archivists shattered to pieces in the Halls of the Past and a second stabbed to death in the Halls of the Present, his body covered in wounds from a fight he clearly lost only after a hard struggle. [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]]'s vision in the Halls of the Past confirmed the culprit: [[zhengyi|Zhengyi]], who blasted the giants apart with an Iron-Skeleton avatar while Oni swarmed the elemental guards — and who may have learned the location of [[ythryn|Ythryn]] during the attack. The third archivist, [[ustova|Ustova]], survived undisturbed in the Halls of the Future.

The group carried the remains of both fallen giants home in a Demiplane, with an eye toward resurrection.
