Bruenor Battlehammer is King of [[gauntlgrym|Gauntlgrym]] and Emperor of the reunified Delzoun nation — the confederated dwarven realm spanning Gauntlgrym, [[sundabar|Sundabar]], Citadel Adbar, Mithral Hall, and Citadel Felbarr. Under his rule, the ancient Delzoun holds have been drawn back together into a single power, their citadels linked by magical gates through which trade, reinforcements, and dwarven craftsmanship flow freely.

Bruenor personally intervened alongside [[mordenkainen|Mordenkainen]] to destroy the Zhengyi Ziggurat grown beneath Sundabar — the one that claimed the life of [[megno-slatesunder|Megno]]'s apprentice Molly — a mark of how directly the Delzoun Emperor involves himself in threats to his people.
