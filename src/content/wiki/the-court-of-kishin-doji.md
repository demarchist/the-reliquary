The Court of Kishin Doji is a hidden lair belonging to the Oni Queen [[Kishin Doji]], reached through a heavy iron door at the far end of a ruined tomb in [[The Catacombs of Helioglabulus]] and up a long stone spiral stair. The door showed signs of frequent, recent traffic—dust and cobwebs pushed well back from its edges—long before the group understood why.

## The Transformation

Climbing the stair, the group found the air thick with a sickly-sweet haze — opium, [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]] recognised at once. As they climbed further, the human stonework began to shift and morph unnaturally: stone sprouting wood and cloth, wall sconces becoming paper lanterns, until the stairwell had transformed entirely into foreign construction. The door at the top read not as a human tomb but as the entrance to an Eastern tavern or brothel. [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] confirmed it: a powerful Mirage Arcane laid over the space, the unmistakable mark of a monster's lair.

## The Foyer

Beyond the door, a front desk and foyer are staffed by a trio of Tengu — squat demonic creatures in white robes and ornate hats, red-faced, with unnaturally long noses. The Tengu turned away two Vampire nobles — [[dimitri-muskov|Dimitri Muskov]] among them — at knifepoint when they demanded entry, on the grounds that vampires hold no authority in Kishin Doji's domain, "especially spawn."

## The Dining Hall

Past the foyer, a plush lounge of low wood tables and ornate pillows surrounds a small bamboo-matted fighting ring. Powerful Oni recline at every table but one, and dozens of baby Oni — fat, red and blue little demons — climb over each other and steal from their elders' plates while giving the larger Oni a wide berth. A curtained doorway leads to an adjoining chamber; sounds of revelry from within carry undertones of violence — tearing flesh, ravenous gluttony, cries of pain — audible only when the curtain briefly parts. A grand paper door at the top of a short flight of steps, painted with fiends devouring humans, seals off a VIP chamber. A red silk curtain blocks a side hallway from which faint, overlapping moans of pain can be heard.

A Succubus serves the hall's tables, her charms failing against the group outright. The kitchens produce a barbaric feast carried in by a massive Oni cook in a bloodstained apron and gi — pork, buns, and glazed bread that, beneath the Mirage's glamour, resolve into roasted humanoid flesh and severed, dumpling-wrapped heads.

## Encounter with Nobutada

It was here the group met [[Nobutada]], son of Kishin Doji, who emerged from the VIP chamber, introduced himself and his lineage, declared he had business in the North that did not involve the party, and led every large Oni in the hall down through the establishment and back into the catacombs toward the ruined Thayan tomb—leaving the baby Oni to swarm the abandoned tables behind him.

## A Court, Not a Tavern

The establishment imitates hospitality, but each detail expresses jurisdiction. The foyer decides who may enter. The dining room gathers an armed aristocracy. The private chamber separates Kishin Doji's family from ordinary patrons, while servants and food conceal violence beneath glamour. Even the opium haze softens the boundary between willing guest and captive.

Its position beside the Thayan workshop is therefore politically important. The regularly used door proves traffic between the spaces, but the Tengu's contempt for vampires and Nobutada's independent command show that no single faction simply owns the undercity. The court is a neighbouring power with its own rules—and a pleasant surface constructed to make predation look like civilisation.

## The Second Dining Room

Behind the blue curtains of the main hall lay a larger room: booths ringing a curving bartop, Tengu hurrying between tables, a Succubus pouring drinks, and the porcine chef chopping in the back. At each booth an Oni blew opium smoke into the faces of four humans, twenty in total, who gorged on a feast of human flesh while slowly gaining porcine features and ballooning in size. Every one of them wore the holy symbol of [[Kelemvor]]: the missing Warlock Knights of the [[Crimson Dawn Knights|Crimson Dawn]]. [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] decided they had to intervene, and the party attacked.

The room's defenders included the chef with his cleaver, a great human-faced serpent that vomited decapitated heads, a Bloodkiss that attacked from a side passage, a Succubus, and finally [[Kishin Doji]] herself, who stepped out from behind a set of paper doors as a porcelain-masked geisha and multiplied.

## The Fall of the Court

When Kishin Doji died, the *Mirage Arcane* began to warble and fail. The paper walls, silk curtains, and lacquered wood slid away to reveal the plain human stonework of the Helios Palace crypt, with the cursed humans lying unconscious on the floor and moans still rising from a side passage. A commotion could be heard from the old entrance, once the foyer of the sake-house. The Queen's private chamber dissolved into a large mausoleum off the main catacomb, three black coffins standing in the centre of the room while liquid dripped onto their closed lids.
