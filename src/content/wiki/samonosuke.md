Samonosuke is a [[The Warlocks of Ceregorach|Warlock of Ceregorach]] known directly only through the dead spy [[Letji]]. When Letji's spirit voluntarily answered questions petitioned through [[Selune]], he identified Samonosuke as his master and said that Samonosuke had sent him to spy on [[Cirrus Al Kayan|Cirrus]].

The relationship implied by *master* remains undefined. Samonosuke may have been Letji's commander, employer, teacher, or some other superior. Nothing establishes that Letji held a pact with [[Ceregorach]] himself.

## The Agent in Thunder Bluff

Letji entered [[Thunder Bluff]] as a bowyer while the party was away in [[Al Askar]]. After [[Crisserei]] granted Cirrus awareness of hostile creatures within the Barony, Cirrus connected one of those presences with the newcomer and had him arrested.

Letji fled, declared that the Warlocks of Ceregorach would all die, and activated a concealed device by biting down on it, destroying his own head. Posthumous questioning later established the espionage mission. The party presumed that the mission might have had murderous intent, but Letji's answers established only that he had been sent to watch Cirrus.

The operation shows that Samonosuke can place an agent inside Cirrus's domain while keeping himself remote from the mission. The lethal concealment measure suggests serious concern about capture, although it is unknown whether Samonosuke supplied it, ordered its use, or merely benefited from Letji's decision.

## The Samurai in Selûne's Visions

Years before Samonosuke's name surfaced, Selûne showed Cirrus four other servants of Ceregorach. One vision depicted an Elven samurai in the far east directing retainers to rip teeth from fallen warriors. A later vision placed this samurai in Kara-Tur.

Samonosuke is very likely that figure: he is explicitly a Warlock of Ceregorach, while the Kara-Turan samurai is the only member of Selûne's original four who has not otherwise been identified. The connection is nevertheless an inference. No source has directly called Samonosuke an elf, a samurai, or a resident of Kara-Tur, so those traits remain out of his settled biography.

If the identification is correct, the earlier vision adds an important dimension to his methods. Samonosuke does not merely act through solitary spies; he commands retainers and harvests teeth from battlefields, converting organised violence into tribute for the Tooth Fairy.

## The Other Survivor

[[Zulkhir Atlan Issan]] later called himself one of only two servants of the Tooth Fairy who remained. Selûne's original quarry consisted of [[Strigu]], [[Bilrigger]], a Red Wizard, and the Kara-Turan samurai. Strigu and Bilrigger are dead, while Atlan is the Red Wizard. On that accounting, Samonosuke is very likely the other survivor.

Atlan did not name him, however. The count might exclude lesser servants or refer to a category broader than Selûne's original four. It also does not establish that Atlan and Samonosuke cooperate, recognise one another as equals, or share a command structure. Letji's cry that all the warlocks would die may even hint at hostility within the surviving network, though its meaning remains unresolved.

Where Atlan acts through Thayan office, battlefield magic, and overt provocation, Samonosuke remains distant and works through intermediaries. That contrast may represent different resources, different temperaments, or two entirely separate campaigns conducted beneath the same patron.
