The Erlking rules the Wild Fae — the Spiorad Fae — the third and least predictable of the Fae Courts, owing allegiance to neither the Summer Court of [[titania|Titania]] nor the Winter Court of [[mab|Mab]]. Where Seelie and Unseelie alike operate by codes of bargain and obligation, however self-serving, the Wild Fae answer only to their own untamed natures — closer to the raw, feral magic of the Feywild than to any court's civility.

The party has not met the Erlking directly, but they have crossed paths with Wild Fae before — [[mediocris-dente|Mediocris Dente]], also known as [[ceregorach|Ceregorach]], numbered among them, and the Fae Bog's stranger inhabitants hint at a domain closer to the Erlking's than to either Summer or Winter.
