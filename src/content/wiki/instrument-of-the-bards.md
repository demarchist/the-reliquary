This Instrument of the Bards is the enchanted Lyre recovered by [[Jack Mayer Johnson Mathews|JmJm]] beneath the [[Mines of Und]]. It became the first known piece in his quest to assemble [[The Instruments of the Bards|the legendary set of seven]].

Arcane tradition uses *Instrument of the Bards* for a class of ancient elven instruments named for elven houses. Such instruments enhance bardic spellcasting and can channel powerful enchantments through performance. [[Lady Isabel|Izmakella]], however, identified JmJm's Lyre as one of a singular set made by gods and goddesses associated with bardic magic. Whether the seven are divine originals within the broader elven tradition or a separate class entirely has not been resolved.

## Recovery

The party found the Lyre in a small ornate chest beneath the balcony of the [[Elder Orb]]'s lair, beside a [[Scroll of Demiplane]] and a [[Potion of Supreme Healing]]. The cache was modest for an aberration of such age, suggesting the bulk of its hoard remained in the Netherese ruin from which [[Zhengyi]] had displaced it.

JmJm tucked the Instrument under one arm and walked away without breaking eye contact with anyone. No one contested the claim.

## Recognition

Izmakella recognised the Lyre in JmJm's theater chambers and first offered to buy it cheaply under the pretext of assembling an orchestra. When pressed, she revealed the legend of the seven. The Lyre was therefore not a known quest reward or a deliberate divine gift when JmJm claimed it. The quest grew backward from an object he had already chosen on instinct.

That instinct may be simple bardic possessiveness. It may also be the same sensitivity to art, gold, and planar objects that later made him recognise [[The Acheron Spelljammer|the stripped Acheron spelljammer]] as culturally familiar when no one else saw anything worth saving.
