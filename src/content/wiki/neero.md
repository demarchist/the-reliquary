Neero first appeared to the group as a lone Wood Elf bard, walking in out of a blizzard near [[silverymoon|Silverymoon]] with a shamisen, a small Kara-Turan tanto, and a fine bottle of wine. He won the household over with music and tales of the far east through a long evening — and then, alone with the sleeping [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]], "accidentally" nicked him with the poisoned tanto and put him to sleep.

## The Attack

The party woke to a ransacked, freezing house and Neero's true form: a nine-foot, blue-skinned Oni with small white horns and milky eyes, conferring with a band of half-ogres. In the fight that followed he cast a Cone of Cold, fought Patches to a standstill in a furious exchange of blows, and — with his forces failing — turned invisible and fled upstairs, snatching the household's baby on his way out a shattered window.

The chase carried into the blizzard. Neero hurled the baby at [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] to slow him, then turned his extending katana on [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]], carving up the warlock before the party surrounded him. Cornered, he shifted to Gaseous Form to escape — and [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]], feeling the subtle magic move across the snow, dropped a Fireball on the drifting mist. The blast evaporated a sphere of snow into steam and left Neero's broken, burning body at its edge. An Azer finished him with a hammer to the skull.

His flint tanto passed to the group; [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] later gave the blade to [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]] as a gesture of trust when the group prepared to descend into the Underdark without him.

## Kishin Doji's Son

Years later, in his mother [[kishin-doji|Kishin Doji]]'s hidden domain beneath [[helioglabulus|Helios]], Neero's younger brother [[nobutada|Nobutada]] named him and [[li-xian|Li Xian]] both to the party as family — the first confirmation that Neero's true allegiance ran through the Oni Queen herself, not merely to [[zhengyi|Zhengyi]]'s wider network of Oni servants.
