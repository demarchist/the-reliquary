Mulmaster is a grim, storm-lashed city on the southern shore of the Moonsea. It entered the group's story only as a mistake: when [[megno-slatesunder|Megno]] Teleported [[lord-aimu|Lord Aimu]] and his paladins toward the Bloodstone Lands ahead of the assault on [[helioglabulus|Helios]], the spell landed the group in Mulmaster instead — a good hundred miles off target — leaving Lord Aimu's detachment stranded and out of the fight while Megno worked to scry the correct destination and try again.
