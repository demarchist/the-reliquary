Gelmira Aldmann is a friend of [[magistrate-li|Magistrate Li]], recruited to help build out the Barony of Thunder Bluff alongside [[woburn-walsh|Woburn Walsh]]. She specialises in archery and scouting, rounding out the Barony's early defensive capabilities. Gelmira resides at the Barony as one of its NPC followers.
