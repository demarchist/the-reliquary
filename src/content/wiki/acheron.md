Acheron is the plane of endless war: an infinite grey void studded with iron cubes that range from the size of dice to the size of planetoids, drifting and slowly tumbling past one another. Goblinoid souls come here after death, and the plane's machinery recycles them — a warrior slain in battle disintegrates and is returned to the war elsewhere. The Blood War of the Tanar'ri spills across it, and Modrons march its faces in ordered columns. [[dean-rochester|Dean Rochester]] of [[candlekeep|Candlekeep]] tracked a book missing for a century to a wreck on one of its cubes, and traded the group a Planeshift fork for its return.

## The Cubes

The cubes are flat-faced and enormous, and each face reckons its own gravity, so crossing from one face to the next means a disorienting moment of falling "up" before the new face catches you. Cubes pass close enough to skim one another, and warbands leap the gap between them — many make the crossing, many miss and tumble screaming into the void. Every surface the group saw was covered in peoples warring for the sake of warring, absorbing and shattering one another in a cycle with no apparent end.

## The War Party

To cross dozens of miles of contested metal toward the wreck, the group turned a scrap-hall of Goblinoids into a **War Party** — killing the local boss on arrival, rallying the hall around raw strength, and then rolling across the cube as a growing horde that crushed everything in its path and absorbed the warbands it met. [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]] rode a sled dragged by an Ogre; [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]] repaired a damaged Modron along the way for directions. The party left the horde behind on the plane when their Gate home closed, the Goblinoid souls returning to the war that owns them.

## The Warform

A cube bearing the [[the-warform|Warform]] — a shapeshifting demon-mass of a thousand warriors — drifted down onto the group's route and slaughtered the trailing edge of their horde before [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] dropped it into the void with a Wished Reverse Gravity.
