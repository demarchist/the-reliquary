The Warform is a colossal Demon of War encountered on [[Acheron]]. Its surface is black and oily, perpetually changing as hundreds of warriors and beasts pass through one another: arms carrying weapons, clawed limbs, torsos, legs, and bodies without a stable anatomy to contain them. It attacks with the accumulated arsenal of those shapes and with great gouts of acid.

The creature is not merely large. It looks aggregated, as though Acheron's endless recycling of war-souls can sometimes assemble combatants into a single predatory form.

## Worship Without Protection

The Warform approached on the face of a descending cube while two goblinoid war parties fought below. Warriors on both sides dropped to their knees and cried out its name in awe. The Warform answered their reverence by leaping into the combined host and slaughtering everyone within reach.

That relationship is the clearest insight into the creature. It is known and venerated, but it is not a protector. It recognises no distinction between worshipper, enemy, or bystander. If it embodies anything, it is war stripped of every stated purpose: violence that consumes the people who praise it as readily as the people they oppose.

The party made the cold decision to leave the first victims behind. The goblinoids were already dead and bound into Acheron's wars; the party had come to recover [[Candlekeep]]'s book, not to challenge every apex horror of the plane. The surviving war party followed them toward a cube-face transition. The Warform pursued.

## Falling Up

[[Fëanon Sërindë|Fëanon]] defeated the pursuit by understanding the battlefield rather than the creature. Gravity on Acheron is local to each face of its cubes. He waited until the Warform began to cross an edge and used Wish to impose Reverse Gravity at the transition.

The Warform fell upward into the grey void. It made no sound as it receded and eventually vanished from sight.

This was removal, not a confirmed killing. Nothing was recovered and no death was observed. The Warform may remain somewhere in Acheron, may eventually collide with another cube, or may have met a danger in the void that the party never saw. Its disappearance ended the immediate pursuit, not its dossier.
