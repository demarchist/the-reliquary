Allips are dark, incorporeal undead encountered by the party in ancient ruins, a distant swamp, and a wreck on [[Acheron]]. Those encounters establish a recognisable form—ragged or cloaked shadows, movement through solid matter, and mentally destructive whispers—but the campaign has never shown an Allip being created. Their origin and any necessary connection to forbidden knowledge remain unconfirmed.

## Promethi and Tantalisia

The first named Allips haunted the [[Sanctum of Krasus]].

Promethi emerged from its ruined library as twisting shadow wrapped in tattered robes. It retained enough speech to greet the intruders, produced mirror images, passed through walls and a marble statue, and attacked in repeated sorties from solid cover. Its babbling whispers clawed at nearby minds. The party eventually stopped pursuing it, held a defensible position, and destroyed it when it emerged again. A golden medallion marked with one of the [[Netherese]] schools of magic fell from its dissipating form.

Tantalisia appeared when [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] lifted the incomplete [[Mythallar]]. Her babbling reached into Gundren and [[Tarkathee Daru|Tark]], and her touch briefly compelled both to attack [[Fëanon Sërindë|Fëanon]]. She used the same wall-phasing tactics as Promethi and left another school-marked medallion when destroyed.

Their names, location, and medallions suggest that Promethi and Tantalisia may once have been practitioners or occupants of the Sanctum. That is an inference: no surviving account identifies either one in life or explains the catastrophe that made them Allips.

## The Standing Stones

On [[Al Askar]], the party found a ring of standing stones in [[Thueba Swamp]] swarming with unnamed Allips. They chose to detour rather than investigate or fight.

This encounter confirms that Allips are neither unique to the Sanctum nor confined to Faerûn, and that they can gather in substantial numbers. It does not establish whether the stones attracted them, imprisoned them, marked the site of their deaths, or were merely terrain they occupied.

## The Acheron Wreck

A century-overdue book led the party to [[The Acheron Spelljammer|a ruined spelljammer]]. Using the High Elf borrower's collateral Ioun stone, Fëanon scried a cloaked Allip moving through the wreck and suspected it was what remained of the borrower. The party later destroyed the spirit and recovered [[Candlekeep]]'s missing book from the captain's quarters.

The proximity of spirit, collateral, and book makes the borrower identification plausible, but it was never independently confirmed. Nothing establishes that the book caused the borrower's death or transformation; the Allip could instead have been another member of the crew or a later occupant of the wreck.

## A Pattern, Not a Rule

Every known encounter places Allips amid old magic, death, and abandoned structures. Promethi and Tantalisia guarded the remains of a Netherese disaster; a swarm drifted through ancient standing stones; the Acheron spirit haunted a crashed vessel containing lost knowledge. That repetition makes ruined scholarship and unresolved magical catastrophe a useful warning sign.

It is not yet a proven law of their nature. The standing stones revealed no scholarly connection, the High Elf's identity remains uncertain, and the party has never witnessed an Allip's creation.

The Sanctum did provide one reliable tactical lesson: chasing an incorporeal enemy through stone favours the enemy. Holding open ground and preparing concentrated attacks for its return worked against both named Allips.
