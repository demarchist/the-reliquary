The Dojo is [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]]'s training hall in the Barony of [[thunder-bluff|Thunder Bluff]]. It stands set back in the treeline, in the woods on the scarp east of the farms that run up from [[selunia|New Daggerford]], and a footpath climbs to it off the farm road along the shelf. Further along the road, down in a glade, is Doc's druid grove, [[old-stone-grove|the Old Stone grove]].

## The Grounds

It is a long hall with a great hip-and-gable roof of dark tile, white plaster and dark timber, and paper screens across its front, raised on posts over a gravel terrace with the forest behind it. Below the hall, a court of raked gravel lies inside tile-capped walls, with training posts down one side and stone lanterns at the stairs. The slope is steep there, so both terraces stand on walls of dry-laid stone, and the court is entered by a roofed gate at the head of a stone stair. Off the hall's west end, under its own roof, is the small room where the painter Jin Sakama slept.

## History

Jin Sakama, the illusionist painter in Tark's service, was living at the Dojo when [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] came knocking one evening, put him to sleep, and copied two spells out of his spellbook; [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] had already been by that day looking for Tark, and laughed at Jin for wanting to paint him.

Later, word reached Tark that someone was teaching *Tark Kwon Do* at the Dojo. That was [[rex-kwondo|Rex Kwondo]], whose lessons [[lord-aimu|Lord Aimu]]'s paladins, at the Dojo with their lord while he sought refuge in the barony, considered buffoonery; they expelled him for trading on Tark's name. Tark defended him, and the paladins set out to find Rex and put things right.
