The Instruments of the Bards are a legendary set of seven enchanted musical instruments. No one is known to have assembled the complete set, though many have tried. [[Jack Mayer Johnson Mathews|JmJm]] intends to be the first.

Their origin is not settled. General arcane lore describes Instruments of the Bards as works of ancient elven loremasters, each associated with a particular elven house and school of performance magic. [[Lady Isabel|Izmakella]] offered a more exalted account: seven unique instruments made by gods and goddesses of bardic magic—Oghma, Sune, [[Sharess]], and others whose identities are now uncertain. The two traditions may describe different classes of instrument, a divine set later imitated by elven makers, or the same objects through incompatible histories.

What Izmakella recognised in JmJm's possession was enough to begin the quest.

## The Lyre

The first Instrument came from the hoard of the [[Elder Orb]] beneath the [[Mines of Und]]. It lay in a small ornate chest beside a [[Scroll of Demiplane]] and a [[Potion of Supreme Healing]]. JmJm tucked it under one arm and walked away without breaking eye contact; no one contested the claim.

Only later, in JmJm's chambers at the theater, did Izmakella identify the Lyre as one of the seven. She first tried to buy it for a fraction of its worth under the pretext of assembling an orchestra. When JmJm pressed her, she explained the larger legend instead.

This corrects the easy story that the Lyre was a gift from Sharess. Sharess gave JmJm the [[Tavern of Doors]] and may be associated with one of the seven makers, but the Lyre itself was recovered from the Elder Orb's cache. How the aberration acquired it remains unknown.

## The Mandolin

At JmJm's Thespian Banquet, one of his actors—a wizard playing the role of [[Fëanon Sërindë|Fëanon]]—scried for another Instrument. The spell found a Mandolin displayed in an artifact hall at [[Candlekeep]]. It was the first actionable lead in the new quest.

The Mandolin could not simply be taken. Its donor had required that it pass only to a worthy bardic hero, and [[Dean Rochester]] proposed a test: recover a century-overdue book from [[Acheron]]. JmJm rejected the premise that Rochester could judge him, stormed out, and later sent the Dean twenty-five thousand gold by Wish.

The gold improved the correspondence, not the conditions. The party still crossed Acheron, raised a goblinoid war party, escaped the [[Warform]], destroyed the Allip haunting [[The Acheron Spelljammer|the wreck containing the book]], and returned the volume. Candlekeep then sent the Mandolin with a note addressed to *The Bard*.

JmJm now possesses two of the seven.

## The Quest

JmJm dispatched Thespians across the world to chase further leads, including a company sent into the Underdark to [[Aliyah Faen Tlabbar|Patches]]' considerable alarm. The search combines his grandest virtues and most dangerous instincts. It is an artistic pilgrimage, a claim to historical uniqueness, and a competition against everyone who failed before him. It also asks other people to walk into places like the Underdark and Acheron because he has decided the complete set should belong to one bard.

The Candlekeep trial proved that JmJm can inspire an army and finish a heroic task even while insisting he is above being tested. Whether collecting all seven will make him the bard he believes himself to be—or simply give that belief seven louder voices—is still open.
