The Instruments of the Bards are a legendary set of seven instruments, each said to have been crafted by a different god or goddess associated with bardic magic — Oghma, Sune, [[sharess|Sharess]], and others of similar domain, though no one is entirely certain of the full roster. No one has ever assembled the complete set, though many are said to have tried.

## The Lyre

[[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]] already possessed one of the seven — his Lyre, a gift from [[sharess|Sharess]] — without knowing its significance. [[lady-isabel|Izmakella]], visiting his chambers at the [[the-establishment|Establishment]] ahead of his Thespian Banquet, recognised it immediately and offered to buy it for a pittance under the pretence of assembling an orchestra. Pressed by JmJm's charm, she relented and told him what she knew of the legend.

## The Quest

JmJm resolved on the spot to be the one who finally assembles the full set. At the banquet, a Thespian standing in for [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] scried for the next instrument on his behalf and found a Mandolin, displayed in a glass case in a hall of artifacts identified as Candlekeep — one of the most famous universities in the world, and the group's first lead. JmJm dispatched groups of his Thespians across the region to chase down further leads, including one bound for the Underdark, to [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]]' considerable alarm.
