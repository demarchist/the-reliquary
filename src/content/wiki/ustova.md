Ustova is one of the three Stone Giant archivists who maintain [[gravenhollow|Gravenhollow]], the legendary demi-plane library. He serves as the Archivist of the Future, residing in a great natural cavern beneath a magnificent amethyst chandelier that sheds crystal shards like falling stars. Easily twenty feet tall and clad in elegant purple robes with gold bangles, Ustova greeted [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] by name when the monk arrived to peer into possible futures, warning him that the experience would be hard on both body and mind.

## After the Attack

When the party returned to Gravenhollow to find the library's entrance ajar, its foyer soaked in blood, and its Earth Elemental guardians turned hostile, they found one of the two other Stone Giant archivists shattered and the other stabbed dead — but Ustova remained alive and aloof in the Halls of the Future. He welcomed [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]], remembering her from her prior visit, and either did not know or would not reveal what had happened to his kin. He bid her join him on the dais, where she saw a vision of her own death at [[vladalma|Vladalma]]'s hand in the tundra of [[vaasa|Vaasa]].
