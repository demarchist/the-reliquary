## Flight Training

- [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] spends the day deep in the forests of the Barony, learning to master the powers of his newly forged [[windrunner-armor|Windrunner Armor]]
- He tumbles and flies through the air, crashing through branches, skipping off the water, and landing in front of a group of Barony children before jetting off again
- After twenty-four hours of practicing falling into the sky, the Monk finally gets the hang of the armor's gravity manipulation enough to emulate true flight, and returns to the heart of the Barony

## Buumi's Stakeout

- Past midnight, the town center is sleepy, with only a few folks milling about
- Tark notices [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]] standing behind a cart, attempting to look inconspicuous while watching the front door of [[jmjm-theater|JmJm's Theater]], and approaches silently
- Buumi jumps a little but quickly and quietly admits he's hoping to surprise [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] as she comes out
- Tark thinks it's a great idea and waits with his friend, showing off some of his new skills by standing perpendicular on the wall of the alley
- Buumi is impressed but isn't sure whether it's a monk skill or some other magic
- They wait some time, but it's clear either they missed her, or she isn't coming out
- Buumi sighs and excuses himself, heading off to bed for the evening

## The Ghostwise Halfling

- Tark shrugs and heads into the Theater himself, finding [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]] and his guests preparing for an evening of…rest together
- He gets a quick update on the happenings and is told to meet the group for breakfast at Patches' establishment
- [[lady-isabel|Izmakella]] remarks that it's been a long while since she's seen a Ghostwise Halfling, and Tark asks her to explain
- He asks what the folk of Helios have been calling him — a *Kryschek*
- The dragon chuckles and explains that the Kryschek are local folktale: small faerie folk who come down from the mountains, normally bringing good luck
- It's a folksy twist on reality — the Ghostwise Halflings, who every decade or so come down from the mysterious highlands to trade before retreating into obscurity
- It's been twenty years since she's seen one other than Tark. Most of them have stark red hair
- Elated by the tale of halflings he suspects may be kin, Tark excuses himself and returns to the forest to sleep

## Patches and the Poison Kiss

- Patches, meanwhile, watched the whole exchange between Tark and Buumi outside the Theater, and stalked Buumi back to his room within the [[Cathedral of Helm]]
- She waits for him to fall asleep, breaks in, and climbs into bed beside the big man, stroking his hair to try to wake him nicely
- He doesn't stir
- She slaps him awake, startling the poor man
- She thanks him — and Tark — for making her want to be a better person, then kisses him with drow-poison-laced lips. The Paladin falls unconscious
- Patches sighs — eventually he'll build up an immunity to the poison, but not today
- She leaves and returns to her own sanctum to rest

## Gundren and the River

- [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]] heads to the [[rockseeker-manufactory|manufactory]] for a much-needed rest, his mind wandering as he travels
- He can't help but remember a river with a gleaming metal eye beneath the waters, like some half-remembered dream
- He looks over the modifications [[gwen|Gwen]] is undergoing and notes a few Modrons working in the Keep courtyard, clearly constructing some kind of magical gate
- He suspects it will be a planar portal to Mechanus, and reminds himself to get [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] to whip up some security measures
- He makes some adjustments to Gwen, checking her internal magazine for the new weapons, then heads to bed — still thinking about the rushing river and the gleaming metal eye beneath the surface…

## The Wake

- In the Wizard Tower, [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] excuses himself as [[mordenkainen|Mordenkainen]] Gates home, then retires to his private study to work with the [[iron-tome|Iron Tome]] on a new spell while waiting on another scrying report from his acolytes
- [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] makes it to the Fane to turn in for the night and finds a tiny white horse waiting for him with a letter in its mouth
- He accepts the letter; the small horse thanks him in Common and gallops off
- The letter is from Lady Huron of [[helioglabulus|Helioglabulus]] — the soirée [[house-huron|House Huron]] had planned to host was postponed by the untimely death of [[brusso-muskov|Lord Muskov]]
- The Lord has lain in the Cathedral for some days now, and it's time for his Wake and Burial. The Queen has personally invited Cirrus and his retainers to attend
- Knowing about the Thayans, the Amnian spelljammer delivering Saurian weaponry, and the vampiric nature of the foes in the city, Cirrus strongly suspects a trap
- He prays and meditates until morning

## The Queen's Dream

- Tark finds himself laying with his head in the lap of a beautiful human woman in a silk gown
- The dark-haired lady with cream-colored skin looks down with a sloe-eyed gaze, smiling with shining white teeth and delicately pointed canines, and sings him a nursery rhyme:

> *"Hush, hush, little one, your queen has come to call, Mommy's waiting by the door — she's waited through it all. One for sorrow, two for sleep, three to make it right, Four to keep you close to her, forever, through the night. Who's my darling? Who's my dear? Come lay your head, there's nothing to fear — Mommy's kind, she longs to see Another son and cherish thee."*

- Tark sits up, backs away, and realizes he's dreaming
- He questions the lady and concludes this must be [[queen-concetinna-frostmantle|Queen Concetinna]] of Helios
- She's delightfully charming and playful, answering his questions — mostly — and laughs off his accusations of being a vampire; she says she is not, and oddly, he believes her
- She says she felt him when he died, and that she waits for him — as does [[gaddock|Gaddock]]
- She caresses his face, and he finds himself falling back into her lap as she leans in, her canines elongating into fangs…
- Tark wakes covered in sweat to see the horizon turning pink with the pre-dawn glow, his resolve steeled to go back and hunt down his old master

## Breakfast and the Plan

- The group convenes for breakfast at [[the-establishment|The Establishment]] just as Piotr brings out the first batch of baked confections from the bakery
- Claudine delivers Patches her batches of poisons, and [[gwen|Gwen]]'s retrofit is finished and delivered by Gundren's brothers
- The old Dwarf questions his brothers about the river and metal eye he can't get out of his head, and they share a quizzical look
- They easily recognize the description as the River Surbrin by Mithral Hall, where their Pa' used to take them panning for gold — a favored Dwarven pastime
- Gundren should remember it easily, but the last time he and Pa' Rockseeker went there was with their gnome friend [[cornubel|Cornubel]] — who never returned
- Afterward, Gundren and Pa' Rockseeker were different: more remote, more calculating, and cold…
- The group has breakfast and looks over their maps and current goals, outlining a plan for the day
- They agree they need to finish their upgrades before going to Helios to confront the Queen and the Thayans
- Tark relays his dream, and Cirrus his message — they all agree the invitation is a trap
- They plan to Planeshift to Dokkar and deliver [[finn|Finn]] his amulet of protection, so he can rejoin the Prime Material Plane as one of Fëanon's Acolytes, then use the amulet to Planeshift back and investigate Gundren's river by Mithral Hall
- They join hands and Planeshift to the Svartalfheim city of [[dokkar|Dokkar]]

## Dokkar and the Amulet

- They find themselves in the heart of the Penitent Gardens
- As known souls, they're greeted casually by the nearby guards and wardens, but otherwise ignored
- The gardens hold large groups of lounging Drow, with the occasional other race scattered about
- Finn sits reading a book by a small fountain, surrounded by Dark Elves — only occasionally does one say something, with the others simply hanging out with the young elf
- Finn looks up, and his face lights up as he greets the group
- A few nearby Drow turn their gazes onto the group, but none offer a word of greeting save the Ancient Matron [[kallista|Kallista]], who welcomes Patches — though even her responses and mannerisms are slow and dreamlike, a reminder that this is an afterlife for the long dead
- Finn is thrilled when presented with the amulet, granting enough protection that he can finally return to the Prime Material Plane without fear of instant kidnapping. He takes the hour to attune while the group lounges in the gardens
- Tempted to explore, they settle instead for calling over a nearby Drow worshipper of [[eilistraee|Elistraee]] so Fëanon can inquire about Moonfire
- He learns the Moonfire ritual happens at a "regular" cadence — regular for an extra-planar, timeless afterlife — and is quite the occasion: a festival of worship to Selûne and Elistraee
- Fëanon asks about receiving the Moonfire blessing himself, and the guard notes a centuries-long waitlist — though the list can be superseded by the Council
- Fëanon sighs. Nothing is ever simple. He mulls over what the Ruling Houses of Dokkar may want in exchange for jumping the Moonfire queue
- The group remembers rumblings of visiting dragons from their last visit, and learns two were supposed to lair here, but only one has arrived — Fëanon wonders if this is connected to [[azoo-the-archer|Azoo]] the Copper Dragon they rescued
- Finn finishes attuning to the necklace and promptly Planeshifts to the Barony — leaving the group behind
- Fëanon looks a little dumbfounded. Did no one mention the plan to Finn? Oh well
- The Wizard opens a Gate to the mountainside wilderness near Mithral Hall, and the group steps through, intent on looking into the strange river Gundren remembers before heading to [[candlekeep|Candlekeep]] for JmJm

## The River Surbrin

- They find themselves on the summer slopes of the Spine of the World Mountains, surrounded by alpine wildflower meadows and small lakes
- The roar of a river can be heard, and after a quick orientation, Gundren heads north and begins to ascend
- Between the mounts and magical means, the trek is relatively easy, and before long Gundren comes to the river his brothers mentioned — the [[river-surbrin|River Surbrin]] — and recognizes it
- He begins to remember: over a century ago, he and his father came on a gold-panning trip with their gnomish friend Cornubel. While camping, they saw a shooting star that night and felt it crash-land nearby
- Excited by the prospect of sky-iron, they went looking for it, and the following day began to find debris — a golden alloy they didn't recognize, and fragments of complex machinery, including a great mechanical eye at the bottom of the rushing waters
- The group follows Gundren as he retraces his ancient footsteps, ascending past the snow line
- They come to a slight divot in the mountainside, thick with ice but bare of any wreckage. Gundren swears the thing is beneath them, and they burrow down with magic until they reach the golden hull of some great vessel
- They identify it as of Modron origin — clearly not built by humanoid hands
- It takes some time to find an access hatch and squeeze inside. It's definitely a Spelljammer, built from Fabrial circuitry and artifice — beyond the level of tech they can make heads or tails of the function
- They descend into the heart of the ruined ship and come to a core: a nine-sided tetrahedron that appears to defy physics, two-dimensional and yet with a kaleidoscopic polygonal interior — cracked and clouded
- Nearby lie the desiccated remains of a long-dead Gnome
- Gundren remembers — they crawled into the core and came across this thing: a Nonagon of the Modrons
- It was dying, calling out to their minds. They were charmed into touching it
- The extra-planar entity fragmented and took refuge in their minds, killing the Gnome but managing to coexist within Gundren and his father. It changed them — their personalities, their affinity for technology and science
- They had no memory of the encounter afterward, and returned home without their friend, with no knowledge he had even been with them
- The unsolved disappearance of [[cornubel|Cornubel]] haunted Pa' Rockseeker and drove the family into exile — the rest is history
- The group removes Cornubel's long-dead body and puts it into storage, to give him a proper burial and return him to his family
- They leave the vessel, mark the location, and send word to Gundren's brothers to send a team to excavate and recover him

## Candlekeep

- With the mystery of the hitchhiking Modron somewhat revealed, the group teleports to [[candlekeep|Candlekeep]]
- They arrive in the display-case hall, right in front of the instrument of the Bards, as an alarm begins to blare…
