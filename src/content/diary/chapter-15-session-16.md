## The Lyre

- Returned from [[gravenhollow|Gravenhollow]], the group finds themselves back at [[the-establishment|The Establishment]] to chat and plan
- Meanwhile, [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]] has been furiously preparing for his Thespian Banquet, where he means to reveal his grand plan to his friends
- [[lady-isabel|Izmakella]] stops by, loving the chaos and hilarity of the Barony
- She barges into the Theater despite not being allowed entry — the Thespians try to stop her, but she simply shifts into her true form and pushes through them
- Her copper dragon head shoves through the door to JmJm's private chambers; the bard squeals in fake coyness
- She shifts back to human form and joins him, playfully teasing — though her eyes keep drifting to his Lyre
- She offers to buy the instrument for a pittance, claiming she's putting together an orchestra
- JmJm senses she's holding back and presses her — she relents easily under his charm
- She recognizes the Lyre as one of the seven [[instruments-of-the-bards|Instruments of the Bards]], created by the goddesses of song, love, dance, and festivals
- No one has ever assembled the full set, though many have wished to — and she saw her chance
- JmJm's eyes light up. He will be the one to assemble the [[instruments-of-the-bards|Instruments of the Bards]]
- A knock at his door breaks the daydream — the group has returned to the Barony

## Lo'Dan and the Vision

- Back at The Establishment, [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] excuses herself to go find [[lodan|Lo'Dan]] and dig for more on what her mother is planning
- The vision with the dragon has her seriously concerned
- She descends into the gambling den and spots the Drow agent watching the room from the shadows
- She approaches and learns that her family has hit-squads, led by her sisters, scattered across the world looking for her
- She presses for more, but that's the extent of what Lo'Dan currently knows
- She returns upstairs just as the group is discussing "getting rid" of Lo'Dan — [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]] is aghast(ish) at the notion
- Patches explains her fears about her mother allying with [[zhengyi|Zhengyi]] and [[ancalagon|Ancalagon]]
- She asks [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] to scry on her sisters

## Scrying Ancalagon

- Fëanon tries several times without luck — the Priestesses of [[lolth|Lolth]] are too well protected
- He tries a different target and scries on [[ancalagon|Ancalagon]] instead. It works
- The image resolves: the armoured black dragon flying above a mountain range with a Thayan Red Wizard on his back
- Ahead of him, two massive armoured red dragons — one of them the largest dragon any of them have ever seen, though there's something unnervingly familiar about it
- They hear Ancalagon grumble to his rider about searching for the "creatures" like hounds, eager for battle
- The great Red Wyrm turns its head to admonish Ancalagon — and notices the invisible scrying sensor, warning its allies
- The riding Thayan casts a spell and the scrying ends

## The Monastery and the Edificent Library

- The group discusses the monastery [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] is looking for, and [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]] sends messages out to his contacts
- From what they already know, it's a monastery to [[selune|Selûne]] somewhere in the far east of [[amn|Amn]], in the Sunset Mountains
- This stirs a memory for Fëanon — there was once a great library in those mountains, the Edificent Library, long since destroyed. He mulls a visit to the site
- As the group worries over the array of enemies before them, the sun sets and they remember their invitations to JmJm's banquet
- They excuse themselves and scatter to get ready for the dinner party

## Getting Ready

- [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]] heads to the [[rockseeker-manufactory|manufactory]], puts on his finest attire, then his intricate helmet, breastplate, and cloak over the top, with his [[thundercannon|Thundercannon]] in an ornate back holster — and takes his limo to the Theater
- [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]] dons his finest nobleman's clothes complete with breastplate and side-cape, picks up some dawn-lilies for Patches, and waits outside The Establishment intending to walk
- [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] heads to his Fane and bathes in silver — a product of the manufactory that he sells to his followers — then puts on his finest pearl robes and is escorted to the Theater by his most silver-infused followers
- [[cassian|Cassian]] has no interest in the affair and heads into the woods to the Dojo to find [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] and [[lord-aimu|Lord Aimu]]
- [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] rushes to her closet with no idea what to wear, lets fate decide, and pulls out a noblewoman's riding suit she once wore escorting [[lady-faye|Lady Faye]] in [[baldurs-gate|Baldur's Gate]] — a crimson waistcoat and bodice with a slanted outer-skirt and ruffled petticoat, high at the front and long at the back, with black leggings and shining riding boots
- She braids her hair and meets Buumi out front of The Establishment, tentatively taking the flowers, a little confused by the offering, then tucking them into her braid — and the two head to the Theater together

## Fëanon's Report

- [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] returns to [[feanons-tower|his tower]] to freshen up and dress in his finest robes
- While there, he gets the initial scrying report from his acolytes on the situation in [[helioglabulus|Helioglabulus]]
- The Holotank hums to life: the great golden orb of the Amnian Spelljammer arrives over the city, hovering by the tower of [[house-huron|House Huron]]
- Amnian cowled wizards walk the golden surface as if it were a tiny planetoid with its own gravity, unloading crates by telekinesis down to waiting crimson-robed Thayans
- The largest Saurian cannon the group has ever seen is transferred from the Spelljammer to the Thayans — some thirty feet long. The Basilica: a cannon meant to be fired through a Gate or Ringed Veil, the ultimate artillery weapon
- Fëanon curses — Vecna must have overheard them discussing the idea back when he held the dark book on Al Askar
- The report turns to the wider city: the strange murders and disappearances continue to mount, with only blurry glimpses of the culprit — a tall, thin humanoid in a heavy cloak or trenchcoat
- Fëanon tells the acolytes to keep watching, then has Maya "drive" his arcane carriage to the Theater while he thinks

## Arrivals at the Theater

- Cirrus and Fëanon arrive at the same moment, neither willing to be first — they notice each other lingering, then both rush to beat the other, arriving almost simultaneously with their entourages in tow
- A few townsfolk look over, but it's common enough for the two of them to bicker in public that no crowd gathers
- Patches and Buumi arrive a few minutes later, and Gundren's limo pulls up last
- The doors swing wide to reveal the grinning face of a half-elf Thespian, who introduces himself as Steven and asks for invitations
- The group gives him a skeptical look; he realizes he's forgotten his mask, blanches, leaps behind a curtain for a goblin mask, and returns to the act with a touch of exasperation
- As the limo-carriage rolls up under its own artifice, a crowd of actors rushes forward to unfurl a red carpet and gather as a screaming crowd of admirers for Gundren
- The carriage door opens and the Dwarf Lord steps out in shining leather boots, gleaming breastplate, and bejeweled crown, bass-heavy music escaping the carriage and cutting off as he shuts the door behind him
- Everyone hands Steven their invitations and heads inside

## The Thespian Banquet

- [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]] welcomes the group into the main hall and beckons them to the stage to join him at the banquet table
- The hall is filled with matching tables, and a hundred or so Thespians pretending to be different members of the group crowd around plates piled with fake food
- The food at the main table is very real, and the group sits, confused but willing to entertain their strange friend
- [[lady-isabel|Izmakella]] is there in human form, seated at JmJm's right hand, delighting in the bizarre performance and drinking it all in
- The group tries to question the bard but he cuts them off, spinning into an epic performance detailing his plan to secure the full set of the [[instruments-of-the-bards|Instruments of the Bards]] — a legendary set of which he now has one
- Izmakella fills in a few gaps: they were each made by a god or goddess of bardic magic — Oghma, Sune, [[sharess|Sharess]], and the like — though no one is entirely sure
- JmJm needs the group's help tracking them down and turns to "Fëanon" to scry for him — except it isn't the real Fëanon
- The bard turns to one of his Thespians, evidently a wizard himself, who casts the scrying instead
- A Mandolin resolves in the crystal ball, displayed in an ornate glass case with a plaque nearby, in a hall that appears to be a larger collection of artifacts
- The group inspects the plaque and quickly identifies the location — Candlekeep, one of the most famous universities in the world. Their first lead
- Gundren promises to get his network sniffing out other prospective leads
- JmJm snaps his fingers, shouts orders, and his Thespians scatter to begin the search — one group heads for the Underdark, which alarms Patches, who suspects they'll be slaughtered
- The group enjoys the banquet for a few more hours before being stirred by a knock at the Theater's front door

## Jarlaxle

- Steven rushes to answer; the group hears his enthusiastic "We are closed for the evening!" cut off abruptly with a stifled yell
- The click-clack of high heels announces a swaggering figure striding out of the darkened foyer and into the main stage-hall
- Tall for a Dark Elf, whip-thin and lean, [[jarlaxle|Jarlaxle]]'s pearly grin shines from beneath a wide-brimmed feathered hat, his single un-eyepatched eye scanning the group and lingering on Izmakella — he winks, and she blows him a kiss
- He sweeps his hat off in a low bow and asks Patches: "I was summoned, my lady?"
- He seats himself, pours a glass of wine, and lets [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] question him — the whereabouts of her sisters and mother, and why he tried to blow them up on the boat
- He laughs off the boat, saying it gave everyone room and warned Patches quite clearly that something was up, all while honoring his obligations to [[house-faen-tlabbar|House Faen Tlabbar]]
- Everyone grumbles but accepts it, with a few threats thrown in for good measure
- Jarlaxle laughs it off — this body is only a simulacrum; the real Jarlaxle is indisposed in [[menzoberranzan|Menzoberranzan]]
- He lays it out: Bushra and Nasra are in [[baldurs-gate|Baldur's Gate]]; Dahlia is somewhere near the Barony, within an hour's magical travel; [[ishta|Ishta]] is in the Bloodstone lands with the House Wizard [[jacopo|Jacopo]] and some elites; the last sister, Fairuza, is with their mother [[vladalma|Vladalma]] in Menzoberranzan
- Menzoberranzan itself has gone strangely quiet since the Demon Lords were banished back to the Abyss — the priestesses reclusive, with worries of another Silence of Lolth, like before the Spellplague when [[lolth|Lolth]] stopped answering prayers
- Their divine magic still works fine, though — so it's something else
- His agents have flagged two things of note: Bushra is summoning a great number of extraplanar entities in Baldur's Gate — not demons, perhaps looking for information — and Ishta has been inquiring through back channels about fleeing House Faen Tlabbar through [[bregan-daerthe|Bregan D'aerthe]], much as Patches did decades ago, which could simply be her fishing to lay a trap
- Patches thanks him; he raises his glass
- JmJm starts talking business, wanting to import a herd of Carrion Crawlers to Snorbul's Stables — with the Khonsfa, Carrion Crawlers, and Cave Fishers, they'll have quite the menagerie
- Jarlaxle agrees, in exchange for some discounted Khonsfa goods
- The banquet draws on into the late hours. Everyone eats and drinks well, and one by one they head out into the nighttime Barony, toward home

## Mordenkainen's Favor

- [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] makes it back to his tower a little after midnight to find a familiar form leaning on the front desk, waiting for him
- The bald pate of [[mordenkainen|Mordenkainen]] shines in the ambient light of the foyer; the human grins at him and waves
- Fëanon sighs, approaches the human Archmage, and makes small talk
- Mordy is here because he needs a favor — he produces a small ceramic jar covered in runes, stoppered with some kind of tar, a long white spell scroll affixed to the front
- Mordy says it's a Soul Jar, currently holding a Lich named Robert the Red — from Oerth, part of a Cabal that Mordy and his associates are battling
- They couldn't find the Phylactery, so trapping him on another world with no body was the next best thing
- Fëanon agrees and takes the jar to stash in the vault, near [[quil-sygoth|Quil'Sygoth]], the Elder Evil spawn of Halashnar
- Fëanon then asks Mordy if he'd be willing to help with the creation of his Archwizard's staff — the Archmage of Oerth agrees
