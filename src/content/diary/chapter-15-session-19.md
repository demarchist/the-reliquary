## The Warbosses

- The group battles the leadership of the attacking warband
- They're tough, but the group cuts them down one by one — each body disintegrating as the plane's machinery recycles the soul back into the endless war
- As the fight winds down, a great cube begins to drift out of the void overhead
- On its surface, something huge is shifting: black and oily, a shapeshifting mass of hundreds of arms, legs, and bodies — an amalgam of a thousand warriors and beasts
- Goblinoids in both warbands drop to their knees, crying out in awe: **"WARFORM!"**

## The Warform

- The great Demon of War drifts closer as the group finishes the last of the enemy warbosses and rallies the now-combined warband to keep moving
- Hundreds strong, the army is too slow to clear the area. The cube passes overhead and the **[[the-warform|Warform]]** leaps on a thousand limbs
- It lands with a small earthquake, smashing into the departing warband, and begins slaughtering everything in reach — hundreds of weapon-wielding arms and claws, and great gouts of acid
- The group looks back and decides to leave the Goblins to it. They're already dead, locked into this plane forever, and the group is here for a book, not to kill a god of war
- They run for the plane-change at the edge of the cube. The warband comes with them; the Warform pursues
- The transition is ugly — gravity is reckoned per face — and the group leaps, flies, and runs across the edge
- [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] loses his orientation and falls up into the sky, then asserts control through his new [[windrunner-armor|robes]] and floats easily down to the new face
- [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]] tumbles from his seat atop [[arkto|Arkto]] but catches the saddle before he falls too far
- [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] turns to face the massive blob of war-torn souls bearing down — and, just as it moves to change faces, **Wishes** a Reverse Gravity onto it
- Eerily silent, the Warform simply falls up into the endless void and vanishes
- The surviving warband makes the crossing, losing only a few souls to the drop

## The Wreck

- Miles away across the desolate metal, the group can finally see the crashed spelljammer and the location of the missing book
- The warband surges ahead, clearing the path and crushing everything with ease
- At the wreck, they clear out the Swordwraiths and lingering dead, destroy the **Allip**, and find the book in the long-ruined captain's quarters
- Searching the ship, they find it stripped of anything meaningful — but [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]] finds the artistry familiar
- He thinks it's a **[[lunar-elf-spelljammer|spelljammer of the Lunar Elves]]**, and wants to salvage it despite the lack of remaining magic or tech
- They open a Gate back to the Barony and have the warband carry through as much wreckage as it can, [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] altering the gravity of the larger pieces so they simply fall through the portal
- The Goblinoids rush through with the pieces — and lose corporeality after a few steps, the metal and wood clattering to the ground outside the portal
- The Barony guard rushes to repel a perceived invasion and is thoroughly confused by the spectacle
- [[bohd|Bohd]] arrives quickly on his wyvern, spots the group through the portal, takes a fast briefing, and has the guard haul the wreckage clear of the Gate — most of the ship saved, in many, many pieces
- With the book secured and the Lunar spelljammer salvaged, the group rushes back through the Gate before it expires, leaving the warband of Goblinoid souls to their endless plane

## Home

- Worn out by Acheron, the group heads to [[the-establishment|The Establishment]] for an evening meal
- They Instant Messenger the book and the planar fork back to Candlekeep and receive, in return, the **[[instruments-of-the-bards|Instrument of the Bards]]** and the Abyssal Fork — along with a note addressed to "The Bard"
- The party retires to their homes

## The Teeth

- [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] arrives at the [[fane-of-selune|Fane]] to find a very nervous [[topsy-and-turvy|Topsy]] waiting
- There's been a delivery. The Deep Gnome leads him into the main hall: the feet of the great Cirrus statue are buried under thousands of bloody teeth
- The delivery came with a note — a letter from **Zulkhir Atlan Issan**, the Thayan Archwizard and [[warlocks-of-ceregorach|Warlock of Ceregorach]], goading Cirrus to return to the Bloodstone Lands and face him as one of only two remaining servants of the Tooth Fairy
- Cirrus looks over the piles of bloody teeth and turns to Topsy: *"Pack up the teeth and send it to my chambers. Do not disturb me after. I will be building this night."*
