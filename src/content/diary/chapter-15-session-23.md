## The Platter

The group recoiled from the cook's tray and backed away from the table. The great porcine chef laughed and strode back toward his kitchen.

The abandoned feast did not last. Baby Oni darted out from under tables and from behind the curtains, dozens of them, and fell on the platter of human remains. [[Cirrus Al Kayan|Cirrus]] killed one or two with Eldritch Blasts. The rest snatched what food they could and dove for cover, spitting curses at the Warlock from the shadows. The toddlers' language was no better than their table manners.

The party considered slipping away to look for the coffins. Something kept nagging at [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]]: the noises from the kitchens, and the condition of the meal. He wanted to see what lay behind the curtains.

## The Second Dining Room

Blue dividing curtains separated the hall from a larger room. When the party parted them, they found booths set in a ring around a curving bartop. Little Tengu hurried between tables, a Succubus delivered drinks, and the huge porcine chef worked at a chopping block in the back.

Each booth held about half a dozen figures. Closer inspection showed one Oni at every table, blowing noxious smoke from an opium pipe into the faces of four humans. Twenty humans in all feasted in a stupor, enthralled by five Oni.

Their bodies were changing. Every one of them was slowly gaining porcine features and growing enormously fat, and every one wore the holy symbol of [[Kelemvor]].

These were the missing Warlock Knights of the [[Crimson Dawn Knights|Crimson Dawn]], whose absence from the streets of [[helioglabulus|Helios]] [[Mister Luko]] had warned about. They had not simply vanished or been killed. They were being fed and fattened on human flesh, and the porcine change creeping over them echoed the cursed beast-man the party had killed in the sewers.

Gundren took in the scene and decided they had to intervene. The knights were enslaved to a terrible fate. He cocked his thundercannon, looked at the others, and they burst through the curtains on the Oni corruptors.

## The Dining Room Burns

A Hypnotic Pattern blazed above the bar, and a Deep Slumber swept across the room in the same moment. The human captives were pacified, two Oni were entranced, and several Tengu dropped into sleep.

The chef roared and charged out with a cleaver, straight into a storm of arrows and gunshots. The party overwhelmed him quickly and cut down several of the other Oni despite fierce resistance.

The noise reached the rest of the lair. A massive snake with a human face slithered out of a passage toward the room. The Succubus crept across the battlefield to charm [[Aliyah Faen Tlabbar|Patches]], and the drow knocked her to the floor and killed her moments later, with a joke at the devil's expense.

## A Shadow at the Doors

A woman's silhouette fell across a set of paper doors. They slid open on a geisha in a porcelain mask.
