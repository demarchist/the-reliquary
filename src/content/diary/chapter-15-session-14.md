## Back at the Barony

Almost everyone sleeps in.

- [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] doesn't sleep — gets his newest converts situated with the rest of the cult
- Rises from meditation and yodels as the sun starts to rise; the citizens yodel back
- Begins thinking through his plan to sever his own Shadow

## Patches

- Interrupted by the yodeling, heads out early to try and surprise [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]]
- Sneaks into Buumi's room — he's not there
- On the way back into town, spots [[lodan|Lo'Dan]] pretending to be a beggar
  - Gets word he's in town on business for [[house-faen-tlabbar|House Faen Tlabbar]] — they'll talk later
- Knocks on the alchemy shop door and chats with [[claudine|Claudine]] and her daughter Marzeline
- Asks after an Elixir of Viciousness — Claudine's heard of it, but it's rare indeed
  - She'll need to go to [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]] or [[lady-faye|Lady Faye]] to source the recipe

## Gundren

- Starts looking over [[gwen|Gwen]] and the schematics
- Has his brothers install an R-4K repeater internally
- Sources adamantite from his [[duergar-faction|Duergar]] contacts for reinforcement plating
- To get the most out of the adamantite, it needs tempering in dragonfire, or something of similar power
  - The primordial of fire beneath [[gauntlgrym|the dwarven capital]], perhaps?
- Begins the calculations for installing a Staff of Power inside her

## Tark, Buumi, Doc B, and Cassian

- [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] wakes in [[tarks-treehouse|his treehouse]], bruised and battered
- Talks with [[vaketh|Vaketh]] about his death — remembers the pull toward Helios, some vampiric entity calling to him
- Heads into the woods and finds a sleeping [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]], who'd tried to follow and look after his friend
- They talk — Buumi consoles him about the ordeal — and eat foraged berries and nuts
- [[doc|Doc B]], up early finishing his druid's staff and tending the Grove, hears voices on the way into town and finds his friends on the hillside enjoying the morning light
- Doc joins them and adds some Goodberries to breakfast
- Heading into town, they run into [[cassian|Cassian]], who'd been looking for Tark, and then [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] — in her giant sun-hat — who'd been looking for Buumi

## Fëanon

- Gets the morning briefing from his acolytes — everything's going well
- Two clones are finished, and a third vat is ready — this time, to grow [[theodemir|Theodemir]] a body of his own
- No news on the mysterious [[al-askarian-tablet|Tablet]] message
- Sets the acolytes to scrying on [[helioglabulus|Helioglabulus]] — to find out what's going on, and maybe where [[gaddock|Gaddock]]'s coffin is
- Comes across a pile of ash and a moustache… Stason Jatham?
  - At first thinks the tower's wards took care of the old Mindslayer
  - Then realizes the wards would have simply jettisoned [[stason-jatham|the man]] from the tower, not killed him
  - He was murdered
- Doesn't want to alert anyone his suspicions are raised, so heads out nonchalantly to meet the group in town and crosses the lake

## JmJm

- Wakes and finds his bed empty — immediately sours his mood
- Throws on a robe, summons his ThesPians
- Sends groups of adventurer-bards out on errands and heads into town to invite all his compatriots to a banquet that evening
- He has business to go over

## In Town

- The group spots [[lady-isabel|Izmakella]] in dragon form in the middle of town, playing with a group of children and [[thessivok|a small young Copper Wyrmling]]
- Patches asks the dragon for communication with [[jarlaxle|Jarlaxle]]
- Izmakella makes a deal with the rogue — steal something in [[baldurs-gate|Baldur's Gate]] for her, and Patches will put her in touch with [[lady-faye|Lady Faye]]

## The Establishment

- The group meets to discuss next steps — they need to figure out what's happening in [[helioglabulus|Helios]] and find [[gaddock|Gaddock]]'s coffin
- It's also brought up that it's time to upgrade some things
- That Red Wizard was shockingly difficult — [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] died and the Zulkhir fled, though his lackeys were killed
- Next time things need to go differently
- They head to [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]]'s [[rockseeker-manufactory|manufactory]] — it's time to make some long-awaited items

## The Manufactory

- Tark's [[windrunner-armor|Windrunner Armor]] is made — he takes the morning to break down all the dragonscales he's been collecting
  - The scales are taken to the manufactory and wrought into a weave
  - With Tark's scroll, the central eye of a beholder, Elder Brain Ichor, and a Gith Psion's heart, the robes are finally fashioned for Tark
- The group turns to Patches' request — an Amulet of Contingent Planeshift for her son, so he can return to the Prime Material Plane
  - [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] takes the ornate, beautiful necklace from Patches and works with the [[iron-tome|Iron Tome]] to prepare its enchantment: Planeshift, Contingency, Fëanon's Deep Slumber, and immunity to Charm and Fear
  - The powerful necklace is finished — it only needs to be delivered to Finn, in [[dokkar|Dokkar]]
- Fëanon's mind turns to his planned Archwizard's staff
  - [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] chats with Gundren about melting down two Staffs of Power and reforging them with Sovereign Glue and a Shadow-infused Voidgem
  - Gundren likes the idea, but melting the staffs down without blowing them up is going to take some doing
  - Fëanon meanwhile plans his own staff — needs the [[svirfneblin-faction|Svirfneblin]] to craft a kaleidoscope staff of cut gemstones, to be transmuted by Moonfire and Prismatic Light into Prismeum, then empowered by a triplicate of Wish spells
  - He has the [[gemstone-lens|focusing lens]] from the Underdark to do it, which also has the instructions
  - He knows there's Moonfire in [[dokkar|Dokkar]]
  - All he's missing is to actually commission the staff…
  - …and find a third caster of Wish
