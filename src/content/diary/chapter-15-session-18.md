## The Elminster Annex

- The group's Teleport from the [[river-surbrin|Surbrin]] lands them not in a quiet reading room but in the middle of a display hall whose arcane security is already waking
- Arcane armour steps down off its plinths; figures in the paintings along the walls draw their painted weapons and step toward the frames
- A gale whips through the hall, billowing every robe and cloak on display, and coalesces into a humanoid shape that fuzzes at the edges like storm-cloud
- In a booming voice the Djinni introduces himself as **Emir Suleiman [[emir-suleiman-al-zahir|al-Zahir]]** and demands to know why the group has Teleported directly into the **Elminster Annex**
- A closer look confirms it: every case in the hall relates to Elminster — a depleted wand of one of his apprentices, the journal of a lord who witnessed the Shadow Princes of Shade Enclave attack the Dales, and much more
- Fascinating, but not why they're here. They tell the Djinni they've come for one specific piece — the Mandolin
- al-Zahir is charged with the Annex's security and cannot let them take anything from the displays. But if they wish to *negotiate* for the instrument, he can take them to **Dean Rochester**
- The group agrees and is escorted out of the hall
- On the way, [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] asks why a Djinni would let himself be bound to a library, and says he understands the nature of the Djinn perfectly well, having True Polymorphed into one before
- al-Zahir is unimpressed — he likens it to cultural appropriation. The Warlock can never know what it is to truly live in the grand city of Ossa, among the noble houses
- Cirrus agrees to disagree. They arrive at an ornate office door bearing the golden symbol of [[mystra|Mystra]], which opens at a snap of the Djinni's fingers

## Dean Rochester

- Behind the desk sits a thin, elderly human with a monk's tonsure and brass spectacles, wrapped in absurdly ornate shining golden robes, his fingers heavy with precious rings
- He smiles faintly at the group — then frowns as [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]] shoves to the front to introduce himself
- The Dean clicks his tongue at the flamboyant bard, turns to [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]], and remarks that Candlekeep knows of Fëanon of the Lady's College
- Annoyed, JmJm launches into why he needs the Mandolin from the display
- The Dean snaps chairs into being for everyone and they settle in to discuss the instrument
- He is not opposed in principle — but when the Mandolin was donated, it came with a condition: only a sufficiently worthy bardic hero may take it. The college must judge JmJm's worth
- He sizes up the group's power, then smiles knowingly: there is a particular book, missing for a century, that Candlekeep has tracked to the plane of **[[acheron|Acheron]]**
- Acheron — the plane of endless war, where Goblinoid souls go after death, where the Blood War of the Tanar'ri plays out and Modrons march across plains of endless tumbling metal cubes
- JmJm scoffs and stands. He hasn't the time for this. He leaves, complaining about the Dean

## Negotiations

- [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] sends to [[lady-faye|Lady Faye]] about greasing palms at Candlekeep, and learns the [[zhentarim|Zhentarim]]'s influence in the college is sorely lacking — though Faye can loan her 10,000 gold to try
- Back in the office, the Dean is now offended by JmJm's antics and demands payment to repair the wards the group "broke" Teleporting in
- Fëanon suspects the man is simply being obstinate; the group works to mollify him and pull more detail on the missing book
- Terms are struck: the college will provide the Tuning Fork to Planeshift, on condition the group replaces it with one procured on site, and they may take the book-borrower's collateral — an Ioun stone — to help track him down
- [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] separately bargains for an **Abyssal** Tuning Fork
- Fëanon enters into a standing agreement with the Dean for a mutually beneficial exchange of extra-planar and rare tomes
- The group asks for lodging before Planeshifting and is given a cottage in the Candlekeep waystation town — a warren of shops for scholarly supplies and travellers' lodgings
- They rest uneventfully, JmJm grumbling about the Dean until [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] points out the old man is likely just a lonely curmudgeon

## The Bribe

- JmJm has a realisation and burns a **Wish** to have 25,000 gold delivered to the Dean, with a personalised note
- Next morning an Instant Messenger arrives — a tiny, lean copy of the Dean himself — bearing a thank-you note
- JmJm laughs that some people are so easy to manipulate
- A knock at the cottage door: a far more human-looking al-Zahir enters and presents the group with a rusted iron planar fork, which Cirrus takes eagerly
- The group braces for a hostile plane, joins hands, and Planeshifts to Acheron

## The Scrap Hall

- They arrive in a dim, smoky hall of scrap metal, full of Goblinoids drinking and feasting
- The hall goes silent. Every eye turns to the adventurers who just appeared out of nothing
- A roar of challenge — dozens of Goblins, Orcs, and Hobgoblins snatch up weapons
- [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] drops the majority of the charge with a single **Deep Slumber**
- The group ignores the smaller Goblins and goes straight for the largest Orc in the room — the one lounging and watching the fight — annihilating him and his entourage in seconds
- The battle stalls as the Goblinoids realise the newcomers just casually killed The Boss
- The hall rallies around strength: they cheer and raise their flagons and roasted meat (much of it, likely, from humanoid sources)
- JmJm leaps onto a scrap table. If they're going to cross a plane of endless war, they need a **War Party** — and this hall is a fine start
- The Orcs and Goblins are whipped into a frenzy: out across the Great Cube to find a High Elf wizard and a missing book

## The Plane of Endless War

- Outside, the group gets its first clear look at Acheron: an endless grey void studded with tumbling metal cubes, from dice-sized to planetoid-sized, every face crawling with peoples warring for the sake of war
- A cube covered in hundreds of Goblins sails past the flat plain they stand on — it doesn't strike, only skims, and hundreds of Goblins leap to make the crossing. Many land; many miss, screaming off into the void as their vessel drifts on
- [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] uses the Ioun stone to scry the High Elf wizard who took the book
- He sees an old spelljammer, crashed and splintered across the metal, rusted and rotted from decades of neglect, the wreck crawling with skeletal undead and wraiths
- A dark cloaked figure flits through the ruin — Fëanon recognises it as an **Allip**, likely the long-dead spirit of the mage they're hunting
- From the sky and the surrounding cubes, Fëanon judges they're on the right cube, a few faces away
- Dozens of miles of war-torn metal lie between them and the wreck. The group has a plan: roll across the landscape as a War Party, crush everything in the way, and absorb every warband they can

## The War Party Rolls Out

- They set out with the powerful party at the centre of a hooting, hollering Goblinoid horde that gathers strength as it goes
- JmJm plays from a scrap sled dragged by an Ogre; [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] rides a crow's nest lashed to the top, scouting the "horizon"
- Crossing a gouged stretch of cube, part of the warband stumbles onto an injured **Modron** that defends itself and kills a few Goblins
- The alarm goes up; [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]] rushes over before the Goblins can finish it, repairs the construct, and gets information — a Modron pass or channel some miles ahead, in the direction of the crashed spelljammer
- They scoop up the injured Modron and press on
- Just as they near the edge of the cube and the awkward business of swapping faces, another warband crests the horizon — a little larger than their own
- The two forces slam together in a brutal melee. The group drives straight for the enemy leadership, intent on killing or subjugating it fast and absorbing the survivors before the two sides tear each other apart
