## The Queen of Masks

The geisha glided forward, drew a small tanto from her belt, and shimmered. A duplicate stepped out of her, and then another, until four masked women stood around the party. Each giggled in the same silvery voice and introduced herself as [[Kishin Doji]], Oni Queen.

She knew who they were. She said little else.

Her blade, mirrored across every duplicate, shot out sixty feet or more in a single thrust, striking the party from every angle at once. As the group destroyed one copy and then another, new ones appeared with every passing moment. The duplicates had real substance: they took hits, attacked, and cast spells. Kishin Doji could also swap her true location with any of them.

Around her, the remaining Oni fought hard with nightmare rays and cones of cold. They fell one by one, but not before they sent [[Steden]] back to the Nine Hells and injured [[Jack Mayer Johnson Mathews|JmJm]].

Baby Oni tackled Cirrus, wrenched out one of his eyes, and met a warlock who simply wrestled it back, pushed it into the socket, and healed himself. Other babies swarmed [[Tarkathee Daru|Tark]] to claw at his eyes. [[Horatio Von Bumidere|Buumi]] intercepted them and crushed them.

## The Serpent and the Heads

The great serpent slithered into the fight and was stuck fast by one of Patches's Tar bombs. It was shot repeatedly while immobilised, then vomited a deluge of decapitated heads that swarmed the rogue. She stepped out of reach with Misty Step as Buumi moved in to destroy the undead heads.

Tark used his new robes to drop across the room onto an Oni, killing it with a neck-snapping kick. From a side passage, a tentacled horror, a Bloodkiss, rose and assaulted him.

Kishin Doji created a duplicate beside Gundren and charmed the dwarf. [[Fëanon Sërindë|Fëanon]] counterspelled it, then disintegrated a duplicate in response. The serpent coiled around Gundren to crush and swallow him, but the dwarf broke free. In the struggle, [[Gwen|Gwen]] was knocked out of the fight. Gundren unloaded on the serpent with everything he had and killed it.

Cirrus Polymorphed into a Deva and struck at the Oni Queen, who stabbed him repeatedly in return. When he tried to retaliate, the true Kishin Doji turned invisible and teleported into a duplicate. Each copy stabbed at Deva-Cirrus. He destroyed several, but lost concentration and fell back into his tiefling form.

## The Last Duplicate

The last of the Oni fell. Tark finished the Bloodkiss.

Kishin Doji tried to flee, but Faerie Fire revealed her, and the group cornered her. The Oni Queen was slain, but she wounded the party terribly first.

## What the Illusion Hid

The Mirage Arcane began to warble and fail. The paper walls, silk curtains, and lacquered wood slid away, and the Helios Palace crypt appeared in their place: human stonework, plain and ancient.

Moans of pain still came from a side passage. Without their curtains, the cursed humans of the dining hall lay unconscious on the stones. From the entrance to this section of the catacombs, where the foyer of the sake-house had stood, the party could hear a commotion.

The Oni Queen's private chamber dissolved too. Its paper partitions receded into stone, revealing a large mausoleum off the main catacomb. Three black coffins stood in the centre of the room. Liquid dripped onto their closed lids, one drop after another.

Three black coffins in a vaulted chamber was exactly what [[Horatio Von Bumidere|Buumi]] had seen in his vision at [[gravenhollow|Gravenhollow]] while searching for [[Gaddock|Gaddock's]] resting place. The party had not yet opened one.
