## What the Sewers Remembered

The route began beneath [[helioglabulus|Helios]].

One of [[Mister Luko|Mister Luko's]] footpads led the party through the dank sewers, following signs cut in Thieves' Cant. Between the guide and [[Aliyah Faen Tlabbar|Patches]], the first miles passed quickly.

The recent traffic was impossible to miss. [[Fëanon Sërindë|Fëanon]] knelt over the marks and found more than bootprints: grooves cut by torn fingernails, bloody handprints dragged across stone, shoes and pieces of clothing abandoned in struggle. The missing citizens had come this way unwillingly.

The tunnels descended through the buried history of the city. Worked sewers opened into older catacombs, then into compressed ruins through which later masons had forced greywater channels. Every layer sloped toward a great cistern and the oldest undercroft beneath the castle.

A wet commotion echoed ahead.

The party found a man bent over a humanoid corpse and eating it. His body had twisted into something porcine: snout, bristles, and the hunched shape of a beast wearing the remnants of a citizen.

[[Cassian]] shot him dead immediately.

Two other corpses lay badly gnawed in the alcove. Examination of the thing Cassian had killed revealed that it had been human, transformed by a curse rather than born a monster. [[Horatio Von Bumidere|Buumi]] looked down at the body and wondered whether they might have saved him.

Luko's guide had no intention of discovering what else might be saved. He fled back through the sewers.

## The Blue Cistern

Patches heard heavy footsteps carrying through the tunnels. Her Drow senses separated their route from the echoes and led the group forward.

The sewer opened without warning into a colossal cistern. Water poured from a dozen tunnel mouths and fell a hundred feet into a black pool. Bioluminescent algae climbed the walls, washing the space in soft blue light.

Across the gulf, one of the [[Thayan Flesh Golems|black-leather giants]] stepped from a tunnel carrying two motionless bodies. It descended a concealed stair spiralling around the cistern wall toward the levels below.

JmJm jumped into the opening and raised his ten rings. A column of lightning crossed the chamber and struck the golem. The two captives burned away in the blast.

The creature did not.

It turned toward him as the energy dispersed harmlessly through its core. Tark rushed it while the group's ranged weapons opened from the opposite side. Under the concentrated attack, flesh and machinery came apart.

Something beneath the water reached for Tark before the pieces stopped falling.

A tentacle the width of a tree trunk snapped over the ledge and missed him. A gargantuan Otyugh rose in the pool, two more limbs searching the stair for food. [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] fired into it. The rest of the party joined him, and the creature roared beneath the hail before sinking back into the churning water. It was driven off, not proven dead.

The group descended.

## The Oldest Stone

Below the cistern, even the masonry changed. The passages were human work, but ancient enough to predate the city above in any form the party recognised. Patches's copied map kept them moving toward the castle's undercroft.

The golems had left a road of their own. Scrapes in the floor, blood, and the smell of bodies all followed the same route. The constructs attacking the Rising Sun were not simply weapons sent upward. They were collectors bringing the people of Helios down.

The stench of rot thickened until breathing became an effort. Patches stopped at the mouth of a broad corridor. Her eyes reached farther into its dark than anyone else's.

Hundreds of zombies milled ahead.

The tunnel was high enough for the party's fliers to pass above the dead. They considered burning the entire mass through Hot Gates, but there were too many bodies and too little understanding of what lay beyond them. Patches went alone.

She climbed onto the ceiling and moved over the horde through a maze of corpse-filled passages. An enormous stone door stood partly open at the far end, too heavy for her to move but wide enough to squeeze through above the dead.

Beyond it lay an ornate tomb converted into a laboratory.

## The Book Beneath the Prisoner

A [[Red Wizards of Thay|Thayan Wizard]] in crimson robes worked beside an elaborate sarcophagus. A warlock in black armour guarded him. Five tall iron frames or cages stood among tables crowded with instruments and apparatus, and a second iron door sealed the far wall.

At the centre of the chamber, a human prisoner hung from the ceiling in chains. Cuts covered the body. Blood fell steadily into a black book floating open beneath it.

Two small blue [[Oni]] crouched near the rear of the tomb, gnawing on one of several corpses and giggling obscenities at the Wizard while he worked.

Patches withdrew without being seen and brought the shape of the room back to her companions.

The details matched [[Horatio Von Bumidere|Buumi's]] vision in [[gravenhollow|Gravenhollow]]: human-built [[The Catacombs of Helioglabulus|catacombs]], infant Oni living among the dead, and a protected complex somewhere beyond. They had not yet found the three black coffins from the vision, but they were inside the architecture that held them.

The party resolved to go over the zombies rather than through them. Gundren's helmet burned a path where one was needed, and the ceaseless noise of the infant Oni covered their approach. They reached the stone door in formation.

Then they burst through it.

## The Tomb Opens

Three Fireballs struck the sarcophagus chamber. The infant Oni died in the blasts. So did the prisoner suspended above the book.

JmJm summoned his Arcane Hand and lost it almost immediately when the armoured warlock hit him hard enough to break his concentration. The warlock fell under the answering assault, then began to rise again as undead flesh twisted and bones returned to place.

Tark, Patches, and Buumi closed on the red-robed necromancer. He fought for his life amid the wreckage of his worktables.

Gundren ignored him.

The floating book bound the room together: prisoner above, blood below, and hundreds of corpses moving in the tunnels outside. Gundren levelled his weapon at it and fired.

The book exploded in a deluge of blood.

Every zombie in the catacombs dropped.

The sarcophagus lid flew from its resting place.

A Mummy Lord rose from the opened tomb and turned on JmJm while the Thayan necromancer, surrounded and bleeding, struggled to stay alive.

Somewhere beyond the iron door lay the coffins they had come to find.
