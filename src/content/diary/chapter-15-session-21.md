## Into the Sewers

- Guided by one of [[mister-luko|Mister Luko]]'s footpads, the group descends into the dank sewers beneath [[helioglabulus|Helios]], following clear Thieves' Cant markers left along the walls
- Between the guide's local knowledge and [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]]' own eye for the dark, the going is quick and easy

## Signs of the Taken

- The tunnels show heavy, recent traffic. [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] crouches to examine the tracks more closely and finds scratches from torn fingernails, dried bloody handprints dragged along the stone, and scraps of torn clothing and shoes
- People are being dragged down here, and fighting for their lives on the way down. Losing
- The group follows the trail deeper. The stonework ages around them, the sewers giving way in stretches to old catacombs and compressed ruin before opening again onto flowing greywater — all of it draining toward a single destination: a great cistern feeding the oldest levels of the [[the-undercroft|undercroft]]

## The Beastman

- An echoing commotion stops them — something bestial, ahead. They move in cautiously to investigate
- In an alcove they find a man bent double over a corpse, feeding. His features have twisted into something porcine — tusked, bristled, wrong
- [[cassian|Cassian]] puts him down before he can look up
- Two more bodies lie nearby, badly gnawed. Examining the beastman, the group realises he was no monster born — he was cursed, a human transformed. [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]] shakes his head. Maybe they could have saved him
- Luko's guide takes one look at the pig-thing and bolts back the way they came

## Into the Cistern

- [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] catches the echo of heavy footsteps somewhere ahead and leads the group toward it, her Drow eyes picking the route through the tunnels without the guide
- The passage opens into the cistern they've been aiming for — water sheeting down into the pit from a dozen tunnel mouths, falling a hundred feet to a pool below, the whole cavern lit a soft blue by bioluminescent algae climbing the walls
- Across the open expanse, a huge black-leather-clad shape steps into the mouth of a tunnel: a Thayan golem, nine feet of it, carrying two unmoving bodies. It turns and descends a concealed stair spiralling down the cistern wall

## The Golem and the Otyugh

- [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]] leaps forward and empties all ten of his rings into it — a column of lightning that incinerates the two bodies in its arms
- Its victims are gone, and the golem turns on JmJm, unharmed
- [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] closes to engage while the group's ranged fire tears the construct apart in moments
- As it falls, a huge tentacle lashes up out of the depths for Tark — he steps clear without trouble, and the group turns to find a gargantuan Otyugh risen from the pool below, tentacles questing for a meal
- [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]] opens up on it; after a hailstorm of shots the thing roars in pain and disappears back beneath the swirling water

## Down to the Undercroft

- The group takes the golem's spiral stair down, descending into truly ancient tunnels — the oldest human stonework in the city
- With [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]]' transcribed map, they make good time and realise they're walking the same paths the golems have been using to drag victims out of the city and down toward the castle
- The air turns thick with the stench of rot

## The Horde

- A particularly wide corridor ahead makes Patches call a halt. In the dark, her Drow eyes pick out hundreds of Zombies milling in the passage
- The tunnel is tall enough that the group's fliers could clear the horde outright, and someone floats the idea of simply hot-gating past it — but there are too many to be sure, and Patches heads off alone to scout before anyone commits to a plan

## Patches' Reconnaissance

- She climbs along the ceiling and picks her way through a warren of tunnels thick with the walking dead, and finds a great stone door standing ajar — too heavy for her to shift, wide enough to slip through
- Inside is an elaborate tomb chamber, a handful of Zombies among the dead already inside
- Her attention goes to three things: a [[red-wizards-of-thay|Thayan]] Wizard in crimson robes working at a desk beside an ornate sarcophagus, a Thayan Warlock in black armour standing guard, and a black book floating open beneath a chained prisoner — a human, hung from the ceiling, lacerated and bleeding steadily down into the pages
- Five large iron cages stand near a set of tools laid out for use. Against the far wall, past two more corpses, two small blobby blue Oni infants gnaw on a body and giggle profanities at the working Wizard. A closed iron door stands behind him, unopened
- Patches slips back out through the maze above the horde and reports everything she saw
- [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]] goes quiet at the description of the baby Oni — the same small blue shapes he once saw swarming a vault of black coffins in his [[gravenhollow|Gravenhollow]] vision. Wherever those coffins are, they can't be far from here

## The Assault

- The group decides to climb the ceiling route Patches scouted, using the constant noise of the baby Oni and the Wizard's own work to cover their approach, and burst through the stone door to storm the chamber
- A trio of Fireballs slam into the sarcophagus, killing the Oni infants and the prisoner still bleeding into the cursed book
- [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]] summons his Arcane Hand — and takes a brutal hit from one of the Warlocks that breaks his concentration
- The first Warlock falls, its undead flesh twisting and reforming to stand back up again within moments — the same trick the [[rising-sun|Rising Sun]] Warlocks pulled
- [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]] unloads on the floating book while the rest of the group closes to melee with the Thayans
- The book explodes in a deluge of blood — every Zombie in the horde outside drops dead at once — and in the same instant the sarcophagus lid flies off. A Mummylord steps out and attacks [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]]
- The red-robed Necromancer takes hits from [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]], [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]], and [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]] all at once, crying out — fighting for his life as the session ends
