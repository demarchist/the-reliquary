## The Gate

- The group decides they need more information before settling on a plan
- [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] mentions [[gravenhollow|Gravenhollow]], the great extra-planar library of the Stone Giants
- He opens a Gate at the [[rockseeker-manufactory|Manufactory]] and they step through into the Plane of Earth
- The great geode of the library glitters ahead, and the group's greeting echoes ominously through the gargantuan chamber
- They ascend the carved stone steps by way of a smaller staircase cut into the giant-sized ones
- No one comes to greet them, and as they approach the great door, they realize it stands ajar

## The Blood in the Foyer

- They move in cautiously, [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] scouting ahead
- Past the foyer, in the first main chamber, something catches the light — a huge pool of blood with footprints leading off toward the Chamber of the Present
- They investigate — it's definitely Giant blood, and a fair amount of it
- Patches feels like she's being watched but can't locate any enemies
- The group is attacked by Earth Elemental Myrmidons, enraged by whatever happened here
- One grabs [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]] and pulls him into the ground — the group reacts fast, counterattacking and killing that one first
- The elementals are tough and hit hard — Patches takes some vicious hits — but the Myrmidons are quickly destroyed

## The Shattered Guardian

- The group turns their investigation back toward the missing library guardians and moves deeper in
- They head into the Halls of the Past
- Before long they come into a grand hall filled with shattered stone shards
- It's quickly apparent they're fragments of a shattered statue
- With the blood in the entryway, the group suspects a petrified creature — and sure enough, once the pieces are gathered, it's clearly one of the Stone Giant Guardians
- They keep the pieces in a Demiplane, then use the Halls of the Past to investigate their chosen subjects

## Visions of the Past

- [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] goes looking for Prismeum secrets, scouring the archives for [[netherese|Netherese]] references
  - He finds a vision of ancient arcanists channeling pure Weave energy into the prismatic crystal — Silver Fire from [[mystra|Mystra]], the way the Netherese made it, not Moonfire like the Svirfneblin
- [[cassian|Cassian]] searches for any sign of his parents' recent whereabouts and sees the High Sun Games of [[neverwinter|Neverwinter]]
  - A group of older adventurers rushes through a gauntlet of moving pillars, chased by Displacer Beasts
  - The arena is suddenly filled with obscuring red mist from a figure floating above — a Red Wizard of Thay holding a glowing red artifact
  - His parents and their group vanish into the red mist
- [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]] looks into what happened here in Gravenhollow — he already has his suspicions, with Corpsetaker sniffing around some time ago
  - Sure enough, he sees a vision of the attack on the library — an Iron-Skeleton blasting the Stone Giants while Oni swarm the Earth Elemental guards
  - [[zhengyi|Zhengyi]] — and he may have found the location of [[ythryn|Ythryn]], the lost Netherese city, while he was here

## The Halls of the Present

- In the Halls of the Present, the group finds the slumped form of a second Giant guardian, dead for some time from a deep stab wound in his side
- Numerous other, freshly healed wounds cover the corpse — it clearly took a lot to bring him down
- They put the body in the Demiplane and press on into the archives, intent on getting what they came for

## More Visions

- [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] looks into the location of the knife he can use to sever his Shadow
  - He sees a knapped obsidian knife with a white linen-wrapped handle, glowing with a soft light, resting on a purple velvet pillow atop a marble altar
  - It sits in a temple of [[selune|Selûne]] — a monastery of monks and nuns high in the Mountains of Amn, a quiet place of gentle reflection nestled atop green slopes and snowy peaks
- [[cassian|Cassian]], closer to his parents than he's ever been, looks into the present location of the Thayan wizard from his last vision
  - He sees a pale, emaciated human woman marching through stone corridors as pale human servants bow, eyes down
  - She passes through a guarded doorway flanked by undulating spectral undead and sets something on an altar — a crystal bottle with intricate golden filigree, filled with swirling liquid
  - A face resolves in the bottle — a soul trapped within. Either a Soul Jar or a Phylactery
  - The Thayan leaves the chamber; a slab of runed metal seals the doorway and its undead guards behind her
  - She passes dozens of other similarly sealed chambers on her way out. This is the Doomvault of Thay
- [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]] looks into the location of [[gaddock|Gaddock]]'s coffin
  - He sees a human-wrought catacomb of ancient tombs and vaults, crawling with small, pudgy, baby-like humanoids — snarling twisted faces, curling tusks, glowing eyes
  - Hundreds of small Oni crawl over one another, fighting, devouring the weakest, shapeshifting as they learn their powers
  - The baby fiends crowd a well-kept section of the catacombs, and the paladin sees three black coffins in a large vaulted chamber
  - The baby Oni keep clear of the coffins despite the crowding
  - This must be where Gaddock lies. Perhaps the other two belong to the Queen and the Vampire Knight

## The Halls of the Future

- [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] heads to the Halls of the Future — she knows her mother is plotting and wants insight
- In the viewing chamber she finds a living Stone Giant Guardian — [[ustova|Ustova]]
- The aloof, reserved archivist welcomes her and either doesn't know or won't reveal anything about her dead kin
- She remembers Patches and bids her join her on the dais for a vision
- Patches takes a seat, looks over the massive enchanted stone tablets, and is transported
- She sees fields of flowers blooming across a flat expanse of summer tundra
- Oni and Dark Elves surround the dead forms of her friends, and Patches herself lies defeated in the dirt
- Her sisters surround her, and her mother stands over her with the ritual dagger in her delicate charcoal hands
- A massive form shifts behind the Matron Mother — the great Black Dragon [[ancalagon|Ancalagon]] looks on with glee as [[vladalma|Vladalma]] plunges the knife into Patches's chest
- Patches comes out of the vision and quickly memorizes everything she saw
- She recognizes the blooming flowers from the group's recent trip to the Bloodstone lands
- Her mother intends to work with Ancalagon — maybe even [[zhengyi|Zhengyi]] — in the tundra of [[vaasa|Vaasa]]

## Going Home

- The group has much to think about, and two Giants they might attempt to resurrect
- They Planeshift back to the [[thunder-bluff|Barony]], but Gravenhollow's temporal nature costs them the better part of a day
- They arrive the next day as the sun begins to set
