## The Last Work of the Necromancer

The [[Red Wizards of Thay|Thayan necromancer]] died quickly. The Mummy Lord followed him, though not before curse and wound had passed through the party.

When the fighting ended, the tomb began to explain itself.

Five iron frames stood around the ruined workshop. They had not been cages for prisoners, as they first appeared, but cradles for greater [[Thayan Flesh Golems|Thayan flesh golems]]. A needle on the Wizard's worktable lay beside a human skull, the remains of a Banshee bound into the machinery of construction. The dead were not merely raw material here. Their labour continued after death, stitching other corpses into weapons.

[[Fëanon Sërindë|Fëanon]] picked up the skull.

For roughly a minute, the Banshee wore him. The others noticed the subtle wrongness in their companion and cleansed him before the possession could settle. The ghost screamed back into its prison. Fëanon, restored to himself, pocketed the skull.

The opened sarcophagus offered two brilliantly glowing potions and a collection of cursed Canopic Jars. Fëanon used a Mage Hand to put the lot into a bag of holding without touching them. Then memory caught up with method. In Gracksuluth, another Mummy Lord had rebuilt itself from its jars inside just such a bag and escaped in a storm of violence.

This time, Fëanon intended to deal with the thing before it grew a body.

## The Iron Door

The heavy iron door at the rear of the tomb had been used recently and often. Dust and cobwebs had been pushed away from its edges by repeated passage.

Beyond it, a stone stair spiralled upward. A sweet, sickly haze drifted down the steps. [[Jack Mayer Johnson Mathews|JmJm]] recognised opium before anyone else named it.

[[Aliyah Faen Tlabbar|Patches]] climbed alone while the others rested below. As she ascended, the human masonry changed. Stone softened into timber. Cloth spread over bare walls. Sconces became paper lanterns, and the ancient stair took on the architecture of an eastern tavern or pleasure house.

The transformation was not a simple disguise. Fëanon later identified a powerful *Mirage Arcane*: the reality of the place rewritten around a monster's lair.

## Authority at the Threshold

At the top, Patches found a foyer and front desk staffed by three squat, red-faced Tengu in white robes and long-nosed hats. Voices carried from the far side of the room. One belonged to [[Dimitri Muskov]].

The young noble was no longer merely pale. He was red-eyed, fanged, and accompanied by another vampire dressed in Helios finery. Dimitri had heard that the establishment served the finest rice wine in the city and demanded admission.

The Tengu refused him.

Vampires held no authority in the domain of [[Kishin Doji]], they said—especially spawn. Knives appeared when Dimitri pressed the matter. He and his companion retreated through the outer doors, furious and unaware that Patches had watched the exchange.

The encounter confirmed that the infection among [[helioglabulus|Helios's]] nobles had reached House Muskov. It also revealed a boundary inside the conspiracy below the city. Whatever arrangement joined Thayans, vampires, and Oni, the newly turned could not simply walk into the Oni Queen's court and command respect.

## A Table Among Oni

Patches returned to the group just as JmJm was preparing to leave for reinforcements. The report of a hidden court, opium, and sake changed his mind.

They climbed together. JmJm, Fëanon, and [[Cirrus Al Kayan|Cirrus]] would approach openly and talk their way past the attendants. The others would remain ready to move. Near the landing, [[Horatio Von Bumidere|Buumi]] and [[Tarkathee Daru|Tark]] barricaded a storeroom door. Through it, Patches glimpsed a massive pig-man in a blood-soaked apron squeezing between shelves of sake before disappearing into a kitchen beyond.

The Tengu were alarmed when the party emerged from below, but Cirrus and JmJm demanded a table with enough confidence that one attendant led them inside. Patches slipped unseen into the coatroom behind the desk.

The dining hall was arranged around a bamboo-matted fighting ring. Adult Oni occupied nearly every low table. Dozens of their children—fat, red, blue, and quarrelsome—clambered through the room while carefully avoiding their elders.

A Succubus in elegant silk emerged through a curtain to take the party's order. Behind her came the sounds of dishes, laughter, tearing flesh, and muffled cries. When the curtain fell, the uglier noises vanished with it.

At the head of the room, a paper door painted with fiends devouring humans sealed a private chamber.

## The Queen's Son

The door opened.

A small red Oni descended the steps, followed by the larger warriors who had been inside. He introduced himself as [[Nobutada]]: son of Kishin Doji and brother to [[Li Xian]] and [[Neero]], two Oni the party had killed years apart without knowing they belonged to the same family.

Nobutada did not challenge them. He said that he had business in the North which did not involve fighting the party.

Then he clapped his hands.

Every adult Oni in the dining hall rose and fell into formation behind him. Nobutada led them out of the court and down the spiral stair, toward the ruined Thayan workshop the party had just left. Whether they meant to reinforce it, investigate its destruction, or pass through it on some other errand remained unclear.

The children swarmed the abandoned dishes as soon as their elders were gone.

## Food's Ready

Patches moved while the front desk stood unwatched. She killed the three Tengu, hid their bodies in the coatroom, and reshaped herself into a likeness of the serving Succubus.

She returned to the party's table with an apology: she had forgotten their order.

A red silk curtain covered a side passage nearby. From behind it came several overlapping voices, low and pained. Patches had barely begun to warn the others when the kitchen curtain heaved aside.

The enormous pig-man entered carrying a platter. Steam rose around glazed bread, dumplings, suckling pig, and racks of ribs. The court's glamour struggled to hold as he approached. The dumplings became severed heads wrapped in dough. The roast resolved into a humanoid body.

The cook lifted the feast and bellowed across the room.

"Food's ready!"
