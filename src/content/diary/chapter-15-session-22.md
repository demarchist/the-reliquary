## The Necromancer Falls

- The [[red-wizards-of-thay|Thayan]] Necromancer is quickly slain and the Mummylord destroyed alongside him — not before both curse and wound several members of the party

## Searching the Tomb

- The group searches the chamber and the remains of the necromantic book, working out that the Necromancer had been attending five Greater Thayan Golems, with a worktable set up beside them holding a cursed Banshee skull and needle for stitching the constructs' flesh back together
- [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] picks up the skull and is possessed by the ghost bound inside it for a minute or so, before the rest of the group notices his odd behaviour and cleanses him — the Banshee wrenched back into its skull, which Fëanon promptly pockets
- In the sarcophagus they find two brilliantly glowing potions and a number of cursed Canopic Jars. Resisting the urge to touch them, Fëanon scoops the lot into a bag of holding with a Mage Hand
- The move triggers a shared memory: the last time the group dealt with a Mummy Lord, they took its Canopic Jars into a bag of holding the same way — and the thing reconstituted inside the bag and escaped, causing chaos. Fëanon resolves to deal with this one before it can build itself a new body

## The Iron Door

- The group investigates the heavy iron door at the far end of the tomb and finds it's been opened repeatedly and recently — the dust and cobwebs all pushed back
- Beyond it, they're at the bottom of a large stone spiral staircase, a sickly sweet haze drifting down from above
- [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]] names the smell at once: opium
- Resisting the intoxicating smoke, [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] moves up the stairs to scout while the others take a quick rest

## The Mirage Arcane

- The human stonework begins to shift and morph unnaturally as Patches climbs, sprouting wood and cloth until the stairwell is entirely wooden and of foreign make — the door ahead reading not like a human tomb but an Eastern tavern or brothel, paper lanterns on the wall in place of sconces
- The catacombs beneath the palace have been unnaturally transformed
- The door opens easily and the opium haze thickens, catching in Patches's throat before she centres herself

## Dimitri, Turned

- Patches hears voices ahead and recognises one of them — [[dimitri-muskov|Dimitri]], the young House Muskov noble the group met days ago, whose father [[brusso-muskov|Lord Brusso]] may or may not have become a vampire
- She slinks forward and cracks the next door to peer through at the commotion: a front desk and foyer staffed by a trio of squat, demonic Tengu wrapped in white robes, ornate long-nosed hats, and bright red faces
- Arguing with them are two pallid human nobles in exquisite Helios finery, red-eyed and fanged — vampires. Dimitri is one of them
- He's heard the Oni serve the finest rice wine in the city and wants in. The Tengu refuse him outright: vampires hold no authority in the domain of [[kishin-doji|Kishin Doji]] — especially spawn
- Knives come out when Dimitri raises his voice, and he gets the message. He and his companion turn and storm off through a large set of wooden doors
- With the vampires gone, the Tengu laugh and mock them, returning to their posts. Patches has seen enough and starts back down to the group

## JmJm Nearly Leaves

- Meanwhile, JmJm grows impatient and checks back the way the group came — the horde of Zombies now lies still, dropped dead with the destruction of the book
- He tells [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] he's going to leave for reinforcements, just as Patches returns. Her account of Kishin Doji's mysterious lair, sake, and opium piques the bard's interest immediately, and he stays

## Into the Court

- With the only way forward to [[gaddock|Gaddock]]'s coffin lying onward and upward, the group begins a stealthy ascent
- They decide [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]], [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]], and [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] should simply talk their way past the front desk with charm, while working out where the coffin could be
- Fëanon studies the shifting, changing stairwell as they climb and confirms the magic: a powerful Mirage Arcane, marking this unmistakably as a lair
- At the top of the landing they prepare and barricade a nearby storeroom door
- Just as [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]] and Tark seal it, Patches glimpses through into the sake-laden shelves beyond and sees a hulking figure squeeze through a door on the far side — a massive pig-man with savage tusks and pink flabby skin, wearing a blood-soaked apron over a gi. The door closes

## The Front Desk

- The group strolls out onto the landing and into the foyer to approach the desk
- The Tengu look up in surprise, then at each other in alarm
- Cirrus and JmJm demand a table. Nervous, fangs bared in tight smiles, the little Oni agree, and one hops down to lead them in
- They pass through a wood-paneled hallway with a single door into the coat closet behind the desk — Patches slips inside, unseen

## The Dining Hall

- The group is led into a plush lounge of low wood tables and ornate pillows, a small bamboo-matted fighting ring at its centre
- Every table but one holds huge, powerful-looking Oni, all giving the party the stink eye. Dozens of baby Oni — fat, red and blue — climb over each other nearby, harassing one another but steering well clear of their elders
- A Succubus in elegant silk emerges through a curtained wall from an adjoining chamber, spilling out the sound of clinking dishes and laughter with an undercurrent of danger beneath it — tearing flesh, ravenous gluttony, squeals of pain
- The sounds vanish as the curtain falls shut. The Succubus takes the group's order, her charm failing outright against them
- One of the large Oni rises, climbs a short flight of steps to an ornate paper door — calligraphy and art of fiends devouring humans worked into it — and slips inside a VIP chamber, the door shutting behind him
- The Succubus disappears behind the curtain to place the order, briefly revealing more of the gluttonous sounds within

## Nobutada

- A small, red-skinned Oni opens the grand paper door a moment later and descends the steps, the other Oni filing out behind him and sealing the VIP chamber again
- He saunters over, sneering, and introduces himself: [[nobutada|Nobutada]], brother of [[li-xian|Li Xian]] and [[neero|Neero]], son of [[kishin-doji|Kishin Doji]]
- He has business in the North that doesn't involve fighting the group, and turns to leave
- Nobutada claps his hands. Every large Oni in the hall rises and assembles behind him. He leads them all from the chamber, leaving the horde of baby Oni to surge forward over the abandoned plates and bones — many of them eyeing the group
- Nobutada and his Oni descend the spiral stair, deep into the Undercroft — toward where the Thayan Necromancer was slain

## Patches Moves

- With the desk unwatched, Patches assassinates the Tengu, hides the bodies in the coat closet, and Alters Self into a passable copy of the serving Succubus
- She shifts out into the dining hall and approaches the table, apologising that she "forgot the order"
- A hallway near the table, up a small set of stairs, is blocked by a red silk curtain. Mid-sentence, Patches catches the sound of moaning — several overlapping voices, faint behind the fabric
- Before she can finish warning the others, a huge figure pushes into the chamber from the kitchens: the hulking porcine cook, hauling a massive tray of steaming buns, glazed bread, suckling pig, and racks of ribs
- The glamour doesn't quite hold — the wontons resolve into dumpling-wrapped heads, and the "suckling pig" is unmistakably a roasted humanoid
- The Pig strides forward, hefting its barbaric feast. **"FOOD'S READY!"**
