## The Effigy

- [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] heads up to his sanctum while his servants gather the teeth
- Once they're collected, he has [[topsy-and-turvy|Topsy]] summon his top cleric, [[temura|Temura]]
- The high elf arrives and looks over the boxes and boxes of teeth with open distaste
- Cirrus has him fabricate the teeth into a passable likeness of a demonic Cirrus — an effigy of what he might have become if he'd stayed with the Marilith and [[ceregorach|Ceregorach]]
- The Warlock then True Polymorphs the tooth-statue into the equivalent of an iron golem
- Temura leaves, but questions Cirrus's intentions aloud and wonders whether he ought to contact the Church of [[selune|Selûne]] in Silverymoon about it
- Cirrus is incensed. He summons [[topsy-and-turvy|Topsy and Turvy]], who admit they've been questioning Temura's allegiance — whether he's a spy for the "real" church
- Furious at the remark, Cirrus slaps [[topsy-and-turvy|Topsy]] a few times and sends him from the chamber in shame
- They will keep an eye on Temura

## Gundren and the Gate

- [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]] checks in with his brothers at the [[rockseeker-manufactory|Manufactory]] — an expedition is being assembled for the Modron spelljammer in the mountain, and the [[lunar-elf-spelljammer|derelict from Acheron]] is already being catalogued
- He asks to be notified when the burial service for the gnome [[cornubel|Cornubel]] will be held — honour demands he appear
- Across the keep courtyard, his eyes settle on the Modron gate under construction, and he remembers his compatriots' worries about it
- They're deeply concerned from a security standpoint. Gundren agrees, and begins drafting ideas for how to gain control of the construct

## Tark and the Dojo

- [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] visits the Dojo and talks with [[lord-aimu|Lord Aimu]]'s paladins
- They've evicted **[[rex-kwondo|Rex Kwondo]]** as a charlatan — a man trading on Tark's good name to teach buffoonery — and are stunned when Tark defends him
- They'd been certain they were doing a good thing. Confronted with their own arrogance, they gather to go find Rex and set it right
- Tark heads into the woods to rest, not knowing [[cassian|Cassian]] has found his treehouse and stayed the night in it

## The Barovia Dream

- Tark sleeps and dreams of Barovia — of Strahd after the final battle, standing amid the ruin of Castle Ravenloft, now perched precariously in the middle of the Anauroch desert
- The horizon turns pink with the pre-dawn light and Strahd walks to the edge of the wreckage, just as Tark remembers it
- The monk joins him, sitting beside the spent Vampire Lord to watch the sunrise
- Unlike what really happened, a woman's sobbing echoes through the ruins. Tark turns to survey his friends — blurry and indistinct — and recognises the dreamscape for what it is
- The sobbing grows louder. Strahd begins to talk: he blames Tark, accuses him, says the group led everyone to their deaths — despite Strahd being promised to someone, or something, or promised his freedom
- It's unclear. The sobbing continues as Strahd grows more agitated, then lunges
- The Vampire grabs the monk and sinks his teeth into his neck
- Tark wakes drenched in sweat and exhausted, the bite wound on his neck red and sore. He gets no rest that night

## Luko's Distress

- [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] is pulled from her reverie in the early morning by a message from [[mister-luko|Mister Luko]] of the Helios thieves' guild
- Something is smashing through their security doors from below, and nothing slows it. The last two nights his people have been slaughtered, the streets are in chaos, and people are vanishing all over the city
- The Summer merchants are all clamouring to leave as fast as they can, saying something dark is coming
- Patches gathers her bombs and poisons and summons the group for an expedition to [[helioglabulus|Helioglabulus]]
- They meet at [[the-establishment|The Establishment]], ready for the fray but worried it's a trap
- They decide to arrive at Luko's gambling house through the backrooms and kitchens rather than walking straight up to the "embattled" Rogue Lord
- [[lord-aimu|Lord Aimu]] and his paladins arrive with [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]] — now too many to Teleport, so they summon [[megno-slatesunder|Megno]] to ferry the Lord to the Bloodstone Lands. [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] Teleports the party

## The Rising Sun

- The party arrives on target — but Megno's group is nowhere to be seen
- The kitchen is empty; the sounds of battle carry from the adjoining dining room, something big shaking the walls, men screaming
- Fëanon gets a Sending from Megno: they're off target, in [[mulmaster|Mulmaster]] a good hundred miles south. He'll Scry the target and try again soon
- The group moves to the door to survey the room
- [[mister-luko|Mister Luko]] cowers against the near wall — a wand spitting Magic Missiles in one hand, a potion clutched in the other — while his men stand against the enemy
- It's humanoid, roughly nine feet, clad head to toe in black leather: boots, gloves, trenchcoat, and a conical hood with two eye-holes cut out. The eyes behind them are milky white
- The thing silently hoists a man off his feet one-handed, palms his skull, and squeezes — popping him like an egg
- Two assassins flank it and bury their blades deep. The creature seems unharmed
- Seeing it's clearly not a trap Luko set, the group rushes in

## The Exquisite Golem

- The group's enchanted weapons bite where the guild's steel did nothing, slashing the leather to reveal a mosaic of stitched-together parts, assembled in a complex and arcane manner — a Flesh Golem of exquisite power
- [[feanon-serinde|Fëanon]] recognises the Thayan necromancy just as two Thayan warriors — pale white skin, dark armour — rush up the stairs into the fight
- [[tarkathee-daru|Tark]] engages the pair as they draw swords wreathed in green fire and hurl Eldritch Blasts
- His strikes trigger a backlash of ice from the Warlocks' Armour of Agathys, hurting the monk badly
- The golem takes heavy damage, its flesh ripping open to show an intricate core of machinery and enchanted gemstones arcing with lightning
- Fëanon hears two more Thayans coming up behind and fills the corridor with ice, sealing it
- A second golem charges up behind the Warlocks just as the first is destroyed — it explodes in a flash of lightning that disintegrates the two Helios assassins
- Luko shields his eyes, downs his potion, and turns invisible

## The Dead Rise

- The group kills the first two Warlocks — Tark's strikes send both to the ceiling, gravity reversed — but moments later the dead Warlocks get up again, bones snapping back into place
- Fëanon hits one with a Disintegrate, removing it entirely; the other is fed to [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]]'s Bag of Devouring
- Tark rips at the security shutters sealing the dining room, looks down onto the game floor, and sees the two Thayan Warlocks the ice had blocked finally joining the fight. He shadowsteps down behind a table to ambush them
- He notices a dying serving girl nearby and realises the establishment was hit while preparing for the day's revelry. He holds her hand as she dies
- The group destroys the last golem just as Tark falls on the two Thayans, and makes quick work of them out in the parlour — their bodies to the Bag of Devouring

## The Aftermath

- Luko becomes visible again and slumps into a chair, breathing hard. Only one of his retainers survived
- Questioned, he lays it out: things have gotten markedly worse over two days. People vanish by the score each night, and the Summer merchants are fleeing
- The [[crimson-dawn-knights|Knights of the Crimson Dawn]] have been strangely absent — not gone entirely, but almost always escorting the nobility, who have turned reclusive. He suspects they're all compromised
- The group explains their intent to assault the castle and hunt the vampires. Luko eagerly offers to help — and then flee
- He opens his best potions and poisons to them and has his survivors send for maps of the undercity so the group can find its way to the catacombs
- The group takes a short rest. The man returns quickly with bottles and scrolls, and [[aliyah-faen-tlabbar|Patches]] transcribes the multi-layered map of the Undercity leading to the Castle
- Luko says his man will lead the group through the start of the sewers. The party gathers their things to move out

## The Wish and the Reflection

- [[gundren-rockseeker|Gundren]] sees the Crystal Sunsword on [[horatio-von-bumidere|Buumi]]'s back — the marring fingerprint still warping the blade — and asks whether they could try Wishing it clean
- [[jack-mayer-johnson-mathews|JmJm]] cracks his knuckles and steps forward, the rolling thunder of the spell on his lips, and touches the fingerprint, wishing it unmarred
- As he touches it, they all see movement within the blade: a pallid creature with glowing yellow eyes reaches out from inside the crystal and touches the fingerprint at the same instant the bard does — a reflection within the sword
- A flash of light, the smell of sulphur, and JmJm is thrown ten feet across the room into a plush chair, eyes wide
- [[cirrus-al-kayan|Cirrus]] locks eyes with the reflection as it shifts into the porcelain beauty of [[queen-concetinna-frostmantle|Queen Concetinna]] within the blade
- The thing had the power to reject the cleansing Wish — and Cirrus, of all of them, has the background to know what carries that kind of power
- He reaches back through everything the Laughing God's service and a working priesthood ever taught him about the courts of the Abyss, and the recognition lands cold and total: the face, the tether, the appetite for vampires all belong to one name
- Queen Concetinna of the Bloodstone Lands is no longer human, or even flesh and blood
- Cirrus names her without a flicker of doubt — **[[malcanthet|Malcanthet]]**, the Demon Queen of the Succubi, Mother of Vampires
