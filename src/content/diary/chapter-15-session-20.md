## The Shape He Might Have Worn

The teeth waited in boxes inside [[Cirrus Al Kayan|Cirrus's]] sanctum.

They had arrived as [[Zulkhir Atlan Issan|Atlan's]] insult: thousands of bloody reminders of the tribute once demanded by [[Mediocris Dente]], heaped around the feet of Cirrus's statue in the [[Fane of Selune]]. Cirrus had ordered them packed away. Now his servants gathered the last of the boxes and [[Topsy & Turvy|Topsy]] summoned [[Temura]], the highest-ranking cleric beneath Cirrus's roof.

The High Elf surveyed the collection with open distaste.

Cirrus gave him instructions. Under the working of *Fabricate*, loose teeth lifted from their boxes and fitted together into a statue: not the marble priest enthroned below, but a demonic Cirrus, the shape he imagined he might have become had he remained with the Marilith and [[Ceregorach]]. It was an effigy of the life he had escaped, assembled from the currency of his bondage.

Then Cirrus cast *True Polymorph* and made it move.

The tooth-built image became something with the strength and permanence of an Iron Golem. What had been sent as a threat now stood in Cirrus's chambers as a weapon under his command.

Temura did not admire the reversal. Before leaving, he questioned Cirrus's intentions and wondered aloud whether he should contact the established [[Church of Selûne]] in [[silverymoon|Silverymoon]]. The distinction was pointed: the Fane called itself a temple of Selûne, but Cirrus was not the whole of her church.

Cirrus summoned Topsy and Turvy after the cleric departed. The twins admitted that they questioned Temura's allegiance and wondered whether he was a spy for the *real* church. Cirrus answered the remark by striking Topsy several times and sending the administrator from the chamber in shame.

Temura would be watched.

## Accounts of the Dead

At the [[Rockseeker Manufactory]], [[Gundren Rockseeker|Gundren]] checked in with his brothers and found two pieces of work already advancing without him. An expedition was being assembled to excavate [[The Derelict|the Modron spelljammer]] beneath the River Surbrin. The wreckage hauled back from [[acheron|Acheron]] was already being catalogued.

Gundren asked to be told when [[Cornubel]] would be buried. The Gnome had been dead for more than a century, but time did not diminish an obligation. Gundren would attend. Honour required it.

Across the courtyard, [[Modrons]] continued building their gate inside the Keep. The sight returned Gundren to the party's warnings. No one knew where the construct led, who would be able to open it, or what might come through when it was finished. Gundren agreed that it could not remain a machine inside their walls and outside their control. He began drafting ways to seize that control.

The same connection that drew him toward damaged Modron minds now confronted him as a problem in sovereignty: rescue was one thing; granting an unknown hierarchy an unsecured door into the Barony was another.

## A Borrowed Name

[[Tarkathee Daru|Tark]] visited the dojo and found the paladins of [[Lord Aimu]] pleased with themselves.

They had expelled a teacher named [[Rex Kwondo]]. Rex had been using Tark's good name while teaching what the paladins considered buffoonery, and they believed they had defended the monk's reputation by removing the charlatan.

Tark defended him.

The rebuke stopped them cold. They had acted in the name of a man they revered without asking what that man valued, and in correcting Rex's presumption had committed their own. Chastened, the paladins gathered to find the teacher and repair what they had done.

Tark left them to it and went into the woods to rest. Unknown to him, [[Cassian]] had found [[Tark's Treehouse|his treehouse]] and spent the night there.

## Sunrise over Ravenloft

Tark dreamed of [[barovia|Barovia]].

Castle [[Castle Ravenloft|Ravenloft]] stood ruined in the middle of the Anauroch desert, perched precariously on broken stone just as it had after the final battle. The eastern horizon had begun to turn pink. [[Strahd Von Zarovich|Strahd]], spent and defeated, walked to the edge of the wreckage to wait for the sun.

Tark sat beside him.

The memory was almost right. Then a woman's sobbing echoed through the ruins.

Tark looked back at his companions and found them blurred and indistinct. He recognised the Dreamscape. Strahd began to accuse him: Tark had saved no one. The group had led everyone to their deaths. Somewhere inside the vampire's anger was a broken claim about a promise—Strahd had been promised to someone, or promised freedom—but the words would not settle into one meaning. The sobbing grew louder. Strahd lunged.

The vampire seized Tark and sank his fangs into the monk's neck.

Tark woke drenched in sweat. The old bite on his neck was red and sore. Whatever had used Strahd's face had reached far enough through the dream to leave the body unrested.

## Luko's Warning

Before morning had properly begun, [[Aliyah Faen Tlabbar|Patches]] received a message from [[Mister Luko]].

Something was smashing through the [[Rising Sun|Rising Sun's]] security doors from below. For two nights Luko's people had been slaughtered and citizens had vanished by the score. The streets were coming apart under fear. Merchants who had come to [[helioglabulus|Helios]] for the summer were abandoning their business and clamouring to leave before whatever was coming arrived.

Patches gathered bombs and poisons and called the group to [[The Establishment]]. They prepared to return to Helios while still allowing for the possibility that Luko's appeal was a trap. Instead of appearing in the guildmaster's embattled dining room, they would Teleport into the kitchens and approach through the back of the building.

Lord Aimu arrived with [[Horatio Von Bumidere|Buumi]] and a company of paladins. There were too many for one Teleportation. [[Megno Slatesunder|Megno]] would carry Aimu's group; [[Fëanon Sërindë|Fëanon]] would take the first party.

Fëanon arrived on target.

Megno did not. A Sending came moments later: his group had landed in Mulmaster, more than a hundred miles south. He would scry the destination and try again.

From the empty kitchen came the sound of men dying.

## The Men in Black Leather

The dining room shook under heavy impacts. Luko crouched against the near wall, a wand in one hand spitting Magic Missiles and a potion clenched in the other. His remaining assassins surrounded a humanoid figure nearly nine feet tall, covered from boots to gloves in black leather. A conical hood hid its head except for two eyeholes and the milky white stare behind them.

It lifted one of Luko's men one-handed, palmed his skull, and squeezed until the head burst.

Two assassins buried blades in the creature's back. It did not react.

The party went through the door.

Enchanted weapons cut what guild steel could not. The leather opened to reveal a mosaic body assembled from stitched flesh with an intricacy no ordinary necromancer would attempt. Beneath the meat lay machinery, enchanted gemstones, and a lightning-fed core. Fëanon recognised [[Red Wizards of Thay|Thayan]] craft.

Two pale warriors in black armour charged up the stairs, green fire running along their blades as Eldritch Blasts crossed the room. Tark met them and discovered the ice of their *Armour of Agathys* the hard way. More boots sounded from the corridor behind the fight. Fëanon sealed it in ice.

A second [[Thayan Flesh Golems|leather-clad golem]] arrived behind the warlocks just as the first came apart. Its core detonated in a burst of lightning that erased two of Luko's assassins where they stood.

Luko drank his potion and vanished.

The first two warlocks fell. Tark's strikes reversed their gravity and smashed them against the ceiling—but death did not hold. Bones snapped into place. Bodies stood again. Fëanon removed one with *Disintegrate*; another disappeared into Cirrus's [[Bag of Devouring]].

The ice sealing the corridor broke. Two more Thayans joined the battle while Tark tore open the security shutters and dropped to the gambling floor below. A serving girl lay dying beside an overturned table. She had come to prepare the house for another day of revelry and instead found a war beneath it. Tark held her hand until she was gone.

The party destroyed the second golem, killed the remaining warlocks, and fed their bodies to the bag before they could rise again.

## A City Being Harvested

Luko reappeared and collapsed into a chair. Of the retainers who had begun the fight around him, one survived.

He told them the last two days had changed everything. People were disappearing every night. The summer merchants were fleeing. The [[Crimson Dawn Knights]] had not vanished entirely, but their presence in the streets had thinned precisely when the city needed them most. Those who remained visible were usually escorting nobles who had withdrawn behind their walls.

Luko suspected the nobility and the Order had been compromised.

The party told him they meant to enter the castle and hunt the vampires at the centre of the city. Luko offered everything useful in his possession, then intended to leave Helios himself: his best potions and poisons, his surviving people, and the maps by which thieves had learned the buried shape of the city.

During a short rest, one of his men returned with bottles, scrolls, and a multilayered map of the undercity. Patches transcribed it. A guild footpad would guide them through the first sewers and set them on the route to [[The Catacombs of Helioglabulus|the catacombs]] beneath the castle.

## The Fingerprint

Gundren noticed the [[Ashbringer|Crystal Sunsword]] on Buumi's back and the delicate fingerprint that marred its otherwise perfect blade.

Perhaps a Wish could clean it.

[[Jack Mayer Johnson Mathews|JmJm]] stepped forward, cracked his knuckles, and spoke with thunder rolling beneath every word. He touched the fingerprint and wished the blade unmarred.

Something moved inside the crystal.

A pallid creature with glowing yellow eyes reached from within the sword and placed one hand against JmJm's finger from the other side. Light filled the room. Sulphur fouled the air. The Wish threw the bard ten feet backward into a plush chair and left the mark untouched.

Cirrus stared into the blade. The pale reflection shifted, smoothing itself into the porcelain beauty of [[Queen Concetinna Frostmantle]].

The fingerprint was not ordinary damage. Something powerful enough to refuse a Wish had answered through it and worn the Queen's face while doing so. Whether Concetinna was the creature, its vessel, or merely a shape it chose remained unresolved.

Cirrus knew the name of what they faced.

[[Malcathet]]. The Succubus Demon Lord. Mother of Vampires.
